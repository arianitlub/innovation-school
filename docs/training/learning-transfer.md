## Ideas pool (revisit after Budapest, pick ONE as the Goal)

1. **Innovation Hour** (current choice): a monthly 60–90 min session on one shared team bottleneck (onboarding, knowledge sharing, testing practices…), worked through with Design Thinking. The facilitator rotates. It's recurring, which fits the Goal, and the first session can be the Individual Homework.
2. **"Steal this" show & tell**: every 2–4 weeks, 2–3 people from different projects each show something reusable (a tool, script, test approach or lesson). It breaks knowledge silos and needs little preparation. Validate by asking whether anyone actually reused something.
3. **"Fail & learn" round**: 10 min in the regular team meeting where someone shares what didn't work and what they learned. I go first. It builds the "May" part (safety) of Want / May / Can.
4. **Pain-point interviews → team problem backlog**: use "tell me about the last time you got stuck…" questions in 1:1s and collect the answers in one list. This is the Empathize step, and it feeds Innovation Hour topics.

Suggested combination: pain-point interviews (#4) → the first Innovation Hour as homework, before 04.11 → a monthly Innovation Hour as the Goal (#1). #2 and #3 are easy add-ons.

Possible first topics: *How might we share knowledge across projects so nobody reinvents the wheel?* · *How might we onboard new people faster?* · *How might we feel like one team despite working on different projects?*
