## Output

### 1. Direct competitors (CEE, focus on Hungary)

Hungarian banks all sell a similar Premium tier: **named adviser, priority service, wider fund range, entry threshold based on income or savings.** They differ mainly in threshold and in how digital the adviser channel is.

| Bank | Advertised entry threshold | Advertised advice model |
|---|---|---|
| **Raiffeisen Hungary** [S1] | Premium Banking: HUF 400,000 monthly income. Premium Banking Plus: HUF 700,000 income or HUF 10 million savings. The page also shows "400,000 or 10 million" for Plus, so this requires verification | Two levels. Premium gets a **RaiConnect video consultancy team** plus investment specialists in branches. Plus gets a **dedicated adviser** by video or in branch. Online Broker and recurring investment are also offered |
| **OTP Bank** [S2] | HUF 700,000 net monthly income or HUF 10 million savings. Premium Next fee HUF 3,900/month if conditions are met, otherwise HUF 10,000 | Personal adviser by appointment. "Proactive" care "adapted to your life situation". **OTP Pénzügyi Tervező** (financial planner tool) used together with the adviser. More than 70 funds, including HOLD and VIG funds |
| **Erste Hungary** [S3][S4] | HUF 15–50 million savings or at least HUF 1 million monthly income | Own banker builds the portfolio based on risk appetite and "actively supports" its management. Call the banker directly, or start a call from the George app. **Premium webinars**, also available as recordings |
| **MBH Bank** [S5] | Tripla SKY package: at least HUF 8 million savings or HUF 800,000 income. Fee-free conditions mention HUF 15 million | Personal premium adviser. Investment strategy "developed with the support of MBH's analyst team" |
| **UniCredit Hungary** [S6] | Savings above HUF 17.5 million, checked every year | Personal adviser for investment decisions |
| **K&H** | Not checked (site blocked access) | This requires verification |

Elsewhere in RBI markets:
- **Raiffeisen Romania Premium Invest** [S7]: from EUR 60,000 in assets; dedicated banker, 25+ dedicated locations, wider product range, SmartInvest investment plans.
- **Slovenská sporiteľňa (Erste, Slovakia)** [S8] sells a Premium package from EUR 13/month that customers can **buy and cancel in the George app**. The package includes regular investing.
- **Raiffeisen Serbia** [S9] publishes goal-based planning content, including a clear retirement "gap" question (desired income versus expected state pension).
- **Tatra banka (Slovakia)** offers a named banker or a shared Premium team. **Raiffeisenbank Czech Republic** offers automated, limited-scope advice (from our 10.10.2026 notes; not re-checked today).

**Results:** no bank publishes uptake, retention or satisfaction numbers on these pages. Raiffeisen Hungary shows RaiConnect client quotes and "over 100,000 satisfied clients in 15 years": marketing claims, not measured outcomes. Results require verification.

**What stands out:** Raiffeisen Hungary's video team (Premium) versus dedicated adviser (Plus) serves more customers without a named adviser each. Erste's webinars and app-started calls lower the contact barrier. OTP links a planning tool to the adviser conversation.

### 2. Global industry leaders

- **Vanguard (US)** [S10][S11] has the clearest ladder from digital to human. Digital Advisor starts at USD 100, about USD 15–16 per USD 10,000 per year, with no human adviser. Personal Advisor starts at USD 50,000, about USD 30–31 per USD 10,000, and includes adviser access. Personal Advisor Select starts at USD 500,000, with a dedicated CFP (Certified Financial Planner) professional, no more than 0.30%. Vanguard says it was ranked first in Morningstar's 2025 Robo-Advisor Report. That is a third-party ranking reported by Vanguard, and Vanguard paid Morningstar to license it.
- **Charles Schwab (US)** [S12] offers Intelligent Portfolios from USD 5,000. The page says there is no advisory fee below USD 25,000 and 0.35% above. Schwab partly earns from the cash allocation, which the page discloses. Premium needs USD 500,000.
- **HSBC Premier (UK)** [S13][S14] offers qualified financial advisers, a "Future Planner" tool and relationship management. Fees and eligibility conditions apply (for example a GBP 100,000 salary or GBP 100,000 in savings to switch).
- **J.P. Morgan Personal Investing (Nutmeg, UK)** [S15] offers **free guidance** calls, then paid "Core" or "Enhanced" advice. Enhanced includes cashflow planning and stress tests. The page states it is "restricted advice" (own products only).
- **Morgan Stanley, UBS, DBS:** not reachable; this requires verification.

**Results:** fees and minimums are published; no cost-saving or satisfaction results were found.

### 3. Innovative disruptors

- **Trade Republic** [S16]: invest from EUR 1, interest on cash, 1% "Saveback" on card spending into investments. Users get very low friction and saving that happens automatically.
- **Scalable Capital** [S17]: ETF savings plans from EUR 1, plus the "Scalable Wealth" managed offer. It also powers **ING Germany Smart Invest** [S18] (from EUR 20, 0.75% a year, 0.65% from EUR 50,000). This shows that a **bank can partner** with a fintech instead of building the service itself.
- **Moneyfarm** [S19]: tiers by invested amount: Core GBP 500–10,000, Premium GBP 10,000–100,000 with an Investment Consultant team, and Private with a Wealth Manager. Customers below the threshold can **unlock Private for GBP 12.99/month**.
- **Finax** (CEE, including Slovakia) [S20]: questionnaire → proposed portfolio → review the contract "calmly" → investing starts at the first deposit. Named goal accounts and family account monitoring. Return figures are "retrospective models", not client results.
- **Revolut (robo-advisor):** page blocked. This requires verification.

**Lessons:** publish the price per level, sell human help below the wealth threshold, make the first deposit and automatic saving easy.

### 4. Cross-industry leaders

- **NHS 111 (UK health triage)** [S21]: online questions first, then a nurse calls back if needed, then you are sent "to the best place to get help". *Principle:* **digital triage** decides who needs a person, and how quickly.
- **TurboTax (Intuit, US)** [S22]: do it yourself, or get "unlimited, on-demand advice" from tax experts with AI guidance, or a "Full Service" expert who does it for you. Final expert review is guaranteed. *Principle:* **the same journey with three levels of help**, and an expert check before the customer commits.
- **Future (fitness coaching app)** [S23]: a human coach sets a personal plan and adjusts it through the app (USD 199/month). *Principle:* one human serves many customers **asynchronously** (not live), with the app collecting data in between.

### 6. Differentiation opportunities

**Gaps we see (our inference, requires testing with customers):**
1. **The space just below the threshold.** Hungarian Premium starts at HUF 400,000–1,000,000 income or HUF 8–50 million savings. Customers just below these limits get standard service. Moneyfarm and TurboTax show that customers can **pay for human help** without meeting a wealth threshold.
2. **"After investing" is weak.** Bank pages describe onboarding and portfolio choice, rarely regular check-ins, life-event triggers or goal progress. Erste's "active support" and OTP's "proactive" wording are claims only.
3. **Scope and price unclear.** Bank Premium pages rarely say what advice covers, how often, or at what cost. Vanguard and Moneyfarm publish this in a table.
4. **Little triage.** No regional bank clearly uses a digital step to decide which question needs a human, and how quickly.
5. **Confidence and learning.** Only Erste advertises webinars; most providers sell products rather than build understanding.

**What could make RBI unique:**
- **Build on what we already have:** RaiConnect video (Hungary), automated advice (Czech Republic), planning content (Serbia), Premium Invest (Romania). Join them into one guided journey: *goal → digital triage → video expert when needed → monitoring with alerts*.
- **Publish a simple "help ladder"** with clear scope and price at each level, including a paid upgrade to a human below the threshold.
- **Give advisers a pre-filled customer brief** before each call, made from the digital step, so their time goes to judgement and not to collecting data.

**Recommendations to test, not to decide yet:** ask Raiffeisen Hungary colleagues in Budapest how RaiConnect is used and what results it has; prototype one "after investing" moment (for example a life-event check-in); compare our help ladder with OTP and Erste in customer interviews.

## What this means for our challenge

- **H1 (advice not personalised enough):** Hungarian bank pages promise "personalised" and "proactive" advice, but none shows how often it happens or what it covers. Vanguard and Nutmeg write down the planning scope. In interviews, test whether Premium customers in Hungary actually experience personal follow-up.
- **H2 (lack of knowledge/confidence):** only Erste (webinars) and Raiffeisen Serbia (planning content) invest visibly in customer understanding. Nutmeg's free guidance call is a low-pressure first step. This supports testing a "learn and get guidance before you commit" step.
- **H3 (preference for hybrid digital + human):** almost every leader now mixes digital and human help (Vanguard, Moneyfarm, TurboTax, RaiConnect). The difference is *how a customer moves between levels*. Our interviews should ask when customers want a person and when they don't.
- **H4 (advisers lack data/tools/time):** shared video teams (RaiConnect), planning tools (OTP Pénzügyi Tervező) and triage models (NHS 111) all protect adviser time. An adviser brief pre-filled from a digital step is worth testing with RBI advisers. Check with them first what tools they already have.
