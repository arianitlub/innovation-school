## Key findings across all 9

1. **Hungary has a clear Premium threshold.** Raiffeisen HU Premium starts at HUF 400,000 monthly income (video advice via RaiConnect); Premium Plus at HUF 700,000 income or HUF 10m savings with a dedicated adviser. OTP uses the same 700k / 10m rule. The Raiffeisen page is inconsistent about Plus, so this needs checking. **Customers just below the threshold** may get little human help: a possible focus.
2. **Hungarian savings are moving.** MNB data: investment funds overtook retail government bonds by the end of 2025 after bond yields fell. Many term deposits earn under 1%, and savers cite risk and low rates. This supports H2 (knowledge and confidence).
3. **Banks promise personal advice but publish no results.** No Hungarian bank shows uptake, follow-up or satisfaction. Many mass-affluent customers may get **no advice at all**, only a product test. In interviews, separate "no advice" from "advice not personalised" (H1).
4. **Hybrid is the common model; the handover is the gap.** Vanguard, Moneyfarm, TurboTax and RaiConnect mix digital and human. Raiffeisen Czech Republic already has automated advice. Our value may be a better **app-to-person handover**, not a new robo-adviser (H3).
5. **AI helps advisers first.** The proven use is meeting preparation and summaries checked by a person (Morgan Stanley). This matches H4 and avoids the hardest regulatory questions.
6. **Rules shape what we can build.** Guidance and education are cheaper and less regulated than personal advice (MiFID II). Customer-facing AI must disclose that it is AI (AI Act, from 02.08.2026). Check ideas with Compliance early.
