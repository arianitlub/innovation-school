# 2.3.2 Provisional Personas — mass-affluent investment guidance

RBI AI Prompt Library prompt **2.3.2**, run 14.10.2026 with skill `innovation-empathize`. **Updated 14.10.2026** after two street interviews (C1, C2) and the UX research summary. Input: [2.3.1](2.3.1-interview-pattern-analysis.md).

> **Limitation:** each customer persona rests on **one** real street interview plus expert and UX signals. Names are labels, not real people. Ages, jobs and family details are **not** invented; where the prompt asks for them we write "not known". Quotes are from transcripts (lightly edited). Treat these as **provisional personas** until more customers are interviewed.

## Persona 1 — "The Self-Directed Comparer" (based on C1)

### A. Snapshot
- **Label:** Self-Directed Comparer
- **Age / job / location:** not known (English-speaking; market not stated)
- **One line:** Invests long-term on their own, with family and ChatGPT as advisers, after leaving the bank's investment account because of cost and product push.

### B. Background
- Saves and has invested part of the savings for about two years (ETFs).
- Had a bank investment account; **cancelled it this year** because of cost.
- Digital proficiency: comfortable.

### C. Channel behaviour
- Prefers **digital**: wants to "see everything and do everything".
- Used an adviser before, but had to fit the adviser's schedule.

### D. Goals
> "I want to invest long-term and regularly, at low cost, in options that fit my life and plans."

### E. Pain points
1. Bank information was **not clear** and **not personal**: "more of a product push".
2. **Too expensive** to keep the investment account.
3. Had to **adjust to the adviser's availability**.
4. When leaving, the bank asked why but **offered nothing**.

### F. Needs and priorities
- Guidance that asks about **life situation and near-term plans**, not only the MiFID questions.
- Transparent, low cost.
- **Immediate support** when lost, on their schedule.
- Willing to share personal context for better guidance.

### G. Workarounds
- **Nephew** shortlists options.
- **ChatGPT** analyses and compares their funds and suggests where to put new money.
- Moved to an online provider.

### H. Quote
> "It was not too personalised… I felt it was more of a product push." — C1 *(transcript)*

### I. Scenario (from the interview, not invented)
After two years of investing, C1 wants to add money. They upload a list of their funds to ChatGPT and ask what to focus on. The bank, which once had their investment account, is not part of this decision.

### J. Technology attitude
Positive and pragmatic; feels slightly guilty using ChatGPT ("I think it's really bad") but finds it "very helpful".

### K. Competitive perspective
Already switched to a cheaper online option. Would come back for **personal + cheaper**.

## Persona 2 — "The Cautious Branch Saver" (based on C2)

### A. Snapshot
- **Label:** Cautious Branch Saver
- **Age / job:** not known. **Location:** Budapest; prefers Hungarian.
- **One line:** A long-time saver who trusts face-to-face banking and rejects phone and online offers.

### B. Background
- Has saved "for decades". Investing not described.
- Digital proficiency: not known; distrusts internet offers.

### C. Channel behaviour
- **Branch**, or **contacting the bank themselves**.
- Rejects **phone** offers, **internet** offers and **ads**.

### D. Goals
Not stated directly. Safe, long-term saving is implied. **Do not invent further goals.**

### E. Pain points
1. Distrust of offers that come to them (phone, online, ads).
2. Low trust in the wider environment (currency: "If Hungary used the euro").

### F. Needs and priorities
- Trust through **personal, face-to-face** contact.
- **Customer-initiated** conversations.

### G. Workarounds
- Does not research; relies on **other people's** information.

### H. Quote
> "If an offer is made in a bank branch, there is more trust. Not through the internet, and not through advertisements." — C2 *(transcript, translated)*

### I. Scenario
Not enough detail from the interview to write one.

### J. Technology attitude
Distrusts internet offers; app use not known.

### K. Competitive perspective
Not known.

## Persona 3 — "The New-to-Investing Learner" (from UX study + experts; no street interview yet)

- **Evidence:** UX study (8 Serbian savers not yet investing; 4-country themes); E-HU-1 (fear of loss, lack of knowledge).
- **Goal:** "I want to understand enough to start safely."
- **Pains:** complexity, risk and security worries, unclear withdrawal and fund information, text-heavy content.
- **Needs:** simple explanations where they are needed, learn "only what I need", small start amounts.
- **Reported quote (UX presentation, not verified):** "I want to compare options, not just be told."

## 4. Design implications per persona

- **Self-Directed Comparer:** personal, comparable, transparent options in the app; help on demand; a real offer at the exit moment.
- **Cautious Branch Saver:** a face-to-face or self-booked path; **never** cold calls.
- **New Learner:** clarity at the decision point; small, safe first step.
- **Shared need:** the **customer controls the contact**, and guidance starts from **their** situation.
- **Conflict:** digital vs branch. The solution must work in **more than one channel**.
- **Priority for Ideate:** Persona 1 (closest to mass affluent with investable money and a clear story), with Persona 2's channel needs as a constraint.
