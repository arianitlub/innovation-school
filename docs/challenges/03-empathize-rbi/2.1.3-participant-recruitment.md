### 3. Outreach messaging

#### Tone

Friendly, short, honest. On the street: smile, one sentence, accept "no" immediately. With colleagues and bankers: friendly but professional, clear time limit.

#### A. Street approach script (main channel)

All text is in English. **A native Hungarian speaker should translate it** before use, and ideally lead the street conversations. A rough literal translation can sound strange or pushy.

**Approach (10 seconds)**

> "Excuse me, sorry to disturb you. We're a small team from a bank training programme, and we're asking people how they get advice about their savings. It takes about 5 minutes, it's anonymous, and we won't ask about amounts or account details. Would you have 5 minutes?"

**If yes**

> "Thank you! Just so you know: we only take short written notes, no names, and you can stop at any time. Is it okay if my colleague takes notes?"

Then the screening questions (section 4), then the interview from [../03-empathize.md](../03-empathize.md) section 4.

**If no / in a hurry**

> "No problem at all, have a nice day!"

Do not follow, do not ask twice.

**If they ask "Which bank?"**

> "We work for Raiffeisen, but this is not about selling anything. We're interested in your experience with any bank or provider."

**If they ask for advice or a product**

> "We're not allowed to give advice here, and we're not selling. If you want advice, your own bank or adviser is the right place."

The team sends this themselves. Nothing is sent by the AI.

### 7. Sample size recommendation

- **Plan:** 12 street interviews started, to get about 10 in segment; 1 expert; 2–3 bankers if possible.
- **Assess after 6:** after round 1, look at the tracker. Are we hearing the same stories (for example, "nobody ever explained the risk")? Which matrix cells are empty?
- **Extend if needed:** if new themes still appear in interviews 9–10, do 3–4 more on 15.10.2026. If interviews 8–10 add nothing new, we have a first **saturation signal** for this group.
- **Confidence:** 10 short interviews in one city give a **first signal, not proof**. Saturation in qualitative research is usually reached around 8–12 interviews *per homogeneous group*; with several groups mixed in 10 interviews we will not reach saturation for each group. State this in the pitch.
- **H4:** with 1 expert it is "signal only". With 3 or more advisers it can be "supported / not supported".

### 8. Ethical and compliance considerations

**General note:** this section is practical guidance, not legal advice. **This requires verification** with RBI or RBHU data protection or compliance on whether verbal consent and anonymous notes are enough for street interviews.

#### Privacy (GDPR) and confidentiality

- **Data minimisation:** collect only what we need: age band, in segment Y/N, channel, answers, quotes. No names, phone numbers, emails, exact age, address, employer, bank account, amounts or photos of people.
- **Anonymous IDs:** C1, C2… (customers), E1 (expert), B1… (bankers), P1… (proxy colleagues). Never write a name next to an ID, not even on a separate list.
- **Quotes:** write exact words, but remove anything that identifies the person ("my husband at company X…" → "[partner's employer]").
- **Customer confidentiality:** bankers and the expert must not share data about real customers. If they start, stop them kindly: "Please don't tell us anything that identifies a client."
- **Storage:** notes only in RBI-approved storage (e.g. the team's RBI OneDrive / Teams), not personal phones or private cloud. Paper sheets: type them up the same day, then destroy them. No customer data in any AI tool; only anonymised notes may be pasted into an approved tool for synthesis.

#### Consent

Verbal consent is the plan for street interviews (no forms). **This requires verification** whether a written note or a short information card is required.

**Verbal consent script (street, after the approach)**

> "Before we start: we're RBI employees on a training programme. We'd like to ask about how you get advice on your savings. It's anonymous: we don't write your name, and we won't ask about amounts or accounts. We only take short written notes, no recording. We'll use them only inside our team to learn and improve ideas. You can skip any question or stop at any time. Is that okay?"

Note-taker ticks "Consent: yes" on the sheet. No consent → no notes.

**Expert / banker consent (adds recording)**

> "We'll keep your answers anonymous, as 'Expert 1' or 'Banker 1'. Please don't share any client data. May we record this call only to complete our notes? We'll delete the recording after we've written the notes. If you prefer no recording, that's completely fine."

#### What we can and cannot promise

| Can promise | Cannot promise |
|---|---|
| Anonymous notes, no names | That the bank will change anything |
| They can stop at any time | Any product, rate or special offer |
| Short time (5 minutes) and we keep to it | Advice or a personal recommendation |
| Notes used only by the team for learning | Payment or a gift (unless approved) |

#### Right to withdraw

If someone says during or after the interview "please don't use that", cross out the sheet in front of them or delete it, and mark the ID "withdrawn" in the tracker.
