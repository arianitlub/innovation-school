# 2.4 Problem One-Pager — Reflection before Step 3

Programme template "Problem One-Pager, Step 2.4 Reflection" (Budapest workshop poster), filled 14.10.2026 from the Stage 2 synthesis ([2.3.1](2.3.1-interview-pattern-analysis.md)–[2.3.4](2.3.4-how-might-we.md)). Labels: 🟢 public source · 🟡 interview/expert-reported · ⚪ unknown or illustrative.

---

## What is the problem?

Mass-affluent customers outside Premium can invest digitally, but the bank's guidance feels like a **product push**: unclear, not based on their life situation, and on the bank's terms (phone offers, adviser's schedule). Costs feel high. So customers **decide elsewhere** (family, ChatGPT, cheaper providers, government bonds) and the bank loses the investment relationship. 🟡

## What is the size of the problem? How often does it occur?

- Hungary: mass-affluent customers are served **non-advisory** by default. 🟡 (E-HU-1)
- 1 of 2 street interviewees **left the bank's investment account** (cost, product push). The other rejects phone and online offers. 🟡
- Hungarian households: **HUF 16,400bn in funds**, HUF 13,700bn in retail government bonds; about half of term deposits earn under 1%. 🟢 (MNB Savings Report 2026)
- Number of customers affected: ⚪ needs internal data.

## What metrics and sources is this based on?

2 expert interviews (HU, RS) · 2 street interviews in Budapest · internal UX education study (8 Serbian interviews + 4-country synthesis) · MNB Savings Report 2026 · bank Premium pages. **Missing:** segment size, cancellations and reasons, My Life drop-off, Premium uptake.

**Is the problem size worth pursuing?** ☑ **Yes, provisionally**: large savings in low-yield products, and customers are leaving or not starting.

---

## Who is affected? Who are the stakeholders?

- **Customers:** self-directed investors outside Premium, cautious branch savers, new-to-investing savers.
- **Bank:** Premium/relationship managers, RaiConnect team, My Life digital team, investment product team, segment management, compliance, branches.

## How many people or departments are involved? (FTE)

About **7 departments**. FTE ⚪ unknown. Reported: a Premium relationship manager contacts each client **2–3 times per year** 🟡, so full adviser coverage for everyone doesn't scale.

## Who did you talk to?

- 2 internal experts (Hungary, Serbia)
- 2 street interviews in Budapest (C1 English, C2 Hungarian); more short street conversations reportedly agreed on "not by telephone" but were not recorded
- Existing internal UX research (secondary)

**Are they willing to support an attempt to solve this?** ☑ **Yes, partly.** The experts engaged and pointed to existing tools; C1 is willing to share life context for better guidance. Still needed: the segment owner and the digital team.

## Process

**1. As designed today**
- **Mass:** My Life app → securities account → MiFID questionnaire → choose 1 of 3 options → invest (4–6 min, **no advice**). 🟡
- **Premium:** qualify (reported about €1,100–1,900 monthly income or about €27k assets, to verify) → relationship manager or RaiConnect video → advisory tool → digital signing. 🟡

**2. As lived (differences)**
- Trigger and advice come from **family or ChatGPT**, not the bank (C1, C2).
- Bank offers feel like a **product push**; **phone offers are rejected** (C1, C2).
- The customer had to **fit the adviser's schedule** (C1).
- **Cost** made C1 cancel; the bank asked why but **offered nothing**.
- Some customers trust **only the branch**, when they start the contact themselves (C2).
- Information is enough in amount but **not clear** (C1, UX).

**3. Planned changes**
"Learn to Invest" module (HU), RAIA virtual assistant could connect to a human later, easier transfers between the current account and the securities account. 🟡

**4. Ideal process**
Customer starts → guidance asks about **life and plans** → **clear, comparable, affordable** options → **help on demand** in their channel (app, video, branch) → light review → at doubt or exit, a real alternative.

## What is the business impact?

Lost investment business and share of wallet: the salary account stays, while savings go to bonds, cheaper providers or stay in low-yield deposits. Lost future Premium clients. Customers' trusted advisers become family and ChatGPT.

## How big is the business impact?

⚪ **Illustrative only:** 1,000 customers × €50,000, of whom 10% invest elsewhere = €5m. At an assumed 0.5% annual revenue, about **€25,000 a year**. Not RBI figures.

## What data is it based on?

The illustrative model above; EY 2025 (29% of wealth clients intend to switch within 3 years, an intention); hidden defection (Bain); C1's real cancellation. **Needed:** internal cancellations, outflows, segment revenue.

**Is the business impact worth solving?** ☑ **Yes, provisionally.** Present it as a range with stated assumptions.
