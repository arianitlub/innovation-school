## What we test (H1–H7, from 4.1.1)

1. The unlock feels helpful, not like sales.
2. Customers complete the Life Profile and find it accurate.
3. The Snapshot makes "invested vs not invested" and costs clear.
4. Wealth Insights picks are read as ideas, not pushed products.
5. Customers turn to the expert when in doubt and value the context passed on.
6. Branch-trusting or older customers can use it, or a branch version.
7. Notifications are wanted, not annoying.
