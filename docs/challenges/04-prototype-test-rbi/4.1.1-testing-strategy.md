> **Evidence limits.** The concept "My Wealth" was proposed by Arianit on 14.10.2026; team confirmation is still pending. ## Filled prompt

```text
I need to test my solution concept with users/customers before fully implementing it. Help me create a testing strategy.

MY SOLUTION CONCEPT:
"My Wealth" for Raiffeisen Bank International, scope Hungary, for mass-affluent customers outside full Premium advice. A new section inside the existing mobile app (My Life in Hungary), unlocked when the customer enters the mass-affluent segment. Flow: threshold reached → unlock My Wealth → Life Profile → Learn / Investment Playground → AI if wanted → expert if needed → real investment or advice → ongoing reviews. For older or AI-wary customers: Life Profile → Learn → Talk to an expert, without the AI; a branch version (banker opens the Life Profile on a tablet and prints a one-page plan) is planned but not built.
The clickable HTML prototype already has: a landing page; a phone app with Home (€437,000 balance) and a notification bell with 2 notifications (investments up; equity fund down) that open detail screens; My Wealth Snapshot (donut: current account €42k, savings & deposits €238k, investments €157k; 36% invested; deposit interest and investment costs per year; a nudge that a €120,000 term deposit matures on 12.11.2026; Life Profile shown as done); Learn (5 topics); Investment Playground (slider plus a virtual €10k portfolio with Tesla, Mercedes-Benz, Global Equity Fund, Euro Bond Fund and Cash; donut, holdings ±€500, 12-month line, 1/5/10-year fan chart, market-fall bars); a scripted AI Wealth Assistant chat; Goals; Wealth Insights (headline, allocation bar, 4 picks: cash cushion €50k, bond fund & government bonds €150k as top idea, balanced fund €80k, keep current investments €157k); Talk to an Adviser (context passed; video / chat / callback; booking modal). All demo data.
HMW: How might we give mass-affluent customers outside full Premium advice clear, affordable guidance that starts from their own situation and is there when they choose to ask for it, so they can make confident investment decisions with their bank?
Rules: AI explains, banker recommends (MiFID II); AI must disclose itself (AI Act, from 02.08.2026); GDPR consent for profile data; no cold calls; not digital-only; success = a confident, suitable decision, not "invest more".

KEY HYPOTHESES TO TEST:
1. The unlock / invitation to My Wealth feels helpful, not like sales. (Open question 1; RH1, RH3)
2. Customers complete the Life Profile and find the result accurate ("this is me"). (Open question 2; RH1)
3. The Snapshot makes "invested vs not invested" and what I pay clear, and the deposit / bond / fund comparison is understood. (Open question 3; RH2)
4. Wealth Insights picks are understood and trusted as ideas, not as pushed products. (RH1, RH2, RH5; T1 feedback)
5. Customers press "Talk to an expert" at moments of doubt and value that their context is passed on. (Open question 4; RH3, RH4)
6. Branch-trusting or older customers can use My Wealth, or would use a branch version. (Open question 5; RH3, C2)
7. Notifications are wanted, not annoying. (T1 feedback; fix 7; RH3)

Create a comprehensive testing strategy:

   - Nice-to-know vs. need-to-know

     * Clickable wireframes (low fidelity)
     * High-fidelity mockups
     * Functional prototype
     * Wizard of Oz (fake the backend)
     * Concierge MVP (manually deliver the service)

   - Pros/cons of recommended approach

4. PROTOTYPE SCOPE

   - What to simulate vs. actually build?
   - What user flows to include?
   - What scenarios to test?
   - What can be represented with placeholder content?

   Which methods are appropriate:
   - Usability testing (think-aloud protocol)
   - Concept testing (show and get feedback)
   - A/B testing (compare alternatives)
   - Wizard of Oz testing (simulate functionality)
   - Desirability testing (reaction to value proposition)
   - Recommend best mix of methods

6. PARTICIPANT RECRUITMENT
   - How many participants needed? (typically 5-8 for usability)
   - What mix of user types? (personas, segments)
   - Recruitment criteria
   - How to recruit (similar to empathy interviews)
   - Incentive recommendation

7. TEST SCENARIOS & TASKS

   - Specific tasks to ask them to complete
   - How to set context without biasing

8. TEST SCRIPT STRUCTURE

   - Introduction and consent (5 min)
   - Background questions (5 min)
   - Prototype interaction (20-30 min)
   - Post-test questions (10-15 min)

9. OBSERVATION & MEASUREMENT

11. ITERATION PLAN

12. LOGISTICS & TIMELINE

13. ANALYSIS APPROACH
```

## Output

### 1. Testing objectives

**What we need to learn**

1. Does My Wealth feel like **guidance that starts from me**, or like another product push? (C1: "more of a product push")
2. Can customers **read their own position** (how much is invested, what it earns, what it costs) without help?
3. Do they understand the Wealth Insights picks as **ideas to discuss**, and trust them enough to take a next step?
4. Where do they want a **human**, and does "your context is passed on" matter to them? (C1: "I had to adjust my life around the availability of the adviser")
5. Does the concept work, or have a credible path, for **branch-trusting customers** (C2: "If an offer is made in a bank branch, there is more trust")?
6. Are **notifications** (performance up/down, deposit maturing) welcome or intrusive?

**Decisions this testing informs**

- Keep, change or drop each part: unlock, Life Profile, Snapshot, Learn, Playground, AI, Insights, Adviser, nudges.
- Whether the **Playground** stays in the first version (fix 5: least evidence; the UX study found the old simulator confusing).
- Whether the **branch path** must be built before the pitch, or shown as a storyboard.
- What we ask **Compliance** (wording of "Top idea / Best fit for you", AI disclosure, profile consent).
- What we put in the **pitch on 04.11.2026** as evidence and what we label as open.

**What would make us pivot or change direction**

- Most participants describe Wealth Insights or the unlock as **"selling"**, even after wording changes: rethink the bank-led framing (for example start from Goals, not from "€280,000 is sitting in cash").
- Participants do not care about the Snapshot or Insights and only want a cheaper product: the problem is **cost**, not guidance (RH2); pivot toward fix 1 (cost comparer) and the exit moment (fix 2).
- C2-type participants reject the app entirely **and** the branch version: the concept serves only digital customers; it must be repositioned as one channel of a wider service.
- Nobody presses "Talk to an expert", or they press it but say the context does not matter: the hand-over (RH4) is not the value; focus on self-service clarity.

**Success criteria for the test (overall)**

The concept passes this stage if, across round 2, most participants (target ≥ 5 of 7, see section 10) can explain their own position and one next step in their own words, describe the Insights as "ideas" rather than "selling", and name a moment when they would contact an expert. With 5–8 people this is a **first signal**, not validation.

### 3. Prototype fidelity recommendation

**Recommendation: keep the existing high-fidelity clickable HTML prototype as the base, combined with Wizard of Oz for the AI and the adviser, and a paper/tablet mock-up for the branch path.** Do not build a functional prototype in this programme.

| Option | Use for My Wealth? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Paper prototype / sketches | **Yes, for the branch path and the unlock / Life Profile questions** | Not in the HTML yet; fast to draw; feels natural in a branch role-play |
| Clickable wireframes | No | We already have higher fidelity |
| High-fidelity mock-up | **Yes, already built** | Needed to test clarity of numbers, charts and wording |
| Functional prototype | No | Real data and AI are out of reach before 04.11.2026; not needed to test desirability |
| Wizard of Oz | **Yes, for AI chat and "adviser"** | The AI is scripted (3 answer patterns); a team member can type or speak answers live, and play the adviser after booking |
| Concierge MVP | Partly | A team member can "be the banker" and walk a C2-type through the Life Profile on paper |

**What the current prototype can test**

- Understanding of the Snapshot, the 36% invested figure, deposit interest vs investment costs.
- Reaction to the deposit-maturity nudge and the two notifications.
- Understanding and trust of the 4 Wealth Insights picks and the disclaimer.
- Whether and when people press "Talk to an Adviser", and whether "the adviser receives your goal…" matters to them.
- First reactions to Learn and the Playground (clarity of virtual money, fan chart, market fall).

**What it cannot test (be honest in the pitch)**

- **No unlock moment:** Home opens straight into My Wealth; there is no "You've unlocked My Wealth" message. Add a screen or show it on paper.
- **No Life Profile questions:** it is shown as "Done". We cannot test completion or accuracy without adding the questions (paper or a new screen).
- **No real data:** participants see Arianit's demo €437,000, not their own money; reactions to "your" numbers are hypothetical.
- **Scripted AI:** only keyword answers; no AI self-disclosure line beyond the screen title ("AI Wealth Assistant") and the footer note. Free questions get a generic answer.
- **No branch version, no exit moment (fix 2), no opt-in below threshold (fix 4), no nudge settings (fix 7), no GDPR consent screen (fix 6).**
- **No real booking:** the modal only confirms; the adviser receives nothing.
- **Small inconsistencies to fix before round 2:** the Adviser screen shows main goal "Grow money" while the Life Profile says "stability, 5–10 years"; Home labels €437,000 as "available balance" although only €42,000 is in the current account; "Best fit for you / Top idea" may read as a personal recommendation (MiFID; Compliance check).

### 5. Testing methods to use

| Method | Use | For hypotheses |
|---|---|---|
| **Usability test, think-aloud** | Main method, tasks 1–5 below | H2, H3, H5, H7 |
| **Concept / desirability test** | First reaction to the unlock card and the landing page; 3–5 word choice ("helpful", "pushy", "clear", "confusing", "trustworthy"…) | H1, H4 |
| **Wizard of Oz** | Live AI answers; team member as adviser on a short "video call" | H5 |
| **Role-play / concierge** | Branch path with a C2-type participant | H6 |
| **Light A/B (between participants)** | Insights header "Top idea / Best fit for you" vs "One idea to discuss"; or nudge with and without "No pressure: keeping it is fine too" | H4, H1 |

**Recommended mix:** 25 minutes think-aloud on the phone path, 5 minutes desirability words, plus the branch role-play for C2-types. Use the A/B only if we reach at least 6 participants; with fewer, treat it as a comparison of comments, not a measurement.

### 6. Participant recruitment

- **Number:** 5–8 people in round 2. Realistic target: **6–7**.
- **Mix (minimum):** at least **2 Self-Directed Comparer (C1-type)**, at least **2 Cautious Branch Saver (C2-type)**, ideally **1–2 New-to-Investing Learner (UX-type)**, and at least one person aged 55+ if possible.
- **Screening questions (from the empathy interviews):**
  1. Do you have savings or investments of roughly [threshold, This requires verification] or more, with any bank?
  2. Do you currently have a dedicated Premium / Private adviser? (exclude "yes")
  3. Do you invest today? Where do you get advice (bank, family, online, ChatGPT)?
  4. How do you prefer to deal with your bank: app, branch, phone?
- **Do not ask** for exact amounts, account numbers or employer. Do not record personal financial data.
- **How to recruit:** the same way as the street interviews in Budapest (13.–16.10.2026), plus colleagues' friends and family outside RBI sales roles. RBI employees can be used for usability only, not as proof of customer desirability; mark them separately. If testing real RBI customers, check with the coaches and Compliance first (This requires verification).
- **Consent:** short verbal or written consent; no recording without permission; notes anonymised (IDs T2, T3, …).
- **Incentive:** a small thank-you (coffee, chocolate, RBI give-away). No cash or bank product benefits, to avoid any link to sales (check with the coaches; This requires verification).

### 7. Test scenarios & tasks

**Context (read the same way to everyone, neutral):**

> "This is an early idea for a banking app, not a real product. All numbers are made up. Imagine this is your bank account. We are testing the idea, not you. There are no wrong answers. Please say out loud what you think as you go."

**Scenario:** "Imagine you have about €437,000 with your bank, mostly in savings and deposits. You get a message that a €120,000 term deposit ends on 12.11.2026, and you need to decide what to do."

**Tasks**

| # | Task (what we say) | What it tests | Success |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Look at this message (unlock card). What do you think it means? How does it make you feel? | H1 | Describes it as an offer of help / overview, not "they want to sell me something" |
| 1 | Fill in these questions about yourself (Life Profile, paper). Does the result describe you? | H2 | Completes; says result is accurate or names what is wrong |
| 2 | Open My Wealth. How much of your money is invested, and what do you pay for it per year? | H3 | Finds 36% / €157,000 and €1,880 / 1.2% unaided |
| 3 | Your deposit is ending. Find out what you could do. | H3, H4 | Reaches Wealth Insights; explains one pick in own words |
| 4 | Is anything here telling you what to buy? Would you trust these ideas? | H4 | Says "ideas, to check with someone"; gives a trust reason |
| 5 | You have a notification. Open it. What would you do now? | H7, H5 | Opens bell; reacts to the fall without panic; names next step |
| 6 | Suppose you are not sure. What would you do? | H5 | Finds "Talk to an Adviser"; comments on context being passed |
| 7 | (Optional) Ask the assistant anything you want. | H5, AI disclosure | Notices it is AI; knows it does not give personal advice |
| 8 | (C2-types) Imagine you are in the branch; the banker shows you this on a tablet and gives you this printed page. | H6 | Prefers / accepts this path; says what builds trust |

**Avoid bias:** do not say "invest", "advice", "AI" or "recommendation" before the participant does. Do not name the button they should press. If stuck for over 60 seconds, ask "What are you looking for?" and note the hint.

### 8. Test script structure (about 45 minutes; a 25-minute street version is below)

| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction and consent | 5 min | Who we are (Innovation School team, not sales); fictional data; consent for notes; can stop at any time |
| Background | 5 min | How they bank (app/branch); do they save / invest; who they ask for money advice (family, ChatGPT, bank); last money decision |
| Prototype interaction | 20–25 min | Tasks 0–7 (task 8 for C2-types), think-aloud |
| Post-test questions | 10 min | Ratings (below) and open questions |
| Wrap-up | 5 min | "What one thing would you change?"; thank-you; how notes are used |

**Street version (25 min):** intro 2 min, background 3 min, tasks 2, 3, 4, 6 (and 8 for C2-types) 15 min, 3 ratings and one open question 5 min.

**Post-test questions**

- "In one sentence, what is My Wealth for?"
- "Did any part feel like the bank selling to you? Which?"
- "Would this make you more or less confident about your deposit decision? Why?"
- "When would you want a real person?"
- "Compared with what you do today (family, ChatGPT, adviser), is this better, worse or the same?"
- "Would you want these notifications? Which, and how often?"
- "What is missing or confusing?"

**Ratings (1–5, after the tasks)**

1. Easy to understand
2. Feels like help for me, not selling
3. I trust the ideas shown
4. I would feel more confident about my decision
5. I would use this (or the branch version)

### 10. Success metrics

Thresholds are for round 2 with about 6–7 participants. With this sample they are **signals, not proof**.

| Hypothesis | Quantitative threshold | Qualitative signal | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 Unlock helpful | ≥ 5 of 7 rate "help, not selling" ≥ 4 | "Nice that they show me this" | Majority says "they want me to invest" |
| H2 Life Profile | ≥ 6 of 7 complete; ≥ 5 say result fits | Adds own life context unprompted | Questions feel like MiFID / interrogation |
| H3 Snapshot & cost clear | ≥ 5 of 7 find invested share and annual cost unaided | "I didn't know I paid that" | Cannot read the donut or confuses interest and cost |
| H4 Insights trusted as ideas | ≥ 5 of 7 describe picks as ideas; trust rating ≥ 3.5 average | Wants to discuss the plan | "Top idea" read as order or sales; distrust of the bank's interest |
| H5 Expert at doubt | ≥ 4 of 7 press Adviser unprompted at a doubt moment; ≥ 4 value context passed | "Good, I don't have to explain again" | No one wants a person, or adviser seen as sales call |
| H6 Branch / older | Both C2-types accept the app or the branch version | Trust comes from face-to-face plan | C2-types reject both paths |
| H7 Notifications | ≥ 5 of 7 want at least one; ≥ 4 want to choose which | Down-notification calms rather than scares | Feels like pressure or spam |

**Go / no-go for the pitch**

- **Go (pitch My Wealth as tested concept):** H3, H4 and H5 meet their signals and no red flag appears for H1 or H4.
- **Go with changes:** 1–2 of H3/H4/H5 missed, but the reason is wording or layout, fixable in one iteration.
- **Rethink:** sales red flags on H1 or H4 in most sessions after the round-2 wording changes, or C2-types reject both channels.

The success measure stays **confident, suitable decisions**, including "keep the deposit". A participant who decides to keep cash with confidence counts as a success.

### 13. Analysis approach

- **Synthesis:** put every observation on a Miro sticky (ID, screen, quote or behaviour, hypothesis). Cluster by hypothesis H1–H7, then by screen. Run prompt **4.3.3 Test Results Analysis** on the real notes only.
- **Prioritise issues:** severity (blocks task / causes distrust / cosmetic) × frequency (how many participants). Any trust or sales issue is high severity by default, because trust is the core of the concept.
- **Fix vs accept:** fix anything that causes sales feeling, wrong reading of numbers, or missed expert access. Accept cosmetic issues and individual taste (for example colours) unless ≥ 3 participants mention them. Compliance issues are fixed regardless of customer view.
- **Communicate:** one page per hypothesis (signal, evidence count, 1–2 quotes, decision) in `docs/challenges/05-prototype-test.md`; decisions in `team-log.md`; in the pitch, show what was tested, with whom (types, not names) and what is still open. Always state the sample size; never call it "validated".

## What this means for our test
