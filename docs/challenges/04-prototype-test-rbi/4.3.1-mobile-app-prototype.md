## Filled prompt

```text
You are an experienced product designer, UX/UI designer, UX writer, product owner, and front-end developer.

Your task is to create a polished, realistic, interactive visual prototype for the solution described below.

The prototype will be shown to and potentially tested with real users. It must therefore feel like a credible digital product that could actually exist, not like a wireframe, internal concept, consulting slide, or technical demo.

==================================================
1. PRODUCT CONTEXT
==================================================

SOLUTION:
My Wealth is a new section inside the existing RBI mobile app (My Life in Hungary). It is unlocked when a customer reaches the mass-affluent threshold, with an opt-in for customers just below it. It brings together a My Wealth Snapshot (cash, deposits, investments, what they earn and what they cost), a short Life Profile, 2–3 minute Learn lessons, an Investment Playground with virtual money, an optional AI Wealth Assistant that explains (and says it is AI), Wealth Insights with a few illustrative ideas, and an always-visible "Talk to an expert" path (call, video via RaiConnect, appointment in a branch). The adviser receives the Life Profile and what the customer explored, so the conversation does not start from zero. Opt-in nudges (a deposit maturing, a market fall) bring the customer back at the right moment.

CORE PROBLEM:
Outside full Premium advice, guidance feels product-led, unclear, costly and on the bank's terms, so customers decide elsewhere (family, ChatGPT, cheaper online providers, government bonds). C1: "It was not too personalised… I felt it was more of a product push." C1: "I had to adjust my life around the availability of the adviser." C2: "If it is by telephone, then no."

TARGET USER:
Primary: the Self-Directed Comparer (C1). Digitally comfortable, has invested in ETFs for about 2 years, cancelled the bank investment account because of cost, now relies on a nephew and ChatGPT. Wants personal, cheaper, on-demand guidance that starts from their life situation and near-term plans. Design constraint: the Cautious Branch Saver (C2), a long-time saver who trusts the branch and contact they start themselves, and rejects phone, internet and ad offers. Secondary: the New-to-Investing Learner (UX study), who needs simple explanations and a small, safe first step. Age, job and amounts are not known for any persona; do not invent them.

CORE VALUE PROPOSITION:
See all your money with the bank in one place, understand in plain words what it earns and what it costs, explore ideas that start from your own goals, and reach a real expert when you choose to, without a sales push.

PRIMARY USER GOAL:
Make a confident, suitable decision about their savings (keeping cash counts as a good outcome), for example what to do with a term deposit that matures on 12.11.2026, and get human help on their own terms if they want it.

SPECIFIC FEATURE / JOURNEY TO PROTOTYPE:
Core path: Home → My Wealth (Snapshot with costs, deposit-maturity nudge) → Wealth Insights (4 illustrative ideas with amount, one "why", risk and cost) → Talk to an Adviser (context passed on) → booking confirmation. Doubt path: notification bell → "Global Equity Fund fell 4.2%" → detail → Ask AI or Talk to an expert. Learner path: My Wealth → Learn → Playground → Discuss it with an adviser. Concept elements to show: the unlock message ("You've unlocked My Wealth: a new way to understand, plan and manage your money") and the Life Profile questions.

CONTEXT OF USE:
On the phone, at home or on the go, at a moment the customer chooses: after an opt-in notification (deposit maturing, investments up or down), when they have extra money to place (C1: "If I have additional money again, what should I focus on?"), or before talking to an adviser. The prototype is in English with euro demo data; a real Hungarian version would use Hungarian and HUF (This requires verification).

ASSUMPTIONS TO TEST:
From 4.1.1: H1 the unlock feels helpful, not like sales. H2 customers complete the Life Profile and find the result accurate. H3 the Snapshot makes invested vs not invested and annual cost clear. H4 Wealth Insights picks are understood and trusted as ideas, not pushed products. H5 customers press "Talk to an expert" at moments of doubt and value that their context is passed on. H6 branch-trusting or older customers can use My Wealth or would use a branch version. H7 notifications are wanted, not annoying.

==================================================
2. PROTOTYPE OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Design the experience around the user's goal rather than around the organisation's internal structure.

The prototype should:

- Make the value proposition understandable within the first screen
- Immediately answer "What can I do here?" and "Why is this useful to me?"
- Guide the user naturally toward one clear next action
- Minimise cognitive load
- Make complex topics feel simple and manageable
- Use progressive disclosure rather than showing everything at once
- Feel trustworthy without feeling bureaucratic
- Show realistic content rather than placeholder text
- Demonstrate the core value proposition through interaction
- Give users feedback after important actions
- Always provide a clear next step
- Feel like a coherent product from beginning to end

Prioritise the core experience. Do not add features simply to make the prototype look more sophisticated.

==================================================
3. INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE & USER FLOW
==================================================

Before building, internally determine the simplest user journey that demonstrates the value proposition.

Create approximately 5-7 meaningful screens or states.

A typical structure could include:

1. Landing / Home
2. Initial choice or onboarding
3. Main feature interaction
4. Input / information collection
5. Result / recommendation
6. Detail or next action
7. Confirmation / progress state

Adapt this structure to the specific solution rather than following it mechanically.

Each screen must have:

- One clear purpose
- One dominant message
- One obvious primary action
- Minimal competing information

The user should never wonder:
- Where am I?
- What am I supposed to do?
- What happens if I tap this?
- What should I do next?

==================================================
4. UX WRITING
==================================================

Write all visible interface copy as part of the product design.

Apply these principles:

CLARITY OVER CLEVERNESS
Use direct language that immediately communicates meaning.

CONCISE
Remove unnecessary words.

ACTIVE VOICE
Tell users clearly what they can do.

CONVERSATIONAL
Write like a knowledgeable, helpful human.

ACCESSIBLE
Avoid jargon and unexplained terminology.

POSITIVE
Focus on what users can do and what happens next.

SPECIFIC
Buttons should describe the actual action or outcome.

Examples:

BAD:
"Submit"

BETTER:
"Create my business plan"

BAD:
"Proceed"

BETTER:
"See my next steps"

BAD:
"Error. Invalid input."

BETTER:
"We couldn't read this document. Try uploading a clearer photo."

Do not expose internal terminology, technical architecture, product-management language, banking jargon, compliance terminology, or consulting language unless absolutely necessary for the user.

Do not end short UI labels, headings, card titles, or buttons with full stops.

==================================================
5. SCREEN CONTENT
==================================================

For every screen, create realistic production-quality content including where relevant:

- Headline
- Supporting text
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA
- Navigation
- Cards
- Labels
- Form fields
- Placeholder text
- Helper text
- Tooltips
- Recommendations
- Progress indicators
- Empty states
- Loading states
- Success states
- Error messages
- Confirmation messages

Do not use Lorem Ipsum.

Do not leave generic placeholder content such as:
"Feature 1"
"Example text"
"Option A"
"Lorem ipsum"

The prototype should contain enough realistic content that someone could genuinely test the concept.

==================================================
6. CTA & INTERACTION DESIGN
==================================================

Use action-oriented CTAs.

Prefer:

"Build my plan"
"Check my options"
"Upload document"
"See my next steps"
"Compare options"
"Ask a question"
"Review my information"
"Save and continue"

Avoid vague labels such as:

"Submit"
"Proceed"
"More"
"Next"

unless the context makes their outcome completely obvious.

Important buttons, cards, tabs, navigation items, filters, back buttons, and selection controls should be interactive.

Interactions should visibly change the interface.

Where helpful, include:

- Selected states
- Expand/collapse states
- Progress states
- Modals
- Bottom sheets
- Toasts
- Confirmation states
- Form validation
- Loading feedback
- Dynamic results

Avoid creating interactions that do nothing.

==================================================
7. ERROR, SUCCESS & EMPTY STATES
==================================================

ERROR MESSAGES

Structure them around:

1. What happened
2. What the user can do to fix it

Avoid technical error codes unless genuinely useful.

Example:

BAD:
"Upload failed. Error 403."

GOOD:
"We couldn't upload your document. Check your connection and try again."

SUCCESS MESSAGES

Confirm:
1. What was completed
2. What happens next
3. What the user can do now

EMPTY STATES

Never show an unexplained blank area.

Explain:
1. Why there is nothing here yet
2. What the user can do to get started

==================================================
8. VISUAL DESIGN
==================================================

Create a premium, modern, approachable mobile product.

Design principles:

- Mobile-first
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Generous whitespace
- Rounded cards and controls
- Soft shadows used carefully
- Clear typography
- Short readable content blocks
- Large touch targets
- Consistent spacing
- Visually obvious CTAs
- Subtle depth
- Minimal clutter
- High perceived quality

BRAND / VISUAL DIRECTION:

Primary accent:
RBI-style yellow

Primary text/navigation:
Black or near black

Background:
Warm off-white / very light neutral

Supporting colours:
Muted neutral tones with restrained use of status colours

Overall feeling:
Modern financial technology meets friendly digital guidance

Avoid:
- Corporate PowerPoint aesthetics
- Dense dashboards
- Excessive gradients
- Tiny typography
- Large amounts of explanatory text
- Too many cards on one screen
- Overuse of icons
- Decorative elements without a purpose

==================================================
9. MOBILE PRESENTATION
==================================================

Present the prototype as a realistic mobile web application.

If displaying the prototype on a desktop viewport:

- Place it inside a clean smartphone frame
- Keep the phone straight
- Keep it vertically oriented
- Center it horizontally
- Do not rotate or tilt the device
- Give the surrounding presentation enough breathing room
- Make the product screen the dominant visual element

The experience itself must remain responsive.

==================================================
10. NAVIGATION
==================================================

Use navigation appropriate to the product.

If bottom navigation is appropriate, use approximately 4-5 clearly differentiated destinations.

For this concept use:

- Home
- My Wealth
- Learn
- Adviser

Example:
- Home
- Plan
- Ask
- Bank
- Experts

Use simple, recognisable icons accompanied by text labels.

Clearly indicate the active destination.

Navigation should work.

==================================================
11. TRUST & GUIDANCE
==================================================

Because this is a business-oriented product, communicate trust through design and content rather than through excessive disclaimers.

When information touches financial, tax, legal, regulatory, or similarly sensitive topics:

- Clearly distinguish guidance from professional advice
- Do not present fictional recommendations as authoritative professional advice
- Make assumptions visible where relevant
- Explain why sensitive information is requested
- Give the user control over important decisions

Any fictional experts, companies, users, financial values, documents, or personal information must clearly be fictional demo data.

==================================================
12. ACCESSIBILITY
==================================================

Build accessibility into the experience.

Include:

- Sufficient colour contrast
- Large touch targets
- Visible focus states
- Semantic HTML
- Accessible form labels
- Meaningful button and link labels
- Error messages associated with their relevant fields
- Alt descriptions where relevant
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate status
- Keyboard-accessible interactions where practical

==================================================
13. TECHNICAL OUTPUT
==================================================

Create ONE complete, self-contained HTML file.

Requirements:

- HTML, CSS, and JavaScript must all be contained in the same file
- No external libraries
- No frameworks
- No external fonts
- No external APIs
- No external images
- No server
- No build process
- Must open directly in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome
- Responsive design
- Optimised primarily for a smartphone viewport
- All important navigation and interactions must work
- Use CSS and inline SVG where useful for visual elements and icons

The final output must be immediately executable as an HTML file.

==================================================
14. QUALITY BAR
==================================================

Before finalising, evaluate the prototype against these questions:

VALUE
Can a new user understand the value within 5 seconds?

CLARITY
Is it obvious what the user should do next?

REALISM
Does this look and behave like a real digital product?

FOCUS
Does every screen have one dominant purpose?

COPY
Could a real business owner understand the language without additional explanation?

INTERACTION
Do the important interactions actually work?

TRUST
Does the experience feel trustworthy without becoming bureaucratic?

CONSISTENCY
Are terminology, colours, interaction patterns, spacing, and navigation consistent?

SIMPLICITY
Can anything unnecessary be removed?

VISUAL QUALITY
Would this be credible in a professional user test or stakeholder presentation?

If any answer is no, improve the prototype before returning the final result.

==================================================
15. PRODUCT-SPECIFIC CONTENT
==================================================

Now apply all instructions above using the following detailed concept:

Concept "My Wealth" (docs/challenges/04-ideate.md, proposed 14.10.2026, team confirmation pending).
Flow: Threshold reached → Unlock My Wealth → Life Profile → Learn / Playground → AI if wanted → Expert if needed → Real investment or advice → Ongoing reviews. For older or AI-wary customers: Life Profile → Learn → Talk to an expert; they never need the AI.
Parts:
- Unlock: the bank detects the threshold from what it already knows. Message: "You've unlocked My Wealth: a new way to understand, plan and manage your money." Never "You have €50k, please invest".
- My Wealth Snapshot: cash/deposits, investments, total with the bank, what deposits earn, what investments cost per year, goal progress.
- Life Profile: what are you saving for, when you need the money, how important access is, comfort with losses, experience, stability or growth. Result in plain words, e.g. "You value stability, have a 5–10 year horizon and want to grow savings." Not investment advice; ask GDPR consent. The formal MiFID process happens later.
- Learn: 2–3 minute lessons matched to the profile (risk vs return, funds and bonds, inflation, diversification, fees). Reuses Learn to Invest.
- Investment Playground: virtual money, simple portfolio, simulate 1/5/10 years and a market fall. Keep it simple; show clearly what was invested and what was gain.
- AI Wealth Assistant: optional; explains ("Why is this fund risky?", "Explain bonds simply"); says it is AI; personal recommendations stay in the approved advisory process.
- Talk to an Expert: always visible; call now, video (RaiConnect), book an appointment (incl. branch). The banker receives the Life Profile and what was explored.
- Ongoing nudges (opt-in): "Your deposit matures next month: review your options." "See how your plan has progressed." Customer chooses which and how often. No cold calls.
Fixes to include: show costs and a deposit vs government bond vs fund comparer; use the exit moment (cheaper option or talk to an expert); branch way in (banker opens the same Life Profile on a tablet, prints a one-page plan); opt-in below the threshold; simple Playground; "this is not investment advice" + GDPR consent; opt-in nudges.
Guardrails: AI explains, banker recommends (MiFID II); AI discloses itself (AI Act, from 02.08.2026); success = a confident, suitable decision, not "invest more"; not digital-only; reuse My Life, RaiConnect, advisory tool, Learn to Invest.
Real customer language to reuse: "product push", "It wasn't clear", "It costs too much money", "If I get lost, there is immediate support" (C1); "If an offer is made in a bank branch, there is more trust" (C2).
Round-1 feedback (T1, 15.10.2026) to reflect: a notification bell with two clickable notifications (investments up / down); Wealth Insights clearer, "what exactly is good to invest and why", attractive but not a lot of information.
All people, balances, funds, returns and costs are fictional demo data.

If some details are missing, make sensible UX assumptions that support the core concept rather than adding unnecessary features.

Prioritise demonstrating the core user value over completeness.

==================================================
FINAL OUTPUT
==================================================

Return the complete working HTML prototype.

Do not return a wireframe.

Do not return only a UX specification.

Do not explain how someone else should build it.

Actually build the interface.

The result should be polished enough that I can open it in a browser, put the phone in front of a user, and say:

"Imagine this product exists. Please use it as you naturally would."
```

## What this means for round 2

- The prototype tests **clarity and trust** well (Snapshot, costs, notifications, Insights, adviser access), but it **cannot test H1 (unlock) or H2 (Life Profile)**, because those screens do not exist. Add them before round 2 (fix 1), even as simple screens.
- Fix the **contradictory demo numbers** first (fix 2). They are quick to fix and would otherwise distort every reading of H3 and trust.
