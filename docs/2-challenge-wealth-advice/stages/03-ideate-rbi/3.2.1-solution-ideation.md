# 3.2.1 Solution Ideation — mass-affluent wealth advice

RBI AI Prompt Library prompt 3.2.1, run 14.10.2026 with skill innovation-ideate. AI-generated ideas as input for the team; not decisions. Evidence base: 2 experts, 2 street interviews, UX study (small n).

> **How to read this:** the ideas come from the Stage 2 synthesis ([02-empathize.md](../02-empathize.md), [2.3.1](../02-empathize-rbi/2.3.1-interview-pattern-analysis.md)–[2.4](../02-empathize-rbi/2.4-problem-one-pager.md)) and the Stage 1 benchmarks ([1.1.2](../01-understand-rbi/1.1.2-competitive-landscape.md), [1.1.3](../01-understand-rbi/1.1.3-technology-trends.md)). Examples from other companies are what those companies advertise, not proven results. RBI capabilities (My Life, RaiConnect, advisory tool, Learn to Invest, RAIA) were reported by our 2 experts; their exact scope, and whether they can be extended, requires verification. The **Team idea** (Money Guide) is one idea among many and is not favoured here.

### 1. Technology-driven solutions

**1.1 Life Profile Layer**
A 2-minute set of life questions (what the money is for, when it is needed, plans for the next 1–3 years, contact preference) added before or next to the MiFID questionnaire in My Life. It is saved once and can be reused in the app, RaiConnect video and the branch. It does not replace the MiFID suitability assessment.
- Needs: N1, N4 · Impact: high · Feasibility: medium (data model and GDPR consent; integration with My Life requires verification)
- Example: Finax named goal accounts; Vanguard goal-based journeys (advertised).

**1.2 "Explain My Options" assistant (RAIA extended)**
A generative AI assistant inside the app that explains bank-approved content about deposits, government bonds and funds in plain language, and answers "what does this mean?" questions. It is clearly labelled as AI, gives no personal recommendation, and offers a human at any point.
- Needs: N3, N5 · Impact: high · Feasibility: hard (MiFID line between explaining and recommending; AI Act transparency; hallucination risk named by ESMA)
- Example: DBS digibot (Gen AI, human still reachable); Khan Academy Khanmigo as a guided tutor.

**1.3 Side-by-side Comparer**
A rules-based (not AI) tool that puts a term deposit, a Hungarian retail government bond and a bank fund side by side for the customer's amount and time horizon: expected yearly cost in HUF, what can go wrong, how fast you can get money out, and tax notes. Tax and bond details require verification.
- Needs: N3, N5, N6 · Impact: high · Feasibility: medium
- Example: comparison sites; ING Smart Invest publishes fees in a table.

**1.4 Bring-Your-Own-Portfolio check**
The customer lists the funds they hold anywhere (as C1 does with ChatGPT) and the app shows, in plain language, the costs, the risk mix and overlaps. It is an information overview, not a recommendation.
- Needs: N5, N3 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: hard (data entry or aggregation; MiFID boundary; FiDA status requires verification)
- Example: C1's own ChatGPT workaround; account aggregation under PSD2.

**1.5 Tell-It-Once Hand-over**
When the customer taps "talk to someone", their life profile, the options they looked at and their open question go to the RaiConnect expert or branch banker before the call. The customer does not repeat their story.
- Needs: N4, N2 · Impact: high · Feasibility: medium (whether the app can pass context to RaiConnect or CRM today is not known; this requires verification)
- Example: NHS 111 passes the triage result to the clinician; Erste George starts a banker call from the app (advertised).

**1.6 Customer-Controlled Nudge Centre**
The customer chooses which notifications they want (deposit maturing, goal off track, new lesson) and how often. No marketing messages unless they opt in.
- Needs: N2 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy–medium
- Example: DBS personalised nudges, here with the customer in control.

**1.7 Banker Copilot Brief and Notes**
Before a video or branch session, AI drafts a short brief from the life profile; after the session, it drafts notes and a follow-up summary that the banker edits. This frees banker time to serve more customers outside Premium.
- Needs: N4 (capacity) · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: medium (consent to record; data protection review)
- Example: Morgan Stanley Debrief (company-reported time savings, anecdotal).

### 2. Process redesign solutions

**2.1 Life Before Product (reorder)**
Start the My Life investment journey with "what is this money for, and when do you need it?" and only then show options. The same order applies in the branch and on video.
- Needs: N1 · Impact: high · Feasibility: easy–medium
- Example: goal-first journeys at Finax and Vanguard.

**2.2 No Cold Calls (eliminate)**
Stop outbound phone sales for investment products in this segment. Replace them with one consent-based invitation by app or letter, and let the customer start the contact.
- Needs: N2 · Impact: high · Feasibility: easy as a policy; effect on sales unknown and needs a test
- Example: none found in our benchmarks; supported by C2 and reported HU customer research.

**2.3 Fair Exit Moment**
When a customer closes an investment account, show a short, honest screen or script: what they pay today, cheaper options at the bank (if any), and a "talk to someone first" button. The reason for leaving is recorded for the product team. No pressure and no hidden steps.
- Needs: N6, N3 · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: easy–medium
- Example: subscription retention flows (telecom, streaming), but without "dark patterns" (tricks that make leaving hard).

**2.4 Three Paths, One Journey**
At every step of the investment journey, three clear options: "do it myself", "ask a question", "talk to an expert". The customer can move between them without starting again.
- Needs: N2, N4 · Impact: high · Feasibility: medium
- Example: TurboTax do-it-yourself / on-demand expert / full service (advertised).

**2.5 Start in Branch, Finish in App (and back)**
A customer can start a conversation with a banker in the branch and finish the investment later in the app, or start in the app and finish in the branch, with the same profile and options.
- Needs: N2 (serves C2 and C1) · Impact: medium · Feasibility: medium
- Example: omnichannel retail ("buy online, pick up in store").

**2.6 Triage Before Booking**
Three short questions route the customer: a general question goes to content or the AI explainer, a specific question to a written expert answer, a real decision to a video or branch session. Human time goes where it matters most.
- Needs: N4 · Impact: high · Feasibility: medium
- Example: NHS 111 digital triage (advertised).

**2.7 Light Yearly Check-in**
An optional yearly review for customers outside Premium: a 10-minute digital check of goals and plans, with a short video or branch session if something changed. The customer chooses whether and how.
- Needs: N1, N4 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: medium (adviser capacity; Premium RMs reportedly contact clients 2–3 times a year, so full coverage does not scale)
- Example: Vanguard and Nutmeg ongoing planning (advertised).

### 3. Human-centered / support solutions

**3.1 Guidance Desk for Non-Premium (RaiConnect extended)**
Open a part of the RaiConnect video team to mass-affluent customers outside Premium, on request only, including evening slots. Sessions are guidance-first, not product-first.
- Needs: N4, N2 · Impact: high · Feasibility: medium (capacity, cost and licensing of the team require verification)
- Example: Raiffeisen Hungary RaiConnect for Premium; Vanguard Personal Advisor pooled team.

**3.2 Branch Money Hour**
Fixed weekly walk-in hours in selected branches where anyone can bring a savings or investment question without an appointment. No sales target for these hours.
- Needs: N2 (C2), N4 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy–medium
- Example: "Genius Bar" style walk-in help (Apple).

**3.3 Bring Your Family**
Customers can invite a family member (for example the nephew C1 trusts) to join a video or branch session, or to see a shared summary with consent.
- Needs: N5 · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: easy
- Example: Finax family account monitoring (advertised).

**3.4 One-Off Guidance Session at a Fixed Price**
A 45-minute planning session for customers below the Premium threshold, at a fixed and published price (price to be tested). The customer pays for help, not for a product.
- Needs: N3, N4 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: medium (pricing and MiFID set-up require verification)
- Example: Moneyfarm lets customers below the threshold unlock Private for GBP 12.99/month; Nutmeg free guidance call (advertised).

**3.5 Moderated Saver Community**
A Learn to Invest community where customers ask questions and bank experts answer in public, so others learn too. Strict moderation; no personal recommendations in public.
- Needs: N5, N3 · Impact: low–medium · Feasibility: medium (moderation and compliance effort)
- Example: online learning communities; Erste Premium webinars with recordings.

**3.6 Measured on Confidence, Not Volume**
Guidance staff serving this segment are measured on customer clarity and confidence and on suitable outcomes, not on product volume. This addresses the "product push" feeling at its root.
- Needs: N1, N2 · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: hard (organisational change)
- Example: Stage 1 trend research names outcome-based targets as an organisational change.

**3.7 Live Learn to Invest Sessions**
Short live webinars with Q&A based on the Learn to Invest module and podcast, also available as recordings, on topics customers raise (for example "deposit or government bond?").
- Needs: N3 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy
- Example: Erste Hungary Premium webinars (advertised).

### 4. Hybrid (tech + human) solutions

**4.1 Team idea — "Money Guide"**
When a customer's deposits or income pass the mass-affluent level, they get one consent-based invitation: an app notification for younger customers, a letter or branch conversation for older ones, never a cold call. Accepting unlocks a 2-minute life profile (goals, when money is needed, 1–3 year plans, a loss comfort slider, contact preference, optional family member) shared across app, video and branch; an in-app AI assistant that explains and compares a deposit, a government bond and a fund using the profile, with Learn to Invest lessons in context and a simple simulator; and a one-tap "Talk to an expert" to RaiConnect video or a branch booking, with context passed to the banker. The banker uses the advisory tool and gives the MiFID recommendation (**AI explains, banker recommends**). It adds customer-controlled notifications, an optional yearly check-in and a fair offer when an investment account is closed, and serves older customers through the branch with a printed plan.
- Needs: N1, N2, N3, N4, N5, N6 · Impact: high (if the parts work together) · Feasibility: hard as a whole within 6–12 months; medium if built in stages
- Example: combines patterns from NHS 111 (triage), TurboTax (levels of help), RaiConnect (video), Finax (goals).
- Note for scoring: Money Guide **bundles** several ideas listed separately here (1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.5, 1.6, 2.2, 2.3, 2.7, 4.4). The team can score it as a bundle and also score its parts on their own. Open questions: whether the AI part passes compliance, adviser capacity for the hand-over, and whether customers accept a bank-sent invitation at all (C2 rejects offers that come to them). This requires verification.

**4.2 Ask an Expert, Answer by Tomorrow**
The customer writes a question in the app; a licensed specialist answers in writing or with a short video within a stated time (for example one working day). One expert can serve many customers this way, without fixed appointments.
- Needs: N2, N4 · Impact: high · Feasibility: medium
- Example: Future fitness app (human coach works asynchronously); Vanguard callback within two business days (advertised).

**4.3 Second Opinion Before You Commit**
After choosing one of the three My Life options, the customer can tap "check with a person" and a specialist looks at the choice against their profile before money moves.
- Needs: N3, N4 · Impact: high · Feasibility: medium (capacity; MiFID role of the check requires verification)
- Example: TurboTax guaranteed expert review (advertised).

**4.4 Branch Guide on a Tablet, Printed Plan to Take Home**
Banker and customer use the same simplified life profile and comparer on a tablet in the branch. The customer leaves with a printed one-page plan, and the same plan appears in the app if they use it.
- Needs: N1, N2 (C2) · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: medium
- Example: OTP Pénzügyi Tervező planner used with the adviser (advertised).

**4.5 Callback at Your Chosen Time**
The customer picks a time slot for a call or video. Before the call, a short form or the AI assistant collects the question so the expert is prepared. The bank never calls without a booked slot.
- Needs: N2, N4 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy–medium
- Example: NHS 111 nurse callback; common in telecom support.

**4.6 Robo-Plus for Hungary**
A rules-based goal portfolio service (fixed, tested rules, not generative AI) for customers outside Premium, with a person on request. It would adapt the automated advice that Raiffeisenbank Czech Republic reportedly offers.
- Needs: N1, N4 · Impact: high · Feasibility: hard (product and regulatory work; the Czech model was not re-checked and requires verification)
- Example: Raiffeisenbank CZ automated advice; Moneyfarm, Betterment tiers.

**4.7 AI-Drafted, Banker-Approved Plan Summary**
After any session, AI drafts a one-page plain-language summary (your goals, the options discussed, the recommendation and why, costs). The banker checks and approves it before the customer gets it in the app or on paper.
- Needs: N3 · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: medium
- Example: Morgan Stanley Debrief follow-up drafts.

### 5. Communication / transparency solutions

**5.1 Cost in Forints, Not Percent**
For each option, show the expected yearly cost in HUF for the customer's amount ("about HUF 18,000 a year on HUF 2 million"), next to the percentage.
- Needs: N3, N6 · Impact: high · Feasibility: easy–medium
- Example: ING Smart Invest fee table (advertised).

**5.2 Help Ladder Page**
One simple page that shows each level of help (self-service, ask a question, video session, Premium), what it covers, how fast it is and what it costs, including help below Premium.
- Needs: N2, N3 · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: easy
- Example: Vanguard and Moneyfarm publish scope and price per level (advertised).

**5.3 "What This Means for Me" Cards**
Each of the three My Life options gets a one-screen card: what can go wrong, when you can get your money out, what it costs, who it usually suits.
- Needs: N3 · Impact: high · Feasibility: easy
- Example: in-app explanation cards named in Stage 1 trend research.

**5.4 Learn to Invest in Context**
Short Learn to Invest lessons (1–2 minutes) appear exactly at the step where the question comes up, for example "what is risk level 4?" next to the risk score.
- Needs: N3 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy
- Example: contextual tooltips in many apps.

**5.5 "You Call Us" Promise**
State clearly in the app, on letters and in branches: "We will not call you to sell investments. Here is how to reach us when you want." This sets the expectation and builds trust.
- Needs: N2 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy (needs a decision from sales management)
- Example: none found; based on C2 and reported HU research.

**5.6 Plain-Language MiFID Questionnaire**
Rewrite the MiFID questions in simple words, explain why each question is asked and what the result means for the customer.
- Needs: N1, N3 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy–medium (compliance sign-off)
- Example: Finax "review the contract calmly" step (advertised).

**5.7 Help Request Tracker**
After asking for help, the customer sees where the request is: "received", "with Anna from RaiConnect", "answer expected by 18:00 tomorrow".
- Needs: N4, N2 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: medium
- Example: delivery and taxi apps showing status and arrival time.

### 6. Trust and confidence solutions

**6.1 "AI Explains, Banker Recommends" Label**
Every AI answer carries a clear label: this is an AI, it explains but does not recommend, and here is a button to reach a person. This also meets the AI Act transparency duty from 02.08.2026.
- Needs: N5, N2 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy
- Example: ESMA statement of 30.05.2024 (clients must be told when they talk to a chatbot).

**6.2 Fair Comparison Commitment**
The comparer also shows options the bank earns little on, such as deposits and government bonds, and says openly when they may fit better. This is the opposite of a product push.
- Needs: N3, N5 · Impact: high · Feasibility: medium (internal resistance likely; business effect unknown)
- Example: C1 and the HU expert both point to cheaper or safer alternatives winning today.

**6.3 My Data Panel**
The customer sees and edits their life profile, sees who in the bank can see it, and can delete it at any time.
- Needs: N1, N2 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: medium (GDPR design)
- Example: privacy dashboards in large consumer apps.

**6.4 Meet Your Expert First**
Before a video session, the customer sees the expert's name, photo, languages and qualifications, and can pick the same person again next time.
- Needs: N2, N4 · Impact: low–medium · Feasibility: easy
- Example: telemedicine apps showing doctor profiles.

**6.5 First-Year Cost Promise**
If a customer leaves within the first months because costs were higher than shown, the bank refunds part of the fees. This makes the cost information a commitment.
- Needs: N3, N6 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: hard (business case and legal check unknown)
- Example: money-back guarantees in retail.

### 7. Cross-industry inspiration

**7.2 Guidance Scripts for Common Worries** — Guidance staff use tested, non-sales scripts for the most common worries (losses, needing money early, inflation). Every customer gets the same quality of answer. · N3, N4 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy · Example: nurse triage protocols.

**7.3 Ready-to-Invest Check** — A short honest self-check before investing (emergency savings? debts? when is the money needed?), which may say "a deposit may suit you better for now". · N1, N3 · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: easy–medium · Example: symptom checkers.

**B. Duolingo** — *principle: small steps, habit, learning in the flow.*

**7.4 Goal Learning Path** — Five short lessons linked to the customer's goal unlock as they go (for example "saving for a flat in 3 years"). · N3, N1 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy–medium · Example: Duolingo learning paths.

**7.5 Practice Mode** — A simulator with play money and past market data, so the customer can see what a 10% fall looks like in HUF before risking real money. · N3 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: medium (simulation rules and disclaimers) · Example: trading app demo accounts.

**7.6 Friendly Reminders You Set** — Reminders to save or review that the customer sets and can pause, in a friendly tone. · N2 · Impact: low–medium · Feasibility: easy · Example: Duolingo reminders.

**C. Uber / delivery apps** — *principle: visible status, choice and rating.*

**7.7 Live Wait Time for a Person** — Show how soon a person is available: "video expert in about 12 minutes" or "next branch slot tomorrow 17:00". · N4, N2 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: medium · Example: ride-hailing arrival times.

**7.8 Rate Your Guidance** — After each session, two questions: "was it clear?" and "did you feel pushed to buy?". Results feed the team's measures (see 3.6). · N1, N3 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy · Example: ride ratings.

**7.9 Choose Your Expert** — Pick an expert by language, topic or channel (video or branch). · N2 · Impact: low–medium · Feasibility: medium · Example: marketplace apps.

**D. Spotify / Netflix** — *principle: personal recap, profiles, "because you said…".*

**7.10 Your Money Year** — A yearly personal summary: what you saved, what you paid in costs, how your goals moved. It doubles as the invitation to the optional yearly check-in. · N1, N3 · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: medium · Example: Spotify Wrapped.

**7.11 "Because You Said…" Content** — Lessons and articles chosen from the life profile ("because you said you need the money in 2 years…"). Content only, never product advice. · N1, N3 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy–medium · Example: Netflix recommendations.

**7.12 Household Profiles** — With consent, family members can share goals or see a shared plan, each with their own profile. · N5 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: hard (consent and data model) · Example: Netflix profiles; Finax family accounts.

### 8. Unconventional / "crazy" ideas

**8.1 The Bank's Own "ChatGPT for Your Money"** — A full AI money companion that sees all your accounts across banks (with consent) and gets a licensed person to approve any recommendation within minutes. · N5, N4 · Impact: high · Feasibility: hard (MiFID, AI Act, open finance) · Example: none at this scale.

**8.2 Free Guidance, Flat Fee Investing** — All guidance is free; the bank earns only a flat, published yearly fee, so it has no reason to push one product. · N3, N1 · Impact: high · Feasibility: hard (business model change) · Example: flat-fee robo models.

**8.3 Life Events Bring Advice to You** — With explicit consent, the bank notices big life moments (a large inflow, a new home loan) and offers a guidance session, never a product. · N1, N2 · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: hard (consent, GDPR; an inflow is not always free money) · Example: DBS life-event nudges.

**8.4 Reverse Auction for Your Goal** — The customer describes a goal; options (including non-bank ones such as government bonds) are ranked openly by cost and fit. · N3, N5 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: hard · Example: price comparison marketplaces.

**8.5 Bank Guidance Inside ChatGPT** — Offer the bank's approved explanations inside the tools customers already use, such as a bank-built assistant in ChatGPT, with a link back to a person. · N5 · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: hard (data protection; control of a third-party platform) · Example: brands publishing GPT assistants.

**8.6 Family Money Evening** — Regular evening video sessions for families, where a bank expert answers questions from parents and their adult children together. · N5, N3 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: medium · Example: school parents' evenings.

**8.7 Clarity or Your Money Back** — If a customer says after a session that it wasn't clear, the session fee (or a month's fees) is refunded. · N3 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: hard · Example: satisfaction guarantees.

### 9. Quick wins (1–3 months)

Release cycles and approvals at the bank are not known, so "1–3 months" is a guess. This requires verification.

**9.1 Exit Script with One Real Offer** — Give staff and the closing screen a short script: cost comparison, one cheaper option if it exists, and a booking link. · N6 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy.

**9.2 No-Phone Test Group** — For a test group, replace phone campaigns with a letter or app invitation and compare responses. · N2 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy.

**9.3 Three Life Questions in the Booking Form** — Add "what is the money for, when do you need it, what is your question?" to the RaiConnect or branch booking form. · N1, N4 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy.

**9.4 Yearly Cost Line on the Three Options** — Add one line with the yearly cost in HUF for an example amount to each My Life option. · N3 · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: easy–medium.

**9.5 "Bring Someone" Tick Box** — Let customers tick "I'd like to bring a family member" when booking. · N5 · Impact: low–medium · Feasibility: easy.

**9.6 Learn to Invest Links in My Life** — Link existing lessons and podcast episodes at the matching steps of the investment journey. · N3 · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy.

**9.7 Printed One-Page Plan Template** — A simple paper template for branch conversations: goal, time, options discussed, costs, next step. · N1, N2 (C2) · Impact: medium · Feasibility: easy.

### 10. Long-term / strategic bets (12+ months)

**10.1 A Guidance Tier Between Mass and Premium** — A new service level with a shared expert team, clear scope and a fixed price or simple eligibility, for customers who are not yet Premium. · N1–N4 · Impact: high · Feasibility: hard · Example: Raiffeisen HU Premium vs Premium Plus; Moneyfarm tiers.

**10.2 Group-Wide Guidance Building Blocks** — Build the life profile, comparer and hand-over once and reuse them in Hungary, Serbia, Czech Republic, Romania and other markets. · N1, N3, N4 · Impact: high · Feasibility: hard (local rules and systems) · Example: Stage 1 idea "join RaiConnect, CZ automated advice, RS planning content, RO Premium Invest".

**10.3 Low-Cost Savings Plan Partnership** — Partner with a fintech for low-cost ETF savings plans under the RBI brand, so cost is no longer a reason to leave. · N3, N6 · Impact: high · Feasibility: hard · Example: ING Germany Smart Invest powered by Scalable Capital (advertised).

**10.4 Whole-Picture Guidance with Open Finance** — If FiDA is adopted, offer guidance that includes the customer's savings and investments at other providers, with consent. FiDA's legislative status requires verification. · N5 · Impact: medium–high · Feasibility: hard.
