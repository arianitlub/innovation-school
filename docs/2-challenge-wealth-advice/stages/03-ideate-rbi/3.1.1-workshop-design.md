# 3.1.1 Ideation Workshop Design — mass-affluent wealth advice

RBI AI Prompt Library prompt **3.1.1**, run 14.10.2026 with skill `innovation-ideate`. AI-assisted draft for the team to adapt.

> **Inputs:** [02-empathize.md](../02-empathize.md) (insights, hypotheses, HMW), [2.3.2 personas](../02-empathize-rbi/2.3.2-personas.md), [2.3.3 journey map](../02-empathize-rbi/2.3.3-journey-map.md), [2.3.4 HMW](../02-empathize-rbi/2.3.4-how-might-we.md), [guide.md](../../../1-innovation-school/guide.md).
>
> **Evidence limits:** our insights come from 2 internal experts, 2 street interviews (C1, C2) and one internal UX study. They are **provisional**. The capabilities named in insight 4 (My Life, RaiConnect, advisory tool, Learn to Invest) were described by the experts for Hungary or Serbia. We have not checked them for other markets. Insight 5 (young and older customers need different entry points) is a **team observation**. The ages of C1 and C2 were not recorded, so treat it as a hypothesis to test.

> **About the team idea:** the "Money Guide" (life profile + AI assistant + expert on request) is used below **only as a seed to challenge**, in a "Build on / Break it" round. It is not the answer, and this workshop is designed to produce alternatives to it.

### 3. Workshop agendas

Roles for both agendas:

- **Facilitator:** explains each round, keeps energy up, does not judge ideas.
- **Timekeeper:** uses a visible timer and gives a 1-minute warning.
- **Capture lead:** keeps the wall in order, photographs it and moves it into Miro after the session.
- **Coach:** contributes ideas and plays the "Break it" challenger in the seed round. The coach has a separately coloured vote (section 8).

The facilitator also writes ideas. With only 5–6 people, everyone generates.

### 4. Ideation techniques

#### D. "How Might We" questions (use first, it feeds every other round)

**How to frame a pain as an HMW:** take a pain point, keep the customer and the need, remove the solution, and start with "How might we…". It should not be too broad ("fix wealth advice") or too narrow ("add a chat button").

**Sub-HMWs for our challenge** (write each on a card and put it on the wall):

| # | Sub-HMW | From insight / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | HMW start guidance from the customer's **life and plans**, not from a product or the MiFID form? | Insight 1, C1 |
| H2 | HMW let the customer **start and control** the contact, in the **channel they trust** (app or branch)? | Insight 2, C1, C2 |
| H3 | HMW make options and **costs clear and comparable** at the moment of decision? | Insight 3, C1, UX |
| H4 | HMW turn the **moment a customer wants to leave** into a moment where we help? | Insight 3, C1 exit |
| H5 | HMW make **what already exists** (My Life, RaiConnect, advisory tool, Learn to Invest) reachable for people outside Premium? | Insight 4, experts |
| H6 | HMW give **young and older** customers (or digital and branch customers) different first steps? | Insight 5 (team hypothesis) |
| H7 | HMW make asking the bank feel **as easy as asking a nephew or ChatGPT**? | RH5, C1 |

**Facilitator tip:** if people keep jumping to solutions in the insight walk, write the solution on a yellow sticky, park it on the wall ("idea parking"), and ask: "Which customer problem does this solve?"

#### F. Reverse brainstorming

1. **Reverse question (4 min, in pairs):** "How might we make sure a mass-affluent customer **never** trusts our investment guidance and **leaves** as fast as possible?"
2. Write bad ideas quickly. Examples to get started:
   - Call them at dinner with a fund offer.
   - Ask only the MiFID questions, never about their plans.
   - Hide the fees until the first statement.
   - Only offer help Tuesday 10:00–12:00 at the adviser's convenience.
   - When they cancel, ask "why?" and then say nothing (this happened to C1).
   - Use jargon on every screen; make withdrawal rules hard to find.
3. **Flip (4 min):** turn each bad idea into a positive one.
   - "Hide fees" → *total yearly cost in euros, shown before you confirm, compared with a simple alternative*.
   - "Say nothing at exit" → *an exit conversation offering a cheaper or simpler option*.
   - "Adviser's hours" → *book a 15-minute call or branch slot yourself, at a time you choose*.
4. **Post (2 min)** the flipped ideas on the wall. Keep the bad ones too; they make good material for the pitch.

**Tip:** this is the best warm-up for a mixed, multi-country team. It is funny, safe, and draws on everyone's own experience as a bank customer.

#### G. Crazy 8s

1. Everyone folds an A4 sheet into **8 panels** (fold 3 times).
2. Choose **one sub-HMW** (H1–H7).
3. The timer runs **8 minutes**; the facilitator calls "next" every minute.
4. Sketch **one idea per panel**: a screen, a branch moment, a letter, a conversation. Stick figures are fine. Add a 3–5 word title.
5. 1 minute: **circle your best 2**, then post them (or take a photo for Miro).

**Examples for H2 (customer controls contact):** a "Talk to someone" button that shows your preferred channel; a branch QR code to book your own slot; a "Do not call me, I'll ask" setting; a monthly "ask me anything" branch hour; WhatsApp-style messages with a named expert.

**Tips:** say "This is about quantity, not drawing skill." Panels can repeat a theme with variations. If someone is stuck, suggest: "Draw the same idea for C2 in the branch."

#### E. Analogies and cross-industry inspiration ("What would X do?")

**How it works:** pairs draw 2 analogy cards. For each one, ask: *"How does X solve a similar problem? What would X do with our HMW?"* Then write ideas for **our** customers (2 × 3 min + 2 min to post).

**Analogy cards for our challenge:**

| X | Their similar problem | Possible lesson for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Family doctor / GP** | Triage, then refer to a specialist only when needed | A short "money check-up" for everyone; refer to an expert only for big decisions |
| **ChatGPT** | Ask anything, any time, in your own words | Let customers ask their own question first, not fill in a form first |
| **The nephew** (C1's adviser) | Trusted, free, knows your life | Let customers bring a family member into the conversation, or share a summary with them |
| **Telecom cancellation desk** | Keeps customers at the moment they leave | Exit offer: a cheaper plan, a pause, a simpler product |
| **Apple Genius Bar** | You book an expert when *you* need one | A self-booked expert slot in the branch or by video |
| **Pharmacist** | Free advice without an appointment, just walk in | Walk-in "money questions" corner in branches |
| **Duolingo** | Learning in small steps that feel like progress | A 5-minute path from "saver" to "first small investment" |
| **Booking.com / price comparison sites** | Clear comparison of options and total price | A side-by-side view of options, total cost and withdrawal rules |
| **Spotify Wrapped** | Personal yearly review people like to share | A yearly "your money year" review for non-Premium customers |
| **IKEA** | Do it yourself, with clear instructions and help when stuck | Self-service investing with a clear "I'm stuck" path to a human |

**Adapting the lessons:** for each analogy idea, ask: (1) What exactly makes it work? (2) Would that work for C1, for C2, or for both? (3) What would be different in a regulated bank (suitability, MiFID)? Note regulatory doubts on the idea card; don't drop the idea for them during the session.

#### Build on / Break it — the team seed (Money Guide)

This round makes sure the team's existing idea is **challenged, not assumed**.

1. **Show the seed (1 min):** "Money Guide = a life profile the customer fills in, an AI assistant to ask questions, and an expert on request." Present it as *one idea on the wall*, written on the same yellow sticky as all the others.
2. **Break it (3 min):** the coach or a chosen devil's advocate leads. Prompts:
   - Would **C2** ever use an AI assistant from a bank? (C2 distrusts online offers.)
   - Is it just **product push with a new face**, if the AI suggests the bank's own funds?
   - Where is the **cost** fix? C1 left over cost, and Money Guide does not change price.
   - **Regulation:** when does AI "guidance" become investment **advice** under MiFID? Who is responsible?
   - **Trust and errors:** what if the AI gets it wrong?
   - Does "expert on request" have enough **adviser capacity** behind it? (RH4: access, not tools.)
   - Do customers want to fill in **another profile**?
3. **Build on (3 min):** "Yes, and…" Prompts: make it work in the branch; make it work without AI; make the expert free for the first 15 minutes; use the life profile at the exit moment; reuse My Life or RaiConnect instead of building something new.
4. **Alternatives (3 min):** everyone writes **at least 2 ideas that are not the Money Guide** and that answer one of the "Break it" points.

**Capture:** "Break it" points on pink stickies (risks), "Build on" points on yellow (variants), alternatives on yellow. All of them go into clustering like any other idea.

#### A. Classic brainstorming (3-hour version)

- **How to facilitate:** stand at the wall. Read one sub-HMW aloud. People say an idea, write it on a sticky and post it themselves. 6 minutes per sub-HMW.
- **Rules to emphasise:** defer judgment, one idea per sticky, build on others ("Yes, and…"), wild ideas welcome, quantity first, be visual.
- **Prompts if energy drops:** "What would we do with no budget?" "What would we do with an unlimited budget?" "What if branches didn't exist?" "What if the app didn't exist?" "What if a 25-year-old and a 65-year-old used it?"
- **How to capture:** the capture lead numbers each sticky (H2-07) so it can be traced in Miro later.

#### B. Brainwriting 6-3-5, adapted to 5–6 people

**Steps:**

1. Each person gets an A3 sheet with the HMW at the top and a grid with 3 columns and 4 rows.
2. **Round 1 (5 min):** write 3 ideas in the first row, silently.
3. Pass the sheet to the left.
4. **Rounds 2–4 (5 min each):** read the sheet, then write 3 new ideas or **build on** the ones above (draw an arrow).
5. After 4 rounds: each sheet has 12 ideas, and 6 people give about **72 ideas**. Each person stars the 2 best ideas on the sheet they hold, then posts them.

**Template / worksheet:**

```text
HMW: ______________________________________________   (sub-HMW H_)
Persona in mind:  [ ] C1 Comparer  [ ] C2 Branch Saver  [ ] Learner

| Round | Idea 1          | Idea 2          | Idea 3          | Initials |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|----------|
| 1     |                 |                 |                 |          |
| 2     |                 |                 |                 |          |
| 3     |                 |                 |                 |          |
| 4     |                 |                 |                 |          |
★ = best 2 (mark at the end)
```

**Tip:** this is the best technique for quiet people and for people working in their second language. Allow one word in your own language with a translation.

#### C. SCAMPER on today's journey (3-hour version)

Apply the letters to the **current journey** from 2.3.3: Trigger → Search → Bank contact → Profile (MiFID) → Decide/invest → Get help → Follow-up → Exit or stay. Each pair takes 2 letters, 5 minutes per letter.

| Letter | Question | Examples for our journey |
|---|---|---|
| **S**ubstitute | What could we replace? | Replace "MiFID first" with "your plans first" (then MiFID). Replace outbound phone offers with a customer-booked slot. |
| **C**ombine | What could we join together? | Learn to Invest content on the decision screen; the life profile and the MiFID profile in one flow; branch + video in one appointment. |
| **A**dapt | What could we copy from elsewhere? | GP triage (check-up → specialist); telecom retention at exit; Premium's 2–3 reviews a year adapted as a light annual review. |
| **M**odify | What could we make bigger, smaller or clearer? | Show cost in euros per year, not %; one-page fund summary; a smaller first investment amount. |
| **P**ut to other uses | Where else could something we have be used? | The cancellation contact becomes a guidance moment; the advisory tool used in a short branch session outside Premium; RaiConnect for "15-minute questions". |
| **E**liminate | What could we remove? | Cold calls; jargon; steps where the customer must adapt to the adviser's schedule. |
| **R**everse | What could we turn around? | The customer invites the bank, not the bank the customer; the customer rates the guidance; the customer brings their own ChatGPT analysis and the bank checks it. |

### 5. Facilitation best practices

**Psychological safety**

- Say it clearly: "No idea is stupid today. Nobody's job title or home bank matters in this room." Arianit is a team lead at work but a peer here; he should speak last on his own ideas.
- The facilitator shares the first wild idea, to model it.
- Reverse brainstorming as the warm-up makes laughing at ideas safe.

**Quiet participants**

- Always **write silently first**, then share (Crazy 8s, brainwriting, dot voting).
- Go round in turns: "One idea each, pass is fine."
- Pair quiet people with each other, not with the loudest person.
- Simple English, with your own language allowed on stickies (and translated).

**Dominant voices**

- One idea per sticky, posted by its author. Talking is not needed to contribute.
- The timekeeper enforces 30-second explanations.
- Give the dominant person a role: capture lead or devil's advocate in "Break it".
- Coach tip: the coach can interrupt kindly with "Let's hear someone who hasn't spoken yet."

**Keeping energy high**

- Stand during clustering and brainstorming.
- Short rounds with a visible timer; change the format every 8–12 minutes.
- Count ideas out loud ("We're at 45!").
- Stretch break at minute 58 (90-min version) or a real coffee break (3-hour version).
- We are on day 2 of Budapest and there is a boat cruise in the evening. Keep it fun and finish on time.

**Preventing early judgment**

- "Idea parking" for solutions during the insight walk.
- Ban the words "but", "we already tried" and "compliance won't allow" until Part 3. Write those concerns on a pink sticky instead.
- The "Break it" round gives judgment its own place and time.

**Capturing all ideas**

- One idea per sticky, thick marker, readable from 2 metres away, with the author's initials and the sub-HMW number.
- The capture lead photographs the wall **after each round**.
- Sketches from Crazy 8s get a title written on them.

### 8. Selection and prioritisation

**Step 1 — Dot voting**

- **Dots:** 3 per team member (about 15–18 dots), plus 1 "bold bet" dot each in the 3-hour version. The coach gets 3 dots in **another colour**; they are shown but counted separately.
- **Criterion (read aloud before voting):** "Which ideas would most help a customer like C1 *or* C2 get clear, affordable guidance that starts from *their* situation, when *they* ask?"
- **Rules:** vote silently and at the same time; max 2 dots on one idea; you may vote for your own idea; clusters can be voted on as a whole.
- **Tip:** the facilitator also votes, not first and not last.

**Step 2 — Impact vs. effort matrix**

Draw a 2×2. Vertical axis: **customer impact** (from the insights). Horizontal axis: **effort** for the bank (build, process change, regulation, adviser time).

```text
             HIGH IMPACT
                 |
   Quick wins    |   Bigger bets
  (do / test     |  (prototype,
   first)        |   build case)
 ----------------+----------------  EFFORT →
   Small fixes   |   Avoid / park
  (add later)    |
                 |
             LOW IMPACT
```

Place only the top 5–6 voted ideas. **Mark the placements as team guesses**; real effort needs input from the experts. Ideas that reuse existing capability (insight 4) often land on the left, but check this per market.

**Step 3 — Desirability / Feasibility / Viability quick score (3-hour version, or in 3.3.1)**

Score 1–5 per idea, out loud, as a team, and write down which scores are guesses:

- **Desirable:** do C1 *and* C2 (or the Learner) want it? Does it keep the customer in control?
- **Feasible:** can RBI do it with what exists today (My Life, RaiConnect, advisory tool, branch staff)? Any regulatory blocker?
- **Viable:** is it affordable for the customer and sustainable for the bank (adviser time, cost to serve)?

**Step 4 — Group discussion protocol**

1. A person who did **not** write the idea gives a 1-minute pitch.
2. 2 minutes of "I like… / I wish… / What if…".
3. Guardrail check (no cold calls, not digital-only, not "invest more", not education-only).
4. Decide: **shortlist**, **combine** with another idea, or **park**. If the team is split, the dots decide, and the coach's view is heard but does not decide.
5. Aim for a mix: at least **1 quick win** and **1 bigger bet**, and at least one idea that works in the **branch**.
