## Output

> **Data limits.** This analysis uses only T1 ([prototype-feedback-T1.md](../../evidence/prototype-feedback-T1.md)): two feedback statements and "the others are okay", reported by Arianit on 15.10.2026. The number of participants, their profile, the tasks and the setup were not recorded. No metrics were tracked. Nothing below is invented; where the prompt asks for something the data cannot answer, the answer is "not known".

### Executive summary

- T1 gave **two concrete requests**: add notifications (investments up / down, clickable) and make Wealth Insights clearer and lighter ("what exactly is good to invest and why… not a lot of infos").
- Both were **addressed on 15.10.2026** (bell with 2 notifications and detail screens; Insights rewritten to 4 picks with amount, one "why", risk meter and cost). The changes have **not yet been tested**.
- "The others are okay" is a weak positive signal for the rest of the prototype, not proof.
- **Hypotheses: all inconclusive** (H4 and H7 have a first signal of interest only).
- **Decision: ITERATE.** Run round 2 with 5–8 screened participants, including at least 2 C1-type and 2 C2-type, using the 4.1.1 plan.

### 3. Pattern analysis

- **Frequency:** not known. Each issue came from the T1 feedback; we cannot say how many people raised it.
- **Intensity:** not recorded. The wording ("make it fancy but not a lot of infos") suggests a preference, not frustration, but this is interpretation.
- **Most problems:** Wealth Insights.
- **Worked well:** "the others are okay": Home, My Wealth Snapshot, Learn, Playground, AI, Goals, Adviser were not criticised. This does not show that they were used or understood.

### 4. Root cause analysis

| Issue | Root cause | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Insights unclear | Too many elements and too much text for one decision; no single "why" per idea | **Copy / communication** and **information overload** (UX/design) |
| Insights: "what exactly is good to invest" | Possibly **conceptual**: the user expects a clear recommendation, while the concept deliberately gives ideas, not advice. Not confirmed | Conceptual (hypothesis, to test) |
| Notifications missing | The prototype had no notification feature; concept nudges were only shown as one card inside My Wealth | **Missing functionality** |

No technical performance issues were reported.

### 5. User sentiment

- **% enthusiastic / sceptical / neutral:** not known (participant number not recorded; no percentages can be given).
- **Overall tone (interpretation):** constructive and broadly positive ("the others are okay"), with two improvement requests.
- **Emotional journey:** confident, confused, frustrated, delighted moments were **not recorded**. The only confusion signal is Wealth Insights.

### 7. Unexpected insights

- The participant asked for **more direction** ("tell what exactly is good to invest and why"), not more education. This may conflict with our guardrail "AI explains, banker recommends". It is a design tension to test, not yet a finding.
- **Performance notifications** were requested by the user, not suggested by us. This fits the "ongoing nudges" part of the concept and the gap "no review for non-Premium" from the journey map.
- No new pain points were reported.

### 8. Segment differences

Not possible to analyse: participant profile, age and digital skill were not recorded. We do not know whether T1 is C1-type, C2-type or neither. **Implication:** screen and record type (C1 / C2 / UX) for every round-2 participant.

### 9. Prioritised changes for the next iteration

Where to go back to in Design Thinking: **stay in Prototype & Test**; no return to Ideate is justified by T1.

**Already changed (15.10.2026), now to verify in round 2:**
1. Notification bell, 2 notifications, 2 detail screens.
2. Wealth Insights rewrite: one headline, one allocation bar, 4 picks with amount, one "why", risk meter, cost.

**What to keep as-is:**
- Screens not criticised in T1 ("the others are okay"), until round 2 shows otherwise.
- The light, 4-pick structure of the new Insights and the calm tone of the notification details.

**What to add (from 4.1.1 and the 4.3.1 review, not from T1):**
- Unlock screen and Life Profile questions (H1, H2).
- Clear "ideas, not advice" framing of picks and "keep as it is" as a valid choice (H4).
- Notification preferences (H7, fix 7).
- Branch path mock-up (H6).
- Fix demo-data contradictions found in the 4.3.1 review.

### 11. GO / NO-GO decision

- **Decision: ITERATE.**
- **Confidence:** low, because the evidence is very thin.
- **Supporting evidence:** no critical issue; both reported issues were fixable within a day; the rest was "okay".
- **Concerns:** unknown participant profile; no tasks or metrics; the two fixes are untested; H1, H2, H5, H6 have no evidence at all; the wish for "what exactly is good to invest" may push the product toward regulated advice.

### 12. Risks & open questions

- **Uncertain:** whether T1 represents our target group; whether the new Insights is clear *and* still read as ideas; whether notifications calm or worry people.
- **Needs more testing:** all seven hypotheses, especially H4 (trust vs push) and H6 (branch path).
- **Assumptions still made:** customers want a My Wealth section; the unlock does not feel like sales; customers will share Life Profile data; the bank can legally show "picks" outside the advisory process (This requires verification with Compliance).
- **What could go wrong:** Insights read as a personal recommendation (MiFID risk, product-push perception); notifications on falls trigger panic selling; the branch-trusting segment is left out.

### 13. Next steps

- **Functional MVP:** no, not yet.
- **Pivot:** no evidence for a pivot.
- **Additional research:** Compliance check on Insights and AI wording.

## What this means for round 2

- T1 is a **first signal only**. We cannot report any hypothesis as validated in the pitch on 04.11.2026 without round 2.
- The two T1 fixes (notifications, simpler Insights) are in the prototype but **untested**; include a task for each in round 2 (down-notification task; "What would you do with the €120,000 deposit, and why?").
- Watch the **tension** T1 revealed: people may want "what exactly to invest in", while My Wealth gives ideas, not advice. Ask participants how they read the picks, and ask Compliance early.
- **Record everything** this time: participant ID and type (C1 / C2 / UX), tasks, ratings and quotes, so 4.3.3 can be re-run with real patterns.
- Recruit **at least 2 C1-type and 2 C2-type** participants (5–8 in total) following the 4.1.1 plan.
