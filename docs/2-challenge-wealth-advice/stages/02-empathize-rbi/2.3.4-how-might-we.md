## Filled prompt

```text
Based on everything we've discussed in this Synthesis phase so far (patterns, personas, journey map, key insights), identify the 1-3 user insights or problems that most deserve a "How Might We" question. Combine them only if they are closely related and a single question can hold them without becoming vague.

If these insights aren't yet clear enough to anchor a precise question, ask me ONE clarifying question before proceeding - do not guess.

Once clear, create the question:

1. HOW MIGHT WE QUESTION

   - If combining insights, make sure they share the same underlying need - do not force unrelated problems into one question
   - Make it specific enough to provide direction, but broad enough to allow multiple solutions

OUTPUT FORMAT:
One concise question starting with "How might we…"

```

## Insights selected

1. **Guidance outside Premium feels like a product push, not built on my life situation** (experts + C1).
2. **Customers want to control when and how they get help**; outbound phone is rejected and the channel differs per person (C1, C2, HU research).
3. **Information is there but not clear, and cost is decisive**, so customers decide elsewhere: family, ChatGPT, cheaper providers (C1, C2, UX).

These share one underlying need: **guidance that fits *my* situation, comes when *I* want it, and is clear enough to act on.** So one question can hold them.

## Final HMW question

> **How might we give mass-affluent customers outside full Premium advice clear, affordable guidance that starts from their own situation and is there when they choose to ask for it, so they can make confident investment decisions with their bank?**

### What changed from the expert-only version (earlier on 14.10.2026)

| Earlier | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "the right level of guidance at the right moment" | "**starts from their own situation**" + "**when they choose to ask**" | C1: product push, not personal; C2 and HU research: customer-initiated contact only |
| (no cost) | "**clear, affordable**" | C1 left because of cost and unclear information; matches our working goal |
| "confident, informed decisions" | "… **with their bank**" | Customers currently decide with family or ChatGPT |

### Alternatives for the ideation session

1. How might we make the bank's guidance feel as personal and useful as asking a trusted relative, or ChatGPT?
2. How might we let customers start a conversation about their money on their terms, in the channel they trust?
3. How might we turn the moment a customer wants to leave into a moment where we help?

Solution-neutral: no app, chatbot, Premium tier or price model is chosen. Those belong in **Ideate**.
