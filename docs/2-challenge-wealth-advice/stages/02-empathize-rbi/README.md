## Synthesis in 60 seconds

1. **Capability exists** (digital investing, MiFID profiling, RaiConnect, advisory tool, education). The gap is not technology.
2. Outside Premium, bank guidance feels like a **product push**: not built on life situation, not clear (C1, experts).
3. **Customers want to control the contact.** Phone offers are rejected; the channel differs per person: app (C1), branch (C2).
4. **Cost decides.** C1 cancelled the bank investment account because of cost; the bank asked why but offered nothing.
5. **Customers decide elsewhere**: family, other people, ChatGPT.

## Hypothesis check

| Hypothesis | Status |

| H2 Knowledge/confidence | 🟡 **Partially supported, reframed**: clarity, trust and cost matter more than knowledge itself |
| H3 Hybrid digital + human | 🟡 **Partially supported, refined**: control over contact; channel differs (C2 = branch) |
| H4 Adviser data/tools | 🔴 **Contradicted as written**: tools exist; adviser availability and access are the limits |

## Final HMW

> **How might we give mass-affluent customers outside full Premium advice clear, affordable guidance that starts from their own situation and is there when they choose to ask for it, so they can make confident investment decisions with their bank?**
