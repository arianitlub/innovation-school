RBI AI Prompt Library prompt 2.2.1, run 13.10.2026 with skill innovation-empathize. AI-assisted draft for the team; the team's own plan is in [../02-empathize.md](../02-empathize.md). Interviews must be with real people.

## Filled prompt

```text
I will be conducting interviews about: "How are mass affluent clients served with wealth management advice and where are the gaps? How can we find more ways to offer more value to customers during the process? Working goal: How can we give mass-affluent customers useful, affordable financial guidance that helps them achieve their goals and strengthens their relationship with the bank? Company: Raiffeisen Bank International (RBI). In Budapest 13.–16.10.2026 we run about 10 street interviews (5–7 min, passers-by, screening for 'in segment') and 1 internal investment-advice expert interview (30 min), maybe 2–3 bankers. We test H1 personalisation (7/10), H2 knowledge/confidence (6/10), H3 hybrid digital + human (7/10), H4 advisor data/tools/time (most advisors). Notes must be anonymous: no names, no amounts, no account details. Results go into a tracker: ID, Type, In segment?, H1, H2, H3, H4 (Y/N/?), Best quote."

I need a structured template for capturing notes efficiently during and after interviews.

Create a comprehensive note-taking template:

1. INTERVIEW HEADER SECTION
   What metadata to capture:
   - Participant ID (anonymized)
   - Date and time
   - Interview duration
   - Interviewer name
   - Note-taker name (if different)
   - Interview format (in-person, video, phone)

2. PARTICIPANT PROFILE SECTION
   Quick snapshot to capture relevant to my challenge:
   [Suggest relevant profile fields based on my challenge, such as:]
   - Demographics (if relevant: age range, location)
   - Relationship to the challenge (role, tenure, experience level)
   - Usage patterns (frequency, context, method)
   - Experience or proficiency level
   - Initial satisfaction/sentiment level (scale 1-10)

3. KEY QUOTES SECTION
   - Format for capturing powerful verbatim quotes
   - How to mark high-impact statements
   - Tip: Use quotation marks for exact words, brackets for context

4. PAIN POINTS & FRUSTRATIONS SECTION
   Structure for documenting:
   - Specific pain point description
   - Context/situation when it occurs
   - Frequency (always, often, sometimes, rarely)
   - Emotional intensity (1-10 scale)
   - Current workaround (if any)
   - Impact on their behavior (e.g., "makes them call support", "makes them consider switching")

5. NEEDS & DESIRES SECTION
   What they want or need:
   - Explicit needs (things they directly state)
   - Implicit needs (things you infer from their stories)
   - Job to be done (what are they trying to accomplish?)
   - Success criteria (what would good look like to them?)

6. BEHAVIORS & PATTERNS SECTION
   Observable behaviors to note:
   - How they currently solve the problem
   - When and where they use our digital services/channels
   - Their typical workflow or process
   - What triggers their actions
   - Where they get stuck or give up

7. EMOTIONAL RESPONSES SECTION
   Capture emotions:
   - What makes them frustrated/angry
   - What makes them anxious/worried
   - What makes them satisfied/happy
   - What makes them confused
   - Use emotion words or intensity scales

8. HYPOTHESIS VALIDATION SECTION
   For each hypothesis tested:
   - Hypothesis statement
   - Evidence supporting (specific examples from interview)
   - Evidence contradicting
   - Confidence level (validated / unclear / contradicted)
   - New insights that emerged

9. COMPETITIVE/COMPARATIVE INSIGHTS
   What they say about:
   - Other companies they've used
   - Fintech apps they prefer
   - Experiences from other contexts they reference
   - What they wish your company would copy

10. SURPRISES & UNEXPECTED INSIGHTS
    - What you didn't expect to hear
    - Patterns that contradict assumptions
    - New problem areas not previously considered
    - "Aha moments" during the interview

11. OBSERVER NOTES
    - Non-verbal cues (hesitation, excitement, confusion)
    - Things they struggled to articulate
    - Context that might be relevant
    - Interview dynamics (rapport, openness, guardedness)

12. IMMEDIATE POST-INTERVIEW REFLECTION
    To complete within 15 minutes after interview:
    - Top 3 takeaways from this interview
    - Most powerful quote
    - How this person is similar/different from previous interviews
    - New questions to ask in future interviews
    - Confidence in data quality (how open/honest were they?)

13. FOLLOW-UP ITEMS
    - Areas to probe deeper in future interviews
    - Things to verify with data
    - Requests for clarification or follow-up

Provide the template in a format that can be:
- Used in real-time during interview (streamlined)
- Expanded immediately after (detailed version)
- Easily transferred to Excel or database for analysis

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Two versions of the template:
1. Streamlined version for live note-taking (key fields only)
2. Detailed version for post-interview expansion (all fields)

Include tips for efficient note-taking during interviews.
```

## Output

**How the templates fit together**

| Template | Used when | Time | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1. Street live sheet** (one page) | During each 5–7 min street interview | Live | Tracker row: ID, Type, In segment?, H1–H3, Best quote |
| **T2. Detailed post-interview sheet** | Right after each interview (street or banker), within 15 min | 5–10 min street, 15 min banker | Tracker + extra columns (themes, journey stage, quality) |
| **T3. Expert interview sheet** | During and after the 30 min expert interview (also usable for bankers) | Live + 15 min | Tracker row E1 / B1… with H4, plus segment facts |
| **T4. Tracker / Excel export** | End of each round | 5 min per interview | [../02-empathize.md](../02-empathize.md) section 6 and the 1.3.2 formulas |

**Coding rule (same everywhere):** **Y** = the person's own story supports the hypothesis. **N** = it contradicts it. **?** = unclear, not asked, or not enough time. "?" does **not** count as support. Only **In segment = Y** rows count for H1–H3.

### Tips for efficient note-taking

1. **Two people, two jobs.** The lead asks and keeps eye contact; the note-taker writes. The lead should not write during street interviews.
2. **Exact words for quotes, short words for the rest.** Put quotes in "…", our context in [ ]. One great quote is worth more than a summary.
3. **Use symbols:** `!` high-impact, `?` check meaning, `→` they did next, `H1+` / `H1-` evidence for / against, `~` hesitation.
4. **Do not code while they watch.** Mark Y/N/? together straight after, in the 3–5 min buffer. Writing "Y" in front of someone can feel like a test.
5. **Write "not asked"** when a block was skipped. It becomes "?" and keeps the scoring honest.
6. **Separate what they said from what we think.** Mark our ideas "I:" (interpretation).
7. **No personal data on any sheet.** If they mention a name, employer or amount, write [name], [employer], [amount].
8. **Translate after, not during.** If the interview is in Hungarian, write key quotes in Hungarian first, then add the English translation in the post-interview sheet, marked "translated by …".
9. **Type up the same day** into the shared tracker in RBI-approved storage; destroy paper sheets afterwards.
10. **15-minute rule.** Complete T2 / T3 within 15 minutes. Memory fades fast, especially after several short interviews in a row.

## What this means for our interviews

- **Print or copy T1 once per street interview** (about 12) and T3 for the expert; bring spare copies for bankers.
- **Fill the H1–H3 boxes together right after each interview**, using the coding rule: Y only from the person's own story, "?" when unclear or not asked, and only "in segment = Y" rows count.
- **Use the T4 column names and allowed values exactly**, so the tracker in [../02-empathize.md](../02-empathize.md) section 6 and the 1.3.2 formulas work without cleaning.
- **Expert gets H4 only in the tracker**; their views on H1–H3 go in the notes as expert opinion, not customer evidence.
- **Never write names, amounts or account details**; keep quotes in the original language plus a translation.
