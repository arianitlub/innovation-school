## Filled prompt

```text
I need to create a pitch deck to present my innovation project to the RBI Innovation School coaches / innovation jury and RBI / Raiffeisen Bank Hungary (RBHU) business stakeholders (assumption; confirm with the coach).

CONTEXT:
- Presentation time: 10 minutes + 5 minutes Q&A (assumption; confirm with the coach)
- Audience background: Innovation coaches and jury (care about Design Thinking rigour and customer evidence) and banking business stakeholders from retail / segment management, digital and possibly Compliance (care about customer value, feasibility in existing systems, regulation, return)
- What they care about: a real customer problem backed by evidence, a clear solution, honest validation, regulatory safety (MiFID II, AI Act, GDPR), reuse of existing assets, a credible path to value
- Decision we need: support and a business sponsor at RBHU to run a 6-month pilot / wider customer test in Hungary (NOT full budget approval; the evidence is too early)

MY SOLUTION & RESULTS:
- Solution: My Wealth, a section in the existing Hungarian mobile app (My Life), unlocked at the mass-affluent threshold for customers outside full Premium: Snapshot of all money with the bank (incl. what it earns and costs), Life Profile, Learn, Investment Playground, optional AI Wealth Assistant that explains (and says it is AI), Wealth Insights (ideas, not advice), Talk to an Expert with the customer's context passed on, opt-in notifications and nudges, branch path with tablet and printed plan. Reuses My Life, RaiConnect, the advisory tool and Learn to Invest.
- Customer validation: 2 street interviews in Budapest (C1, C2) + 2 internal experts (HU, RS) + internal UX education study (8 Serbian interviews + 4-country synthesis, secondary) + 1 prototype test round (T1, 1 customer: asked for notifications and simpler Wealth Insights, both fixed the same day; "the others are okay"; decision ITERATE). Nothing validated yet.
- Expected impact: customers reach a confident, suitable decision (keeping cash can be right); more relationships kept; bankers start with context. Illustrative full-year benefit €15k / €180k / €1.06m (conservative / expected / optimistic); 3-year ROI −98% / −67% / +136%; break-even of a full build needs about €20,500 kept or added per engaged customer (expected case).
- Investment needed: pilot about €150k–€300k (expected €200k), illustrative; full build €0.5m–€1.0m only after the pilot.
```

### 1. Slide-by-slide

#### SLIDE 1 — Title (MAIN)
- **Title:** My Wealth
- **Headline:** Clear guidance on your terms, for customers between self-service and Premium.
- **Bullets:** Team [names] · RBI Innovation School 2026 · Mass-affluent wealth advice challenge · 04.11.2026
- **Visual:** the My Wealth landing hero (prototype landing page) in RBI yellow, phone mock-up with the Snapshot donut.
- **Speaker notes:** "Imagine you have saved €30,000. Your bank knows it. But when you want to decide what to do with it, you ask your nephew and ChatGPT. That is today. We want to change who you ask." → next: why that happens.

#### SLIDE 2 — The problem (MAIN)
- **Headline:** Outside Premium, our guidance feels like a product push, so customers decide elsewhere.
- **Bullets:**
  - Mass-affluent customers in Hungary are served non-advisory outside Premium (internal expert, HU)
  - Guidance feels product-led, unclear and costly, and on the bank's terms (phone offers, adviser's schedule)
  - Customers decide with family, ChatGPT, cheaper providers or government bonds
  - One of our two street interviewees **closed the bank's investment account**: "it costs a lot more". The bank asked why, and offered nothing
- **Visual:** simple journey strip "need → bank offer (push) → nephew / ChatGPT / broker → bank out", with the exit moment in red.
- **Speaker notes:** Open with C1's story; say clearly it is one person, but experts describe the same pattern. Don't quote numbers here. → "How big could this be?"

#### SLIDE 3 — The opportunity (MAIN, includes competitive context)
- **Headline:** Large savings, low-yield deposits and a gap below Premium that nobody fills well.
- **Bullets:**
  - Hungarian households: HUF 16,400bn in funds, HUF 13,700bn in retail government bonds; about half of term deposits earn under 1% (MNB Savings Report 2026)
  - Premium starts at HUF 400k–700k monthly income or HUF 10m savings (RBHU, OTP; to verify). Just below it: standard service
  - Competitors: OTP planner works only in branch; Erste webinars; fintechs and Moneyfarm sell human help below the threshold
  - Size of our segment: **unknown, needs RBHU data** (we model 10k / 25k / 50k)
- **Visual:** a "help ladder": Self-service app → **gap** → Premium → Private, with competitor logos placed on it.
- **Speaker notes:** Market numbers are public MNB data; segment size is a placeholder. → "So we went and asked people."

#### SLIDE 4 — Customer insights (MAIN)
- **Headline:** We talked to 2 customers and 2 experts: guidance is product-led, not life-led, and the customer must control contact.
- **Bullets:**
  - C1: "It was not too personalised… I felt it was more of a product push."
  - C1: "I had to adjust my life around the availability of the adviser."
  - C2: "If it is by telephone, then no."
  - Needs we heard: start from my life · clear and affordable · help when I ask, in my channel
- **Visual:** two persona cards, Self-Directed Comparer (C1) and Cautious Branch Saver (C2), quote on each; small label "n=2, first signal".
- **Speaker notes:** Let the quotes land; pause after each. Mention the UX study (8 interviews, Serbia, secondary) pointed the same way on clarity and simple explanations. Be explicit: 2 customers is a first signal. → "Here is what we built."

#### SLIDE 5 — Customer needs (BACKUP)
- **Headline:** Three needs: life-led, clear and affordable, on my terms.
- **Bullets:** N1 ask about my life and plans (C1) · N2 show what it costs and what I get (C1, UX) · N3 help when I ask, in the channel I trust (C1 app, C2 branch) · Job to be done: "Help me make a decision about my savings I feel confident about."
- **Visual:** empathy-map style 3 columns.
- **Speaker notes:** Use only if asked "what do customers need exactly?"

#### SLIDE 6 — Our solution (MAIN)
- **Headline:** My Wealth: a section in the app you already use, unlocked when your savings grow.
- **Bullets:**
  - **Snapshot:** all your money with the bank, what it earns and what it costs per year
  - **Life Profile + Learn + Playground:** start from your goals; learn in 2–3 minutes; try with virtual money
  - **AI Wealth Assistant (optional):** explains, says it is AI; recommendations stay with a banker (MiFID II)
  - **Talk to an Expert:** call, RaiConnect video or branch; the banker gets your profile, you don't start from zero
  - **Opt-in notifications and nudges; branch path** for those who don't want the app
- **Visual:** flow diagram: Unlock → Life Profile → Learn / Playground → AI (optional) → Expert → decision; branch path below. Tag existing assets reused (My Life, RaiConnect, advisory tool, Learn to Invest).
- **Speaker notes:** Emphasise reuse: we are joining what the bank already has. → "Let me show you."

#### SLIDE 7 — How it works (MAIN)
- **Headline:** From "what do I have?" to "I know what to do": in four screens.
- **Bullets (scenario, illustrative customer "Alex", fictional demo data):**
  1. Alex opens the app: **Snapshot donut** shows how much is invested vs not, and the yearly cost
  2. A **notification**: "Your equity fund is down this week": opens a calm explanation, not a sale
  3. **Wealth Insights:** 4 ideas, each with one "why", risk meter and cost ("keep as is" option to be added before round 2)
  4. Unsure → **Talk to an Expert**: the banker sees Alex's profile and what Alex explored
- **Visual:** real prototype screenshots, 4 phones side by side: Snapshot donut, notifications, Wealth Insights picks, Talk to an Expert. Or a 60-second live click-through.
- **Speaker notes:** If demoing live, have screenshots as fallback. Point out "keep as is" and the AI label. Avoid reading "Top idea / Best fit for you" aloud; replace that wording before the pitch. → "Did anyone try it?"

#### SLIDE 8 — Customer validation (MAIN)
- **Headline:** Early, honest evidence: a first signal, not proof.
- **Bullets:**
  - Evidence: **2 street interviews (Budapest) + 2 internal experts (HU, RS) + internal UX study (secondary) + 1 prototype test**
  - Test T1 (1 customer): asked for **notifications** on investments up or down and **simpler Wealth Insights**; both fixed the same day; "the others are okay"
  - Decision: **ITERATE**. Round 2 with 5–8 people (C1- and C2-types) 16.10.–26.10.2026 [update with results]
- **Visual:** evidence ladder (interviews → experts → UX study → prototype test), each with its real n; before/after of Wealth Insights.
- **Speaker notes:** Say it plainly: "We are not claiming validation. We are claiming a problem worth testing at scale." This builds credibility with a jury. → "Why would it matter to customers and to us?"

#### SLIDE 9 — Benefits: customers and business (MAIN, merges 9 + 10)
- **Headline:** A confident decision for the customer; a kept relationship for the bank.
- **Bullets:**
  - Customer: understands options and costs; help on their schedule; no cold calls; keeping cash can be the right answer
  - Bank: assets kept or added, fewer silent exits, a pipeline into Premium
  - Bankers: start with context, less data collection
- **Visual:** before / after: "Before: product push, phone call, nephew + ChatGPT" → "After: my profile, clear costs, expert when I ask".
- **Speaker notes:** Lead with the customer outcome; AUM is a result, not the goal. → "What could it be worth?"

#### SLIDE 10 — Benefits: business (merged into 9; detail BACKUP)
- **Headline:** Three benefit lines: assets kept or added, leavers kept, banker time saved.
- **Bullets:** B1 expected €150k, B2 €18.75k, B3 €11.25k a year (illustrative, formulas in backup).
- **Visual:** waterfall B1 + B2 + B3 per scenario.

#### SLIDE 11 — Financial case (MAIN)
- **Headline:** The case depends on three numbers we don't have yet; the pilot measures them.
- **Bullets:**
  - Illustrative scenarios (not forecasts; no RBI data): full-year benefit **€15k / €180k / €1.06m**
  - 3-year ROI incl. pilot + build: **−98% / −67% / +136%**; payback none / >3 years / ~20 months
  - Break-even (expected case): about **€20,500 kept or added per engaged customer**
  - Pilot investment: **€150k–€300k** (illustrative, to confirm with RBHU)
- **Visual:** tornado chart of the 3 drivers (segment size, assets per engaged customer, net margin) + small 3-scenario table labelled "ILLUSTRATIVE".
- **Speaker notes:** "We could show you a nice ROI. We won't, because we don't have the data. What we can show is what must be true." → "So here is how we find out."

#### SLIDE 12 — Implementation plan and success metrics (MAIN, merges 12 + 13)
- **Headline:** Pilot first: 6 months, 1,000–2,000 customers, a control group, a clear gate.
- **Bullets:**
  - Nov–Dec 2026: round 2–3 tests, Compliance view, RBHU data
  - Months 1–3: pilot section (manual hand-over), branch tablet version
  - Months 4–6: run with control group → go / iterate / stop
  - KPIs: ≥70% "I understand my options and costs", ≤20% "felt like sales", Life Profile completion, closures vs control, assets per engaged customer
- **Visual:** timeline with a gate diamond at month 6; KPI scorecard beside it.
- **Speaker notes:** The gate is the point: you risk a pilot, not a programme. → "What could go wrong?"

#### SLIDE 13 — Success metrics (merged into 12; full table BACKUP)
- See business case §11.

#### SLIDE 14 — Risks & mitigation (MAIN)
- **Headline:** We know where the line is: AI explains, the banker recommends.
- **Bullets:**
  - MiFID II advice line → "ideas, not advice" wording, "keep as is", Compliance review before pilot
  - AI Act (from 02.08.2026) and GDPR → AI discloses itself; consent for the Life Profile
  - Adviser capacity → capped invitations, wait time shown
  - Cost not solved → we show costs clearly; pricing (light Premium) is a pilot question
  - Digital distrust (C2) → branch path, opt-in only, no phone offers
- **Visual:** 2×2 risk matrix (likelihood × impact), MiFID line and capacity top-right.
- **Speaker notes:** Name "cost not solved" ourselves before they do. → "How does this compare with others?" (or straight to the ask if short on time).

#### SLIDE 15 — Competitive advantage (merged into 3; detail BACKUP)
- **Headline:** Life-led guidance with a hand-over, inside an app customers already use.
- **Bullets:** OTP planner is branch-only; Erste has webinars; fintechs are cheap but product-only; Moneyfarm sells human help below the threshold. Our angle: Life Profile + clear costs + context hand-over across app, video and branch.
- **Visual:** positioning map: x = digital ↔ human, y = product-led ↔ life-led.

#### SLIDE 16 — The ask (MAIN)
- **Headline:** We ask for a sponsor and a pilot, not a budget for a build.
- **Bullets:**
  - **A business sponsor** at RBHU (segment / digital)
  - **Access** to RBHU data to replace our assumptions, and to the My Life and RaiConnect teams
  - **An early Compliance review** of Insights and AI wording
  - **Approval to scope a 6-month pilot** (illustrative €150k–€300k, to be estimated with RBHU)
- **Visual:** four bold boxes, one per ask.
- **Speaker notes:** Pause; look at the decision-maker. "Who in this room could sponsor this?"

#### SLIDE 17 — Closing (MAIN)
- **Headline:** Let's be the bank they ask first.
- **Bullets:** Product push → life-led guidance → confident decisions and kept relationships. Now, because savings are moving (MNB 2026) and customers already ask ChatGPT.
- **Visual:** C1's quote "If it was more personalised for me and if it would be cheaper", then the My Wealth logo.
- **Speaker notes:** "Today our customers ask their nephew and ChatGPT. Give us six months to find out if they would ask us." Stop.

### 3. Design principles
- One message per slide; the headline is the message.
- RBI yellow and black, as in the prototype; big type (≥24 pt bullets).
- Every number slide carries an "Illustrative: assumptions, not RBI data" label.
- Real prototype screenshots, not stock images. Quotes in large type with the ID (C1, C2).
- Charts: tornado for drivers, simple table for scenarios; no 3-D, no fake precision.

### 4. Speaker notes
Included per slide above. General: one speaker per arc (3 speakers), hand-over at slides 4, 8 and 14.

### 5. Q&A preparation

| # | Question | Suggested answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What if customers don't adopt it? | That is the pilot's first KPI. Our model already runs at 10–30% engagement. If activation is low, we stop at the gate after 6 months, at pilot cost only. |
| 2 | Why not buy an existing solution? | Most of what we need exists in-house: My Life, RaiConnect, advisory tool, Learn to Invest. The gap is joining them. Buy vs build is a check after the pilot (e.g. planning engine). |
| 3 | What about regulatory concerns? | AI explains, banker recommends. Insights become "ideas to explore" with "keep as is"; AI says it is AI (AI Act from 02.08.2026); GDPR consent for the profile. Compliance review is part of our ask. |
| 4 | How confident are you in the ROI? | Not confident, and we say so. All numbers are illustrative. That is why we show the break-even (about €20,500 per engaged customer) and ask for data. |
| 5 | What happens if this fails? | We learn cheaply: what customers outside Premium need, at pilot cost. The Life Profile and hand-over can still serve RaiConnect and branches. |
| 6 | Only 2 customers? | Yes, plus 2 experts, a UX study and 1 test. That is enough to frame the problem, not to prove it. Round 2 adds 5–8 people before 04.11.2026; the pilot adds hundreds. |
| 7 | Isn't "Wealth Insights" just a product push in a new dress? | It is the risk we test (H4). Ideas show cost and risk, include "keep as is" and government bonds, and success is measured as confidence, not sales. |
| 8 | Who will answer all the "talk to an expert" requests? | Adviser capacity is open. Pilot invitations are capped and we measure demand; the passed-on context should make each call shorter. |
| 9 | You show costs, but C1 left because of price. Does this solve cost? | No, not alone. It makes costs clear and comparable. Pricing (e.g. a light Premium or paid session) is a pilot question for the business. |
| 10 | Does this cannibalise Premium? | It should feed Premium: customers who grow get a clear path up. We would track upgrades. |
| 11 | What about customers who don't use the app (C2)? | Branch path: the banker opens the same Life Profile on a tablet and prints a one-page plan. No phone offers. |
| 12 | Why Hungary? | Expert access, real interviews in Budapest, clear Premium thresholds, and savings moving between bonds and funds (MNB 2026). The Serbian advisory tool shows it can travel. |
| 13 | What does the AI actually do? | Explains terms and the customer's own Snapshot, using approved content. No personal recommendations. It discloses itself and hands over to a person. |
| 14 | What do you need from us tomorrow? | A sponsor name and a data contact. |
| 15 | How is this different from OTP's planner? | OTP's planner is branch-only with an adviser. Ours starts in the app the customer already uses and works in branch too, with the same profile. |

### 7. Presentation tips
- Rehearse to 9:30; cut slide 14 to one line if over.
- Slow down on slides 4 (quotes), 8 (honest evidence) and 16 (ask).
- Ask the room once, at slide 2: "Who here has asked ChatGPT about money?"
- Demo fallback: screenshots, never a live failure.
- When challenged on numbers, agree and point to the break-even.
