# 5.3.1 Pitch Review — My Wealth

RBI AI Prompt Library prompt 5.3.1, run 15.10.2026 with skill innovation-business-case-pitch. AI-assisted draft. All financial figures are illustrative assumptions, not RBI data. Pitch context (audience, 10 min) is assumed — confirm with the coach.

> **What is reviewed:** the deck in [5.2.1](5.2.1-pitch-deck.md) and the case in [5.1.1](5.1.1-business-case.md), as a critical reviewer for the assumed audience. Scores are the reviewer's judgement, not measurements.

## Filled prompt

```text
I have created a pitch deck and business case. I need help reviewing and strengthening it before presenting to stakeholders.

MY PITCH CONTENT:
Deck "My Wealth" (11 main slides in 10 minutes, 6 merged/backup), from 5.2.1:
1 Title: "My Wealth: clear guidance on your terms, for customers between self-service and Premium."
2 Problem: outside Premium, guidance feels like a product push, so customers decide elsewhere (family, ChatGPT, cheaper providers, bonds); C1 closed the bank's investment account over cost, bank offered nothing.
3 Opportunity: MNB 2026 (HUF 16,400bn funds, HUF 13,700bn retail bonds, about half of term deposits under 1%); Premium thresholds HUF 400k–700k income / HUF 10m; competitors (OTP branch planner, Erste webinars, Moneyfarm); segment size unknown, modelled 10k / 25k / 50k.
4 Customer insights: 2 customers + 2 experts; C1 "more of a product push", "I had to adjust my life around the availability of the adviser"; C2 "If it is by telephone, then no".
6 Solution: Snapshot (incl. cost), Life Profile, Learn, Playground, optional AI that explains and discloses itself, Talk to an Expert with context passed, opt-in notifications, branch path; reuses My Life, RaiConnect, advisory tool, Learn to Invest.
7 How it works: 4 prototype screenshots (Snapshot donut, notifications, Wealth Insights picks, Talk to an Expert), fictional customer "Alex".
8 Validation: 2 street interviews + 2 experts + UX study (secondary) + 1 prototype test (T1, 1 customer: notifications and simpler Insights, both fixed; decision ITERATE). Round 2 planned 16.10.–26.10.2026.
9 Benefits: confident, suitable decision (cash can be right); relationships kept; bankers start with context.
11 Financial case (illustrative, no RBI data): full-year benefit €15k / €180k / €1.06m; 3-year ROI −98% / −67% / +136%; payback none / >3 years / ~20 months; break-even about €20,500 kept or added per engaged customer; pilot €150k–€300k.
12 Plan + KPIs: round 2–3 tests and Compliance view (Nov–Dec 2026), 6-month pilot with 1,000–2,000 customers and a control group, gate at month 6; KPIs ≥70% understand options and costs, ≤20% felt like sales.
14 Risks: MiFID advice line, AI Act, GDPR, adviser capacity, cost not solved, C2 digital distrust.
16 Ask: sponsor at RBHU, data access, early Compliance review, approval to scope a 6-month pilot.
17 Closing: "Let's be the bank they ask first."
Business case alternatives: do nothing, I-08 guidance desk, light Premium, buy a platform. Prototype still shows "Top idea" and "BEST FIT FOR YOU" on Wealth Insights.

AUDIENCE:
RBI Innovation School coaches / innovation jury and RBI / RBHU business stakeholders (assumed; confirm with the coach). 10 minutes + 5 minutes Q&A (assumed). Priorities: real customer evidence and Design Thinking rigour (jury); customer value, regulatory safety, feasibility in existing systems, credible return, adviser capacity (business).
```

## Output

### Overall assessment

**Ready to present? Not yet, but close in structure.** The story is honest and well ordered, and the ask is sized right. The weaknesses are evidence (n=2 customers, 1 test), a prototype that contradicts our own MiFID message, and a financial slide that will confuse a jury in 60 seconds. Fix the five red flags below and run round 2 before 04.11.2026.

**Strengths:** real quotes; honest validation slide; pilot ask instead of a budget; reuse of existing assets; named risks including "cost not solved".
**Weaknesses:** thin customer evidence; three negative-or-mixed ROI numbers on one slide; "Top idea / BEST FIT FOR YOU" in the screenshots; no answer to adviser capacity; segment size unknown.

### 1. Message clarity check

| Message | Clarity | Impact | Evidence | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem: product push → decide elsewhere | 8 | 8 | Medium (2 customers + 2 experts) | Keep C1's exit story; add "experts describe the same" with source |
| Solution: My Wealth | 6 | 7 | Weak–Medium | Too many parts (8). Lead with 3: **Snapshot, Life Profile, Talk to an Expert**; the rest "also" |
| Value proposition | 6 | 7 | Medium | Not stated in one line. Use: "Start from my life, show me what it costs, help me when I ask." |
| Benefits | 6 | 5 | Weak | Customer benefit is clear; business benefit is a range nobody can hold in their head |
| Financial case | 5 | 4 | Weak (all assumptions) | Show only the break-even and the 3 drivers on the main slide; scenarios to backup |
| Ask | 8 | 7 | n/a | Clear; add a name/role ("sponsor from RBHU retail segment") and a date |

### 2. Audience alignment
- **Care most:** jury: evidence and learning; business: value, risk, effort, who does the work.
- **Language that works:** "pilot", "gate", "reuse", "first signal"; avoid "AI-powered wealth platform", "validated", "revolutionary".
- **Objections:** only 2 customers; product push in new clothes; who answers expert requests; MiFID; does it solve cost.
- **Most convincing evidence:** C1's real exit; MNB public data; a working prototype; round 2 results with C2-types.
- **Missing:** round 2 results; an RBHU data point (even one: segment size or closures); a Compliance first view; an RBHU person who supports it.

### 3. Narrative flow check
- Logical progression: yes (problem → insight → solution → proof → money → plan → ask).
- **Weak points:** slide 3 mixes market data and competitors (two messages); slide 11 kills momentum right after the benefits slide; slide 14 comes after the plan, so risk sounds like an afterthought.
- **Fixes:** split slide 3's headline to one message ("Savings are moving; nobody serves the gap below Premium"); put risks (14) before plan (12) so the plan reads as the mitigation; reframe 11 as "what must be true".
- **Pacing:** 11 slides in 10 minutes is tight; slide 7 (demo) at 1:30 is right; cut slide 9 to 30 seconds.

### 4. Data and evidence review

| Claim | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| €15k / €180k / €1.06m benefit | Spread of 70× shows we don't know; looks like a forecast if shown in a table | Label "ILLUSTRATIVE" on the slide, not only in notes; lead with drivers |
| ROI −98% / −67% / +136% | Two negative numbers on a pitch slide invite "why should we fund this?" | Keep them honest but move to backup; main slide shows break-even €20,500 per engaged customer |
| Pilot €150k–€300k | Team guess, no IT input | Say "to be estimated with RBHU IT"; or ask for a pilot *scope*, not a sum |
| Segment 10k / 25k / 50k | Pure placeholder | Get one RBHU number before 04.11.2026 |
| Net margin 0.5% | From the One-Pager, not RBI | Ask RBHU Finance; mention it is net of deposit margin |
| "≥70% understand options" KPI | Target without baseline | Fine as a pilot target; say so |
| MNB data | Credible, public | Keep; cite on slide |
| Comparison with OTP / Moneyfarm | Fair, from public pages (13.10.2026) | Keep "as advertised" |

Projections are not too optimistic in the expected case (they don't pay back). The optimistic case needs 15,000 engaged customers: call it what it is, an upside.

### 5. Visual and design feedback
- Slide 7 screenshots are the strongest visual; make them big, max 4.
- Slide 11 tornado chart is right for "drivers"; a 3×4 number table is not.
- Slide 6 flow diagram risks being a busy box chart; show 3 main parts large, others small.
- Slide 3 "help ladder" with the gap is a good visual; keep it, drop logo clutter.
- Each number slide needs a visible "Illustrative" tag.

### 6. Objection handling

| Objection | Where to address | Response strategy | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technical feasibility | Slide 6 (reuse tags) | Pilot uses a manual hand-over; integration only after the gate | My Life / RaiConnect team view |
| ROI scepticism | Slide 11 | Agree; show break-even and how the pilot measures it | RBHU segment size and margin |
| Implementation risk | Slide 12 | Gate at month 6; capped cohort | Pilot plan in backup |
| Customer adoption | Slide 8 | Round 2 results; activation is pilot KPI #1 | 5–8 tests incl. C2-types |
| Competitive response | Slide 3 | OTP branch-only; our edge is app + branch with one profile | Public pages |
| Resource availability (advisers) | Slide 14 | Capped invitations, measured demand, shorter calls with context | RaiConnect capacity numbers |
| "Product push in disguise" | Slide 7 | "Keep as is", cost and risk visible, success ≠ sales | Round 2 H4 result |

### 7. Impact maximisation
- **Story:** open with C1's exit: "One customer closed their investment account. The bank asked why, and offered nothing." (C1's gender is not recorded; say "they" or "one customer".)
- **Vivid problem:** a single screen of today's journey vs My Wealth, side by side.
- **Differentiation:** one sentence: "The only bank journey that starts with your life, shows your costs and hands you to a person who already knows your story."
- **Confident close:** end on the ask, not the vision.

### 8. Risk mitigation narrative
- Acknowledged appropriately: yes, and "cost not solved" earns trust.
- Credibility gap: **the screenshots contradict the MiFID message** if "Top idea / BEST FIT FOR YOU" is visible.
- Adviser capacity mitigation ("capped invitations") is thin; name who decides capacity.
- Confidence level: right for a pilot ask; don't sound more sure than "first signal".

### 9. Call to action strength
- Clear and appropriately sized (pilot, not build). Good.
- **Missing:** a named role and a date ("a sponsor from RBHU retail by 30.11.2026, pilot scope by 15.12.2026").
- Urgency: present (savings moving, AI Act in force, customers already use ChatGPT); say it on slide 16, not only 17.
- Next steps: defined in slide 12; repeat in one line on 16.

### 10. Practice and delivery tips
- **Nail:** slides 2 (hook), 7 (demo), 8 (honesty), 16 (ask).
- **Slow down:** on the three quotes; on "first signal, not proof"; on the ask.
- **Engage:** one question at slide 2 ("Who has asked ChatGPT about money?").
- **If short on time, cut:** slide 9 (say one line), slide 14 to one sentence, slide 3 to one number.

### 11. Confidence builders
- Customer validation: weak in size, strong in honesty: lead with honesty.
- Research process: full Design Thinking trail (understand → empathize → ideate → prototype → test), 70 ideas → 12 scored → concept.
- Rigour of business case: every number has a formula and assumption; break-even is shown.
- Team: built a clickable prototype and changed it the same day after the first feedback (T1, 15.10.2026).
- Strategic importance: reuses group assets; hybrid direction confirmed by HU expert.

### 12. Red flag check

| # | Red flag | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **"Top idea / BEST FIT FOR YOU" on Wealth Insights** reads as a personal recommendation (MiFID) and contradicts slide 14 | **High** | Change to "Ideas to explore", add "Keep as is", "not investment advice" label before screenshots are taken |
| R2 | **Illustrative numbers look like forecasts** when shown as a precise table (€1,060,500, +136%) | **High** | Round numbers; "ILLUSTRATIVE" tag on slide; main slide = break-even + drivers |
| R3 | **Overclaiming risk:** words like "validation", "customers want", "proven" with n=2 + 1 test | **High** | Use "first signal"; slide 8 title "Early, honest evidence" is right; check all speaker notes |
| R4 | **Cost not solved:** C1's main reason for leaving was price; My Wealth only shows cost | Medium–High | Say it on slide 14; make pricing a named pilot question |
| R5 | **Adviser capacity unanswered:** "Talk to an Expert" for 25,000 people with no capacity figure | Medium–High | Get one RaiConnect capacity fact; cap the pilot |
| R6 | Segment size unknown, so the "opportunity" slide has no size | Medium | One RBHU number before 04.11.2026 |
| R7 | Too many features for 10 minutes | Medium | 3 core parts on slide 6 |
| R8 | AI is in the story but no AI test was done | Medium | Present AI as optional, later phase; not the headline |
| R9 | Team confirmation of the concept is still pending (00-overview) | Medium | Confirm on 16.10.2026 |
| R10 | Prototype demo data contradictions (4.3.1: €157,000 vs €159,150; fund up and down) | Low–Medium | Fix before screenshots |

### 13. Benchmark and comparison
- **Best practices followed:** problem before solution; real quotes; a prototype; a sized, reversible ask; risks named.
- **Missing compared with strong pitches:** a single hard internal number; a stakeholder voice ("RBHU retail says…"); a before/after metric from testing (even round 2: "6 of 7 understood their yearly cost").
- **Innovation-proposal standard:** a stage-gated pilot with kill criteria is the norm; we have it. State the stop criteria explicitly (e.g. activation <10% or "felt like sales" >30%).

### 14. Final polish

**Headline options (pick one):**
1. "My Wealth: start from my life, not from your product."
2. "The bank they ask first."
3. "Guidance on my terms, for customers between self-service and Premium." (current, clear but long)

**Recommended: 1** for the title; use 2 as the closing line.

**Slide title improvements:**
- 2 → "We sell products; they want a plan"
- 4 → "What we heard in Budapest"
- 8 → "Early, honest evidence" (keep)
- 11 → "What must be true for this to pay"
- 16 → "Give us a sponsor and six months"

**Quote selection (most impactful, in order):** C1 "more of a product push" (slide 2 or 4); C1 "I had to adjust my life around the availability of the adviser" (slide 4); C2 "If it is by telephone, then no" (slide 4/14); C1 "If it was more personalised for me and if it would be cheaper" (slide 17).

**Tagline:** "Your life first. Your costs clear. A person when you ask."

**Opening (20 seconds):** "One of the customers we met in Budapest closed their investment account with their bank this year. Why? It "costs a lot more", and it felt like "more of a product push". The bank asked why they were leaving, and offered nothing. Today they ask their nephew and ChatGPT. We want to change who they ask."

**Closing line:** "Today our customers ask their nephew and ChatGPT. Give us a sponsor and six months to find out if they would ask us first."

### Prioritised recommendations
1. Fix the prototype wording (R1) and demo data (R10) before taking screenshots.
2. Rebuild slide 11 around the break-even and drivers; scenarios to backup (R2).
3. Run round 2 with C1- and C2-types; put one real result on slide 8 (R3).
4. Get one RBHU number (segment size or closures) and one RaiConnect capacity fact (R5, R6).
5. Cut slide 6 to three core parts; AI as optional later phase (R7, R8).
6. Add named role, date and stop criteria to the ask.
