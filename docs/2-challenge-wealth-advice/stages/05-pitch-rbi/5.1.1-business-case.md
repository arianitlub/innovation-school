# 5.1.1 Business Case — My Wealth

RBI AI Prompt Library prompt 5.1.1, run 15.10.2026 with skill innovation-business-case-pitch. AI-assisted draft. All financial figures are illustrative assumptions, not RBI data. Pitch context (audience, 10 min) is assumed — confirm with the coach.

> **Evidence base (exact):** 2 internal experts (E-HU-1 Hungary, E-RS-1 Serbia); 2 street interviews in Budapest (C1, C2); internal UX education study (8 Serbian interviews + 4-country synthesis, secondary); 1 prototype test round (T1, 1 customer, profile not recorded, 2 improvements: notifications and simpler Wealth Insights; decision ITERATE). **No internal RBI data.** Every number below is an assumption with its formula, to be replaced by RBHU data. ## Filled prompt

```text
I need to build a business case for my validated solution to secure funding and executive approval.

MY SOLUTION:
My Wealth: a new section in the existing Raiffeisen Hungary mobile app (My Life), unlocked when a customer reaches the mass-affluent threshold (bank already knows income, deposits, investments). Parts: My Wealth Snapshot (all money with the bank, what it earns and what it costs per year, goal progress); Life Profile (guided self-assessment of goals, horizon, access needs, comfort with losses; "this is not investment advice"; GDPR consent); Learn (2–3 minute lessons, reuses Learn to Invest); Investment Playground (virtual money, simulate years and a market fall); optional AI Wealth Assistant that explains and discloses it is AI (personal recommendations stay in the approved MiFID advisory process); Wealth Insights (illustrative ideas, not advice); Talk to an Expert (call, RaiConnect video, appointment) with the Life Profile and what the customer explored passed to the banker; opt-in nudges and notifications; branch path (banker opens the same Life Profile on a tablet, prints a one-page plan). Reuses My Life, RaiConnect, the advisory tool and Learn to Invest. Scope: Hungary, customers outside full Premium. Status: clickable prototype, 1 test round (T1), decision ITERATE. Not yet validated.

MY CHALLENGE CONTEXT:
- Innovation challenge: "How are mass affluent clients served with wealth management advice and where are the gaps? How can we find more ways to offer more value to customers during the process?" Working goal: give mass-affluent customers useful, affordable financial guidance that helps them achieve their goals and strengthens their relationship with the bank.
- Target customers/users: mass-affluent customers in Hungary outside full Premium/Private advisory support (personas: Self-Directed Comparer C1, Cautious Branch Saver C2, New-to-Investing Learner UX); secondary users: RaiConnect and branch bankers.
- Main problem solved: outside full Premium, the bank's investment guidance feels product-led, unclear, costly and on the bank's terms (phone offers, adviser's schedule), so customers decide elsewhere (family, ChatGPT, cheaper providers, government bonds) and the bank loses the investment relationship.
- Expected impact: customers reach a confident, suitable decision (keeping cash can be the right answer); more of them keep or start the investment relationship with the bank; banker conversations start with context instead of from zero.

WHAT DATA I HAVE (if available):
- Customer: 2 street interviews in Budapest (C1 left the bank's investment account over cost and "product push"; C2 rejects phone and online offers, trusts the branch). Internal UX study (8 Serbian interviews + 4-country synthesis, secondary).
- Expert: 2 internal experts (HU: mass affluent served non-advisory, digital investing 4–6 min with MiFID, RaiConnect for Premium, Learn to Invest; RS: advisory tool, Premium identification from salary/deposits). A Premium relationship manager contacts each client 2–3 times a year (expert-reported).
- Prototype: 1 test round (T1, 1 customer, 2 fixes, decision ITERATE). No task, time or satisfaction metrics.
- Public: MNB Savings Report 2026 (household funds HUF 16,400bn, retail government bonds HUF 13,700bn, about half of term deposits earn under 1%); Raiffeisen HU Premium from HUF 400k monthly income, Premium Plus HUF 700k income or HUF 10m savings (to verify); competitor fees (ING Smart Invest 0.65–0.75%, Moneyfarm paid upgrade GBP 12.99/month).
- Illustrative problem model from the Problem One-Pager: 1,000 customers x EUR 50,000 x 10% investing elsewhere = EUR 5m; at 0.5% = EUR 25,000 a year (not RBI figures).
- No internal RBI data: segment size, cancellations, outflows, margins, My Life drop-off, Premium uptake and costs are all unknown.

Help me build a compelling business case:

[PART 1 and PART 2 sections 1–13 and OUTPUT FORMAT exactly as in .claude/skills/innovation-business-case-pitch/prompts/5.1.1-business-case.md, unchanged]
```

**What the problem costs today:** revenue on money customers invest elsewhere; relationships lost at the exit moment (C1 was asked why but offered nothing); future Premium clients lost. **What executives will care about most:** retained relationships and assets, cost to serve per customer, regulatory safety, and whether this is worth a pilot. **Stay qualitative:** trust, brand ("the bank that doesn't push"), customer financial outcomes.

**Guardrail:** success means a confident, suitable decision. AUM is a downstream benefit, never a sales target. Keeping cash can be right.

### 1. Executive summary

**Problem.** Outside full Premium, guidance feels product-led, unclear, costly and on the bank's terms. Customers decide with family, ChatGPT, cheaper providers or government bonds (C1, C2, E-HU-1). The bank keeps the salary account but loses the investment relationship.

**Solution.** My Wealth: an app section unlocked at the mass-affluent threshold. It starts from the customer's life (Life Profile), makes money and costs clear (Snapshot), teaches (Learn, Playground), explains (optional AI, disclosed as AI), and hands over to a person with context when the customer asks (Talk to an Expert, branch path). It reuses existing systems.

**Benefits (illustrative, full year).** €15k (conservative) / €180k (expected) / €1.06m (optimistic) per year, mainly from assets kept or added. Plus customer outcomes we cannot price yet.

**Investment (illustrative).** Pilot €150k–€300k (expected €200k). Full build afterwards €0.5m–€1.0m, plus €0.2m–€0.25m a year to run.

**ROI (illustrative, 3 years incl. pilot and build).** Conservative −98%, no payback · Expected −67%, no payback in 3 years · Optimistic +136%, payback in about month 20.

**Recommendation.** Do **not** approve a full build. Ask for a **sponsor and a 6-month Hungary pilot** (1,000–2,000 invited customers, control group) to measure the three numbers that decide the case: engagement, assets per engaged customer, and net margin. In the expected case, a full build pays back in 3 years only if each engaged customer keeps or adds about **€20,500** (vs €6,000 assumed).

### 2. Problem quantification

No RBI data exists in our files, so the cost of the problem is a formula with three scenarios.

**Formula:** Annual revenue lost = eligible customers × share investing elsewhere × average amount invested elsewhere × net margin.

| | Conservative | Expected | Optimistic (bigger problem) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eligible customers outside full Premium (A1) | 10,000 | 25,000 | 50,000 |
| Share investing elsewhere each year (A2) | 5% | 10% | 15% |
| Average amount elsewhere (A3) | €20,000 | €30,000 | €40,000 |
| Money elsewhere | €10m | €75m | €300m |
| Net margin (A4) | 0.4% | 0.5% | 0.6% |
| **Annual revenue not earned** | **€40,000** | **€375,000** | **€1,800,000** |

Same logic as the One-Pager (1,000 × €50,000 × 10% = €5m → €25k), scaled to our assumed segment sizes.

Other costs, not quantified: lost future Premium clients; money sitting in deposits under 1% (MNB 2026), which costs the *customer*; reputation from cold calls (C2: "If it is by telephone, then no").

**Data needed (RBHU):** segment size by threshold, investment-account closures and reasons, outflows to bonds and brokers, net margin by product, My Life journey drop-off.

**Total annual cost of problem: €40k / €375k / €1.8m (illustrative).**

### 3. Solution benefits — quantified

Three benefit lines. All assumptions are in the annex.

**B1 Assets kept or added** = eligible × engagement rate × assets per engaged customer × net margin
**B2 Retained leavers** = eligible × annual investment-exit rate × share My Wealth saves × average balance × net margin
**B3 Banker preparation saved** = expert conversations × minutes saved ÷ 60 × banker cost per hour

| | Conservative | Expected | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | 1,000 × €3,000 × 0.4% = **€12,000** | 5,000 × €6,000 × 0.5% = **€150,000** | 15,000 × €10,000 × 0.6% = **€900,000** |
| B2 | 20 × €20,000 × 0.4% = **€1,600** | 150 × €25,000 × 0.5% = **€18,750** | 600 × €30,000 × 0.6% = **€108,000** |
| B3 | 300 × 10 min × €30/h = **€1,500** | 1,500 × 15 min × €30/h = **€11,250** | 4,500 × 20 min × €35/h = **€52,500** |
| **Total full year (Year 2)** | **€15,100** | **€180,000** | **€1,060,500** |

B1 counts money that would otherwise go elsewhere or stay in low-yield deposits. Net margin must be *net* of the deposit margin lost when cash moves into funds. **Year 1** = 10% of full year (pilot only). **Year 3** = Year 2 × 1.3 (more customers reach the threshold; earlier assets stay). Both factors are assumptions.

**Total annual benefit (full year): €15k / €180k / €1.06m (illustrative).**

**What must be true (expected case, break-even in 3 years):** benefits of €1,305,000. B2 + B3 give €30,000 × 2.4 benefit-years = €72,000. ### 7. Non-financial benefits

- **Customer outcome:** a confident, suitable decision, including "keep it as cash" or "a government bond fits you". Help on the customer's schedule (C1: "I had to adjust my life around the availability of the adviser").
- **Trust and brand:** no cold calls; the customer controls contact (C2, E-HU-1).
- **Fair exit:** an honest alternative when someone leaves, instead of nothing (C1).
- **Differentiation:** Hungarian Premium pages promise "personal" but rarely show scope, cost or follow-up (1.1.2); My Wealth shows them.
- **Foundation:** a Life Profile and a hand-over that RaiConnect, branches and later markets (RS advisory tool) can reuse.
- **Banker experience:** conversations start with context; less data collection.
- **Data:** consented insight into goals and doubts of the segment.

### 8. Risks and mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **MiFID advice line:** Insights or AI read as a personal recommendation (T1 asked "what exactly is good to invest"; prototype says "Top idea / BEST FIT FOR YOU") | Rename to "ideas to explore"; add "keep as is"; Compliance review before pilot; recommendations only in the approved advisory process |
| **AI Act:** AI must disclose itself (from 02.08.2026); hallucination (ESMA 2024) | Clear "I am an AI" label, answers limited to approved content, fallback to a person, logs |
| **GDPR:** Life Profile data | Explicit consent, purpose and retention; separate from or clearly linked to the MiFID profile |
| **Adviser capacity:** more requests than RaiConnect can take | Pilot with capped invitations; show wait time; measure demand before scaling |
| **Cost not solved:** C1 left because of price; My Wealth shows costs but does not lower them | State it openly; Snapshot + comparer; test pricing options (light Premium) in the pilot |
| **Customer adoption / C2 digital distrust** ("If it is by telephone, then no") | Branch path with tablet and printed plan; opt-in only; no outbound calls |
| **Unlock feels like sales** | Neutral unlock wording; test H1 in round 2 |
| **Technology/integration** (My Life, CRM, RaiConnect) | Pilot with manual hand-over first; integrate only after the gate |
| **Competitive response** (OTP planner, Erste webinars, fintechs) | Speed through reuse; differentiation on life-led guidance + hand-over |

### 9. Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not chosen (now) |
|---|---|
| **Do nothing** | Problem continues: €40k–€1.8m a year not earned (illustrative); customers keep deciding with family, ChatGPT, brokers; competitors (OTP planner, Erste webinars) move first. Zero cost, but no learning |
| **I-08 Guidance desk for non-Premium (RaiConnect)** | Strong customer value, but adviser capacity and cost to serve are the limit (E-HU-1). Could become the "talk to an expert" layer of My Wealth later |
| **Light Premium (lower threshold or paid upgrade, cf. Moneyfarm)** | Pricing and willingness to pay unknown; C1 left over cost. Test as a pilot variant, not a separate project |
| **Buy a robo/planning platform** | Faster, but local rules, language and integration with My Life still needed; keep as a build-vs-buy check after the pilot |

## What to do with this

- **Replace A1–A9 with RBHU data first** (segment size, closures, outflows, net margin). The case changes most with A1, A4 and A6; re-run this prompt when the numbers arrive.
- **Present the break-even, not the ROI:** "the full build pays back if each engaged customer keeps or adds about €20,500". That makes the pilot's job clear and avoids numbers that look like forecasts.
- **Ask Compliance early** about Wealth Insights wording ("Top idea / BEST FIT FOR YOU"), the AI assistant and the Life Profile before any pilot.
- **Get a cost estimate from RBHU IT/Finance** for the pilot (our €150k–€300k range is a placeholder).
- **Confirm with the team and the coach** the concept, the pilot ask and the audience before turning this into slides (5.2.1).
