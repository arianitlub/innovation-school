# Business case briefing — My Wealth (one page)

*15.10.2026. Short version of [5.1.1](5.1.1-business-case.md), built on the three ingredients from the course: **Problem, Solution, Next steps**. Focus: numbers, evidence and arguments, action plan. All euro figures are our illustrative assumptions, not RBI data.*

## 1. The problem

**What we say:** Outside full Premium, investment guidance feels product-led, unclear, costly and on the bank's terms. Customers decide with family, ChatGPT, cheaper providers or government bonds. The bank keeps the salary account but loses the investment relationship.

**How we verified it (first signals only):**
- 2 street interviews in Budapest (C1 left over cost and "product push"; C2 rejects phone offers, trusts the branch).
- 2 internal experts (E-HU-1 Hungary, E-RS-1 Serbia): mass affluent get non-advisory service; Premium gets advisers.
- 1 internal UX study (Serbia, secondary).
- Public data: MNB 2026, nearly half of term deposits earn under 1% (all savers, not only our segment).

**Size of the problem (formula, no RBI data yet):** customers × share investing elsewhere × amount × margin = **€40k / €375k / €1.8m a year** (conservative / expected / optimistic).

## 2. The solution

**My Wealth:** an app section in My Life, unlocked at the mass-affluent threshold. Life Profile, Snapshot (money and costs), Learn, Playground, optional disclosed AI, and **Talk to an Expert** with context passed to the banker. Branch path on a tablet. It reuses My Life, RaiConnect, the advisory tool and Learn to Invest.

**Numbers (3 years, illustrative):**

| | Conservative | Expected | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Benefit per full year | €15k | €180k | €1.06m |
| One-time cost (pilot + build) | €1.3m | €0.9m | €0.65m |
| Run cost per year | €255k | €203k | €214k |
| ROI, 3 years | −98% | −67% | +136% |
| Payback | none | not in 3 years | ~20 months |

**Break-even (the number to remember):** in the expected case, the full build pays back in 3 years only if each engaged customer keeps or adds about **€20,500** (we assumed €6,000). Biggest drivers: assets per engaged customer, net margin, segment size. We do not know any of them yet.

**Non-financial:** confident, suitable decisions (keeping cash can be right), trust ("the bank that doesn't push"), fair exit, reusable Life Profile and hand-over, banker conversations with context.

**Honest risks:** MiFID advice line (rename "Top idea / BEST FIT FOR YOU"), AI Act disclosure, GDPR consent, banker capacity, cost not solved (C1 left over price).

## What we could say (5 minutes)

1. **Hook:** "Nearly half of Hungarian term deposits earn under 1%. Our customers ask their nephew and ChatGPT, not us."
2. **Problem:** "Outside Premium, guidance feels like a product push, and the customer pays or waits."
3. **Insight:** "Customers want to decide alone in the app and reach a person when they choose. No cold calls."
4. **Idea:** "My Wealth: start from the customer's life, show costs, teach, and pass context to a banker on request." (live demo)
5. **Impact:** "Confident decisions first. Retained assets as a result. The full build only pays back at about €20,500 per engaged customer, so we do not ask for a build."
6. **Honesty:** "This is a first signal: 2 customers, 2 experts, 1 test. Euro figures are our assumptions."

## What we could ask

1. **A sponsor** in RBHU who owns a 6-month pilot decision.
2. **About €200k** (placeholder, to confirm with IT/Finance) for the pilot only, not the full build.
3. **Data access:** segment size, investment-account closures and reasons, outflows, net margin, My Life drop-off.
4. **Early Compliance review** of Wealth Insights wording, the AI assistant and the Life Profile.
5. **Access to 1,000–2,000 customers and a control group**, and a few bankers for the hand-over.

## Likely questions, honest answers

- *Is it validated?* No. First signals from 2 customers, 2 experts, 1 test. The pilot is the validation.
- *Where do the numbers come from?* Our assumptions with formulas (annex of 5.1.1). We have no RBI data, and we ask for it.
- *Isn't this advice?* Not in the app. Personal recommendations stay in the approved MiFID process. Compliance reviews first.
- *Doesn't RBI already have this?* Parts exist (My Life, RaiConnect, Learn to Invest). The gap is the guided path and the hand-over for customers outside Premium.
- *What if it fails?* The pilot is small and reversible; the stop gate is at month 6.
