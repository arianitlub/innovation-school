# 5.1.1 — My Wealth business case (illustrative planning model)

Updated **15.10.2026** using RBI Stage 5 business-case guidance and the current evidence map.

> **Important:** We do not yet have RBI internal customer counts, implementation estimates, product margin data, or measured uplift from a pilot. Every euro figure below is an **illustrative scenario for leadership discussion, not a forecast or approved budget**.

## 1. Executive summary

**Problem:** mass-affluent customers outside full Premium/Private advisory support can have digital investment access but still lack enough personalised guidance when they become uncertain.

**Proposed solution:** **My Wealth**, a guided layer inside the existing mobile-banking app: Life Profile, Learn, Investment Playground, optional AI explainer, Wealth Insights, and context-rich handover to a human adviser.

**Recommended decision:** do not approve a full production build yet. Approve a **€300k–€400k controlled pilot** in one market. The pitch uses **€350k** as the working pilot envelope.

**Full-MVP ROM:** approximately **€0.9m–€1.3m** one-time build, with a base planning case around **€1.06m**. Annual run/maintenance is estimated at **€220k–€300k**. These are planning assumptions only.

## 2. Why a banking-app build costs more than a normal app feature

A production solution touches more than mobile UI:

| Cost bucket | Illustrative base case |
|---|---:|
| Customer experience + mobile integration | €240k |
| Backend, data and adviser integrations | €300k |
| AI / knowledge layer / guardrails | €110k |
| Security, privacy, compliance, legal, QA/UAT | €185k |
| Analytics, change, training and operations | €85k |
| Contingency | €140k |
| **Illustrative total** | **€1.06m** |

Typical people involved would include Product, UX, mobile engineering, backend/API engineering, data, investment/advisory SMEs, Security, Compliance, Legal, Privacy/DPO, Model Risk/AI governance, QA/UAT, Release Management, Analytics, Operations and customer-facing advisers.

## 3. Pilot ask — €350k

A pilot should be intentionally narrower than the production solution:

| Pilot workstream | Illustrative envelope |
|---|---:|
| Discovery, UX, customer testing and compliance shaping | €80k |
| Limited mobile + backend integration | €150k |
| AI/education layer + analytics | €60k |
| Security, QA, UAT and release readiness | €60k |
| **Pilot envelope** | **€350k** |

**Pilot scope:** one market, limited eligible cohort, one cross-functional squad, real customer testing, controlled adviser handover, measurable funnel, and explicit stop/scale decision gate.

## 4. Illustrative business upside

Use a sensitivity model instead of pretending we know the answer:

**Eligible customers × activation × incremental invested assets per active user × annual revenue contribution**

For the deck, we use a deliberately transparent illustrative blended revenue contribution of **0.6% per year on incremental invested assets**.

| Scenario | Eligible customers | Active users | Incremental AUM / active user | Incremental AUM | Illustrative annual revenue contribution |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 25,000 | 15% | €10,000 | €37.5m | €0.23m |
| Base | 50,000 | 25% | €15,000 | €187.5m | €1.13m |
| Upside | 100,000 | 30% | €20,000 | €600m | €3.60m |

These scenarios **do not include** possible value from retention, Premium conversion, cards/FX/deposits, cross-sell, adviser-capacity gains, or reduced customer leakage to external investment providers.

## 5. Data leadership should replace before approving scale

1. Actual count of eligible / near-threshold mass-affluent customers in the chosen market.
2. Existing investment penetration and external investment leakage.
3. Average investable assets and realistic incremental AUM opportunity.
4. Product economics / contribution margin by investment type.
5. Adviser capacity and cost-to-serve.
6. Expected technology, compliance and operating costs from RBI delivery teams.
7. Pilot activation, completion, adviser-handoff and suitable-action rates.
8. Retention and relationship-depth impact.

## 6. Pilot success metrics

Do not define success as “more people invested”. Measure:

- My Wealth activation / profile completion
- Learn / Playground engagement
- comprehension and confidence improvement
- adviser handoff rate and reasons
- suitable next action (which can include keeping cash)
- customer satisfaction / trust
- repeat engagement
- incremental AUM where appropriate
- adviser time per supported customer
- compliance / operational issues

## 7. Risks and mitigation

- **Customer adoption:** test value proposition and contact preferences before scale.
- **Regulatory / suitability risk:** AI explains; personal recommendations stay in the approved advisory process.
- **AI/model risk:** narrow knowledge scope, disclosure, monitoring, escalation and human review.
- **Integration complexity:** reuse existing mobile app, RaiConnect/adviser capabilities and investment infrastructure where possible.
- **Cost overrun:** phase gates and a narrow pilot before production architecture is committed.
- **Digital exclusion:** preserve branch / human path; AI remains optional.

## 8. Recommendation

> **Approve a €350k controlled pilot envelope, not a €1m+ production build.**

Use the pilot to replace assumptions with RBI data. If customers engage, understand the guidance, request help at the right moments, and the economics are credible, then prepare the full production business case.
