## How to use the interview material

The available transcript is translated from an imperfect automatic transcription. Speaker labels are unreliable. Treat the 1.5-hour effort, salary threshold, pilot details and other figures as reported examples, with market and uncertainty stated. The four-question profiling suggestion and chatbot coverage estimates are unvalidated opinions. Regulatory remarks are source statements and need market-specific checking before any live implementation.

The 20 customer interview questions cover useful themes, but many ask broad opinions or assume the person already has an adviser. For research, start with a recent concrete experience: what triggered it, what the person did, where they got stuck, whom they asked, and what happened afterwards. Ask about channel and technology preferences later. Compare customer experience with the expert view rather than treating the expert as a substitute for customers.

Next useful evidence: one or two actual customer experiences; the chosen market's segment definition; where the existing journey loses users; adviser time spent on explanation versus administration; and what the reported robo pilot already provides. These are investigation tasks, not confirmed team assignments.
