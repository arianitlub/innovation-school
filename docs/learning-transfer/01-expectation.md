## What I initially wrote in the template

### Learn it
**What would I like to learn or become more confident in?**

> I want to become more confident in facilitating workshops and in using Design Thinking to understand real problems before jumping to solutions. I also want to learn how to build a culture where people feel safe to try new ideas and learn from failures

### Apply it
**In which situations in my daily work could this learning be useful, and what could improve as a result?**

### Share it
**Who could benefit from what I bring back from Innovation School?**

## Initial interpretation

Before Budapest, my main idea was:

- improve my workshop facilitation;
- use Design Thinking more deliberately before jumping to solutions;
- bring that behavior into my team rather than keeping it as training knowledge;
- create a recurring **Innovation Hour** around real shared bottlenecks;
- gradually let other people facilitate too;
- share useful outcomes with other team leads and my line manager.

This is still an **initial expectation**. After Budapest and the practical homework, the final Learning Transfer Goal should become **one concrete recurring change in how I work**, not just a one-time workshop.

## Current candidate direction

A possible progression to validate after Budapest:

**Pain-point interviews → first Innovation Hour as practical homework → recurring Innovation Hour as Learning Transfer Goal.**

Possible first themes:

- knowledge sharing across projects;
- onboarding;
- recurring engineering bottlenecks;
- testing practices;
- feeling more like one team despite working on different RBI projects.
