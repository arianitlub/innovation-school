# Learning Transfer Goal — Version 2 (draft)

*16.10.2026. Replaces the Innovation Hour direction from `01-expectation.md` as the main Goal. To be aligned with the line manager on 05./06.11.2026. Targets are proposals, not agreed.*

## Why the change

The Expectation (09.10.2026) was general: facilitation and Design Thinking with an "Innovation Hour". Now the topic is concrete: **raise the AI maturity of my team of about 20 engineers and testers**. RBI encourages all teams to move up the AI-assisted SDLC/PDLC levels, up to level 4 (orchestration). Some people already work at a high level. Most of the team does not yet. Design Thinking and facilitation are my **method**; AI maturity is the **topic**.

## The Goal (one sentence)

> Every month, I run a short session and a buddy round where the most advanced AI users in my team coach colleagues on real tickets, so the whole team moves up the RBI AI maturity levels step by step.

One focus, recurring, as the programme asks.

## What changes in how I work

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Advanced AI users work alone; their know-how stays with them | They coach others on real tickets, and what works becomes shared |
| Everyone starts from zero with AI | New joiners to AI start from a shared starter kit |
| I guess where people stand | A simple baseline shows progress every cycle |
| Level 4 is a distant target | Each cycle has one small experiment with an owner |

## How it works (each monthly cycle)

1. **Buddy pairs:** one advanced user with one colleague for 4 weeks, on a real ticket.
2. **Live session (30–60 min):** an advanced engineer works a real ticket with AI while the team watches and asks.
3. **Starter kit:** what works becomes a reusable instruction, skill or prompt in a shared place.
4. **Experiments:** 2–3 small ones with an owner, a deadline and a success measure.
5. **Review at 2–4 weeks:** continue, improve, scale or stop.

Optional later: invite advanced engineers from other RBI teams to help one team for one sprint.

## How I will know it works (proposals, to agree with the line manager)

| Measure | Proposed target by 15.12.2026 |
|---|---|
| Baseline of the team's level (simple self-assessment, repeated each cycle) | First baseline in the first session |
| Buddy pairs completed | 4–6 |
| Live sessions held | 2–3 |
| Reusable assets that someone else actually reused | At least 3 |
| People who moved up one level on at least one dimension (self-reported with an example) | First signals, no fixed number |
| Time saved per ticket in the experiments (rough) | Measure, no target |

I do not promise "the whole team at level 4 by 15.12".
