# Draft — Human-AI collaboration in Innovation

Draft of 27.10.2026 for the team to agree. Audience: line managers, 04.11.2026. 5 slides, 5 minutes (about 1 minute per slide). To be moved into the Slide Master.

## Slide 1 — Human-AI collaboration in Innovation

*Human judgment + AI capabilities*

- People bring customer understanding, purpose, and accountability.
- AI helps analyse information, explore alternatives, and create drafts.
- Together, we can develop and test ideas faster.

## Slide 2 — Apply it in everyday work

*Explore → Create → Validate*

- Explore: use AI to prepare research questions and suggest patterns.
- Create: develop alternative ideas and simple prototypes.
- Validate: people check evidence and test concepts with real users.

## Slide 3 — Example: our wealth-advice challenge

*Illustrative collaboration*

- People identify customers' needs through interviews.
- AI helps organise findings and draft alternative app concepts.
- The team selects a concept and tests it with customers.
- Customer feedback guides the next iteration.

## Slide 4 — What management should enable

*Tools, skills, and clear ownership*

- Provide approved tools and clear rules for data use.
- Give teams time to practise and share learning.
- Assign responsibility for reviewing outputs and decisions.
- Encourage small experiments and honest feedback.

## Slide 5 — Make collaboration a habit

*Our proposal: a four-week pilot*

- Choose one recurring task and record today's performance.
- Agree what AI does and what people review.
- Review quality, time, and rework weekly.
- After four weeks, decide: improve, expand, or stop.
