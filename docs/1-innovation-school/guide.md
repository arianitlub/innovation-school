# Innovation School — Guide

Short reference notes from the RBI Innovation eLearning course.

## Why Innovation
Innovation keeps organizations relevant and competitive as technology, regulation, and customer expectations keep changing.

## What Is Innovation
Turning new ideas into products, services, or processes that create value. Two modes:
- **Explore** — new business models/customers.
- **Exploit** — improving existing business.

Innovation covers more than tech (business models, processes, culture). Failure is expected; learning from it matters more than guaranteed success.

## Design Thinking — RBI Innovation Box
Human-centered, iterative process:
1. **Understand** — define problem, map process, find pain points/stakeholders.
2. **Empathize & Synthesize** — validate assumptions via research/interviews.
3. **Ideate** — generate/refine ideas, visualize solutions.
4. **Prototype & Test** — build simple prototypes, test with customers, iterate.
5. **Business Case & Pitch** — cost-benefit analysis, secure stakeholder buy-in.

## Innovation Culture
Framework: **Want** (motivation) + **May** (safe environment/permission) + **Can** (skills via 1-innovation-school/coaching).
Facilitation matters: clear agendas, ground rules, ice breakers/energizers, visual tools (post-its, flipcharts, boards), and follow-up action items.

## Failure Culture
Failure is a stepping stone, not a stigma:
- Transparency, sharing lessons learned.
- Ownership of mistakes.
- Constructive feedback, both ways.
- Celebrate effort and calculated risk, not just success.
Example contrast: Kodak (failed to innovate) vs. Google ("fail fast").

## Resilience
Persistence, adaptability, and stress management sustain innovation efforts. RBI provides courses/workshops for building resilience.

## Turning Learnings Into Action
Apply tools immediately (check-ins, user interviews). Innovation is continuous, supported by RBI's Innovation Management programs.

## Key Concepts Recap
- Explore vs. Exploit
- Empathize & Synthesize
- Prototype & Test
- Business Case (benefits, costs, risks, resources)
- Culture: Want / May / Can
- Failure Culture
- Facilitation
- Resilience

# Innovation School 2026 — Program Notes

Live notes from the actual **Innovation School 2026** cohort (online kickoff + Budapest on-site days + closing + follow-up). This section tracks the real program structure, participation model, and deadlines as they are communicated, and will be extended as new sessions happen.

## Why This Program Exists
Innovation at RBI isn't driven by one central team — it happens wherever people spot a better way of doing things and act on it. Innovation Management's role is to provide structure, method, guidance, and support. Innovation School is one of the main tools for this: it doesn't aim to turn everyone into an expert/facilitator/coach, but to push participants out of their comfort zone to experiment, learn, and discover what's possible — so they recognize opportunities and know where to start.

Throughout the program, participants collect **"puzzle pieces"** — tools added to a personal innovation toolbox — coming from:
- Innovation methodologies (Design Thinking)
- Reflection & practice
- Facilitation
- Innovation culture
- Innovation mindset

Goal: graduate as an **Innovation Ambassador** — someone who applies Innovation School methods/mindsets in daily work to create small real improvements, and shows others how to adopt those ways of working. Not an expert in every method — someone who recognizes opportunities, acts, and shares.

## How Participation Works
- **Main call** (plenum, e.g. the kickoff/closing sessions): all content and instructions are delivered here; **this part is recorded** for communication, marketing, and learning purposes.
- **Breakout rooms**: teamwork with your coach, in a dedicated Miro/team board the coach shares when needed. **No recording** during breakout work.
- **Teams channels**: your coach is your main point of contact for the whole program, including the periods between sessions. For questions about the online sessions themselves, use the Teams chat. For anything else / cross-cohort communication, use the dedicated Innovation School Teams group.
- **Ground rules**: keep an open mind, cameras on during online sessions, avoid interruptions (notifications off / phone on flight mode), be on time at the start of each day and after every break.
- **Team structure**: ~18 coaches guide the cohort; each team works on a fictitious **Innovation Challenge** case together with its coach throughout the on-site days.

## Getting Familiar With Your Challenge (Team Task, Kickoff Day)
Recommended approach (used first ~15 min team breakout):
- First part: align on a shared understanding of the topic — how would you tackle it, what information is missing, what data would you collect next, what would you ask an internal expert?
- Last ~10 min: prepare an action list (who does what) for the prep window until on-site days.
- **Contact at least one internal expert as a team** (not individually) before the on-site days, to understand the current status quo of the issue at RBI. Your coach can connect you with at least one expert already briefed to expect your outreach.
- Optional: collect additional data, do online/AI research.
- No further deliverable is expected before Budapest — on-site days have dedicated time to go through all Design Thinking steps; this is just a head start.

### Innovation Challenge (slides 25–31)
- Teams work on a **fictitious case** during the on-site days; research starts now (introduction 30.09 → research until 13.10).
- Principle: **data brings facts, facts help to orientate** — combine customer insights with hard facts (e.g. 200 customers drop off/month, 2,500 complaints/year, 1,200 Jira tickets for one process, 40% of requests need manual rework, avg. processing time 12 days). If you can't get facts, **experts** are a first starting point.
- Team task (50 min): discuss the topic 40 min (same understanding? 10 · how would you tackle it? 10 · what info is missing? 10 · which data next / what to ask an internal expert? 10), then 10 min to define what to do until on-site (talk to ≥1 internal expert **as a team**; optionally collect data / online or AI research). **No further deliverables** before Budapest.
