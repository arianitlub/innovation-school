Interview Questions
"How are mass affluent clients served with wealth management advice and where are the gaps? How can we find more ways to offer more value to customers during the process?"

1. How do you currently receive wealth management advice from your bank?
2. What aspects of the advice provided do you find most valuable?
3. Are there any areas where you feel the advice or service could be improved?
4. How personalized do you feel the wealth management advice is to your individual goals?
5. What types of financial products or services do you wish were offered or explained more clearly?
6. How frequently do you interact with your wealth advisor, and is this frequency sufficient for your needs?
7. Have you encountered any challenges or frustrations during the wealth management process?
8. How well does the bank help you understand investment risks and opportunities?
9. Are digital tools or platforms provided by the bank helpful in managing your wealth?
10. What additional support or resources would enhance your wealth management experience?
11. How do you prefer to receive advice—face-to-face, phone, digital, or a mix?
12. Are there any gaps in communication or follow-up from your wealth management team?
13. How well does the bank integrate your broader financial needs (e.g., tax, estate planning) into the advice?
14. What motivates you to stay with your current bank for wealth management services?
15. What could the bank do to increase your trust and confidence in their wealth management advice?
16. How do you compare the bank’s wealth management services to competitors?
17. Would you be interested in educational workshops or seminars on wealth management topics?
18. How important is sustainability or ethical investing advice in your portfolio decisions?
19. What role do you think technology should play in enhancing your wealth management experience?
20. Can you share an example of a positive or negative experience with your wealth management advisor?
