# Prototype feedback — My Wealth (T1)

Source: customer feedback on the clickable My Wealth prototype (`docs/prototype/my-wealth-prototype.html`), reported by Arianit in the chat on 15.10.2026. Number of participants, their profile and the test setup were not recorded.

## Changes made after this feedback (15.10.2026)

- Added a notification bell in the app header with 2 clickable notifications (investments up; equity fund down), each opening a detail screen.
- Rewrote Wealth Insights: one headline, one allocation bar, 4 clear picks with amount, one "why" line, risk meter and cost.
