# Ideas and suggestions for team discussion

Our working goal is useful, affordable financial guidance that helps customers achieve their goals and strengthens their relationship with the bank.

These are our proposals prompted by the evidence, not recommendations selected by the team. Pair each with a customer problem and an experiment.

| Candidate | Customer problem it might solve | Small first test | Main uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short public educational lessons | “I do not understand the basics or the explanation in front of me” | Test a brief lesson before a fictional proposal; check understanding and questions still needing human help | Does it improve comprehension and reduce repeated explanation? |
| Plain-language explanation of an existing proposal | “I see a recommendation but do not know why it fits me” | Compare existing text with a one-page explanation using a fictional case | Is explanation the real blocker? |
| Short wealth health check | “I do not know which financial task to address first” | Role-play a structured conversation ending in three actions | Is the scope useful and deliverable in the time? |
| Human handover with context | “I get stuck and must start again with another person” | Test a help button, case summary and callback role-play | Waiting time, capacity and shared information |
| Goal progress and review prompt | “I own a fund but do not know whether my plan is on track” | Paper dashboard with a changed contribution/life-event scenario | Data quality and understood uncertainty |
| Banker meeting preparation | “Too much appointment time goes to administration” | Mock pre-meeting summary reviewed by a banker | Accurate inputs and integration effort |
| Guided first-investment journey | “I abandon the process before completing it” | Walk through a few paper screens and funding instructions | Which stage actually causes drop-off? |
| Needs-based invitation | “The bank contacts me about a product, not my situation” | Compare a generic message with a permission-based planning invitation | Relevance, privacy expectations and reach |

**Proposed exploration order:** investigate a current recommendation and support handover first. They are concrete, quick to prototype and can complement an existing advice platform. If interviews show a broader planning or ongoing-support gap, redirect towards the health check or goal review. This ordering is preparation advice, not a team decision.

Prepared 10.10.2026. Based on [our research](02-mass-affluent-customers-bank-services-and-retention.md); practical experiments are described in [Design Thinking preparation](03-solving-our-challenge-with-design-thinking.md). These are candidate ideas, not agreed team decisions.

## Model discussed with Arianit — 10.10.2026

Combine bank-approved education on the website/app, guided digital steps, a shared adviser team for questions and complex cases, and goal reviews afterwards. Public content APIs are a possible delivery mechanism; their existence does not establish customer value. Personal data and personalised advice require the appropriate service rather than a public educational endpoint.

The hypothesis is that this can improve understanding and timely help while reducing repetitive staff work. Test that benefit first. A more advanced concept should address an observed gap; extra features or AI alone do not prove improvement.
