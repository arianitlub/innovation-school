# Source register and original reading material

Checked 10.10.2026. Summaries and applications: [deep research](02-mass-affluent-customers-bank-services-and-retention.md) and [Design Thinking preparation](03-solving-our-challenge-with-design-thinking.md).

Research method: reviewed the existing local notes and expert transcript, then compared public bank/service disclosures, provider journey descriptions, regulator evidence and research publishers' own findings. This is desk research; no new customer interviews, internal analytics or hands-on account testing were performed. Product pages are current-page observations, not evidence of how well services perform.

| [McKinsey US wealth scenarios](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/us-wealth-management-in-2035-a-transformative-decade-begins) | US-focused forward-looking material, checked 10.10.2026 | Optional strategic reading about broader planning; scenarios are not established future outcomes |

EY's methodology explicitly labels its segment ranges as assets. One sentence in its Australian release describes the ranges as annual earnings; use the methodology for the definition and avoid repeating that inconsistent wording.
