# Innovation School Presentations

This folder contains PDF presentation material for the Mass-Affluent Wealth Advice Innovation Challenge.

## 1. Team alignment deck

- `innovation-challenge-team-alignment.pdf`

Purpose: align the team on the challenge, customer, current evidence, market examples, possible journey gaps and open questions before choosing a solution.

Source: `tools/create_innovation_deck.js`.

## 2. Design Thinking journey & status

- `design-thinking-journey-status.pdf`

Purpose: show the full Design Thinking process, our current position, what we already know, what must be agreed in the Understand phase, what happens in each next phase, and the decision gate before moving forward.

The central rule is:

> Problem first -> evidence second -> solution third.

Working positioning statement:

> How can we give customers with higher income or savings a level of guidance that is more valuable than generic retail guidance, but still cheaper and more scalable than full private banking?

Source: `tools/create_design_thinking_status.js`.

## 3. Future scenario - from challenge to tested concept

- `future-scenario-design-thinking.pdf`

Purpose: show an illustrative end-of-workshop story in which the team moves through Understand -> Empathize -> Define -> Ideate -> Prototype & Test and arrives at a hybrid financial-guidance concept plus a proposed validation plan.

Important: this deck is explicitly a **future scenario**. Research findings, persona details, test feedback and outcomes shown in it are hypothetical and must not be treated as validated project evidence.

Source: `tools/create_future_scenario.js`.

## 4. My Wealth proposed solution

- `my-wealth-proposed-solution.pdf`

Purpose: visualise the proposed My Wealth concept inside the banking app, including Learn, Playground, optional AI guidance and adviser handover.

Source: `tools/create_my_wealth_solution.js`.

## 5. My Wealth 5-minute leadership pitch v3

- `my-wealth-5min-pitch-v3.pdf`

Purpose: leadership-ready 5-minute storyline using **Hook -> Problem -> Insight -> Idea -> Impact -> Investment -> Business Upside -> Ask**.

The deck adds an illustrative business case:

- full-MVP ROM: **€0.9m-€1.3m**
- controlled pilot: **€300k-€400k**
- working ask: **€350k pilot envelope**
- sensitivity scenarios for incremental AUM and annual revenue contribution

All financial figures are explicitly **illustrative planning assumptions, not RBI estimates or forecasts**. Actual customer counts, activation, AUM uplift, product economics, delivery cost and retention impact must be replaced with internal data before a scale decision.

Source: `tools/create_my_wealth_pitch_v3.js`.

The preparation/future-scenario decks should not be read as validated outcomes. The current pitch deliberately preserves evidence limits and uses a pilot decision gate before any full production investment.
