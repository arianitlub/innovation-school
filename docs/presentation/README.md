# Innovation School Presentations

This folder contains presentation material for the Mass-Affluent Wealth Advice Innovation Challenge.

## 1. Team alignment deck

- `innovation-challenge-team-alignment.pptx` - editable PowerPoint
- `innovation-challenge-team-alignment.pdf` - shareable PDF

Purpose: align the team on the challenge, customer, current evidence, market examples, possible journey gaps and open questions before choosing a solution.

Source: `tools/create_innovation_deck.js`.

## 2. Design Thinking roadmap draft

- `design-thinking-roadmap-draft.pptx` - editable PowerPoint
- `design-thinking-roadmap-draft.pdf` - shareable PDF

Purpose: show how the team can work through Understand -> Empathize & Synthesize -> Ideate -> Prototype & Test -> Business Case & Pitch. The central rule is to agree on the customer problem and why it matters before selecting a solution in the Ideate phase.

The roadmap also uses this positioning statement:

> How can we give customers with higher income or savings a level of guidance that is more valuable than generic retail guidance, but still cheaper and more scalable than full private banking?

Source: `tools/create_design_thinking_roadmap.js`.

Both decks are preparation material for discussion, not records of final team decisions.
