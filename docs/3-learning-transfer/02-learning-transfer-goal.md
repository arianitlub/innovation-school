## What the official material requires (Day 4 deck, slides on the Goal; template in `official-materials/Tasks/`)

- The Goal is **not a one-time activity**: a concrete, **recurring** change in how I work. Strong Goal = relevant, concrete, recurring, shared, measurable. **Learning Transfer Goal ≠ Homework.**
- The template asks which **Innovation School learning** (methods, mindset, facilitation, culture) I integrate. So the *learning* is Design Thinking and facilitation; **AI maturity is the topic** I apply it to.
- Template boxes: **My goal · Value for others · Signs of progress · Support needed · First step.** Formula: *"I will [apply/share what] in [which work context] starting [when] and [how often], to contribute to [intended change or benefit]."*
- Homework = first step of the Goal and its evidence (see [04-practical-workshop.md](04-practical-workshop.md)). Hand-in format of the homework is not confirmed **[ask Christine]**.
- Audience: engineers who already code with AI and use skills and agents. The question is how to move up the official RBI levels, not how to learn AI.

## The Goal in template format (draft text)

**My goal**
> I will facilitate a monthly 60-minute Innovation Hour with my team of about 20 engineers and testers, starting in November 2026 (the first session is my homework workshop), using Design Thinking methods (Empathize, Define, Ideate, a small experiment) to move the team step by step up the official RBI AI maturity levels (Dictating → Commanding → Regulating → Orchestrating → Supervising), so that AI know-how is shared and not kept by a few people.

**Value for others**
- Engineers who are further ahead coach colleagues in buddy pairs on real tickets.
- Everyone takes part in finding the barriers and choosing the next experiment.
- What works becomes a reusable asset (rule, skill, instruction) in a shared place.

**Signs of progress (proposals, by 15.12.2026)**

| Measure | Proposed target |
|---|---|
| Baseline of the team's level (anonymous self-placement, repeated each cycle) | First baseline in the first session |
| Innovation Hours held | 2 (month 1 = homework, month 2 = experiment review) |
| Buddy pairs completed | 4–6 |
| Reusable assets that someone else actually reused | At least 3 |
| People who moved up one level on one practice (self-reported with an example) | First signals, no fixed number |

I do not promise "the whole team at level 4 by 15.12".

**Support needed**
- From the line manager: a protected slot (60–90 min a month) and agreement to use real tickets.
- From advanced colleagues: some time as buddies.
- From the network or the AI team: clarity on the AI/compliance rules and the official RBI blueprint and kit.

**First step**
- By **13.11.2026**: share the workshop findings with the participants and ask what they would try.
- By **20.11.2026**: choose one barrier and one small experiment with an owner and a date.

## Monthly cycle (how it runs)

1. **Innovation Hour (60 min):** look at the baseline, pick one barrier, design one small experiment (Design Thinking).
2. **Buddy pairs:** one advanced user with one colleague for about 4 weeks on a real ticket.
3. **Starter kit:** what works is saved as a reusable instruction, skill or rule.
4. **Review after 2–4 weeks:** continue, improve, scale or stop.

Cheapest first experiment (from the RBI guide, L2→L3): turn every repeated AI correction into a shared rule.

## Ambassador check (Day 4 criteria)

Real situation ✔ (my team) · method applied ✔ (Design Thinking) · one concrete improvement (the month-2 experiment) · evidence artifact (maturity map, barriers, 1-page summary) · shown to at least one other person (participants, line manager) · own context ✔ · small and practical ✔.
