## What "good" looks like by 15.12 (realistic)

- Workshop held, findings documented (maturity map, top 3 barriers, assumptions, one experiment).
- Goal sent on time and agreed with the line manager.
- At least one follow-up session or experiment held, with a small result.
- A short honest review: what worked, what did not, what continues.

No big result is required.

## Risks to the plan

| Risk | Handling |
|---|---|
| Not enough time before 03.11 for the workshop | Shorter session (60–90 min), 4 people minimum. Or present the Goal with a firm date for the workshop |

| Team slides and Goal both due on 03.11 12:00 | Finish the Goal draft on 30.10, review on 02.11 |
| Goal v2 (buddy sessions) too big for six weeks | Keep the rhythm, cut the scope: 2 pairs, 1 live session, 1 reused asset |
