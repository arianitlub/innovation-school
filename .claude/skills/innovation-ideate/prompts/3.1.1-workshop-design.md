# PROMPT 3.1.1: Ideation Workshop Design & Facilitation Plan

Use Case: Planning an effective ideation workshop

```text
I need to facilitate an ideation workshop to generate solutions for: "[INSERT YOUR CHALLENGE]"

Workshop context:
- Duration: [e.g., 3 hours]
- Participants: [e.g., 12-15 people from relevant departments/teams]
- Goal: Generate diverse, creative solutions to address top pain points or opportunities
- Key insights from research: [Summarize top 3-5 pain points, needs, or opportunities]

Create a comprehensive workshop plan:

1. WORKSHOP OBJECTIVES & SUCCESS CRITERIA
   - What should we accomplish?
   - How will we know the workshop was successful?
   - What outcomes/deliverables?

2. PRE-WORKSHOP PREPARATION
   What to send participants in advance:
   - Research summary (customer insights, personas, pain points)
   - Workshop agenda and objectives
   - Pre-reading materials
   - Pre-work (if any - e.g., "think of 3 ideas beforehand")

3. WORKSHOP AGENDA WITH TIMING
   Design a 3-hour agenda with detailed timing:

   PART 1: INTRODUCTION & ALIGNMENT (20-30 minutes)
   - Welcome and objectives
   - Review of research insights (personas, journey, pain points)
   - Ensure shared understanding of problem
   - Set rules for ideation (no judgment, build on ideas, wild ideas welcome, quantity over quality)

   PART 2: DIVERGENT THINKING - IDEA GENERATION (60-90 minutes)
   Multiple ideation rounds using different techniques:

   Round 1: [Technique name - e.g., "Brainstorming"]
   - How it works
   - Duration
   - Format (individual, pairs, groups)
   - Facilitation tips

   Round 2: [Different technique - e.g., "SCAMPER method"]
   - How it works
   - Duration
   - Format

   Round 3: [Another technique - e.g., "What Would X Do?" - X = Apple, Amazon, etc.]
   - How it works
   - Duration

   BREAK (10-15 minutes)

   PART 3: CONVERGENT THINKING - CLUSTERING & SELECTION (40-60 minutes)
   - Cluster similar ideas into themes
   - Dot voting or other selection method
   - Group discussion of top ideas
   - Refine and combine ideas

   PART 4: IDEA DEVELOPMENT (20-30 minutes)
   - Take top 3-5 ideas
   - Flesh out with more detail
   - Consider feasibility, impact, customer value
   - Identify quick wins vs. bigger bets

   PART 5: WRAP-UP & NEXT STEPS (10-15 minutes)
   - Recap what was generated
   - Define next steps (who will do what)
   - Thank participants
   - Collect feedback on workshop

4. IDEATION TECHNIQUES TO USE

   Provide detailed instructions for 5-7 different techniques:

   A. CLASSIC BRAINSTORMING
   - How to facilitate
   - Rules to emphasize
   - How to capture ideas

   B. BRAINWRITING (6-3-5 or similar)
   - Step-by-step process
   - Template/worksheet

   C. SCAMPER METHOD
   - Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to other uses, Eliminate, Reverse
   - How to apply to my challenge area

   D. "HOW MIGHT WE" QUESTIONS
   - How to frame pain points as HMW questions
   - Examples relevant to my challenge

   E. ANALOGIES & CROSS-INDUSTRY INSPIRATION
   - "What would [successful companies/leaders] do?"
   - How to adapt lessons from other industries or contexts

   F. REVERSE BRAINSTORMING
   - "How might we make this even worse?"
   - Then flip to positive solutions

   G. CRAZY 8'S (Rapid Ideation)
   - 8 ideas in 8 minutes
   - How it works

5. FACILITATION BEST PRACTICES
   - How to create psychological safety
   - How to encourage quiet participants
   - How to manage dominant voices
   - How to keep energy high
   - How to prevent premature judgment or criticism
   - How to capture all ideas effectively

6. MATERIALS & LOGISTICS
   What you need:
   - Physical materials (sticky notes, markers, flip charts, dot stickers)
   - Digital tools (Miro, Mural, virtual whiteboard)
   - Templates or worksheets
   - Timer
   - Room setup (if in-person)

7. IDEA CAPTURE & DOCUMENTATION
   - Format for recording ideas
   - Who takes notes
   - How to organize during workshop
   - How to document after workshop

8. SELECTION & PRIORITIZATION METHODS
   - Dot voting (how many dots per person, what criteria)
   - Impact vs. Effort matrix
   - Feasibility vs. Desirability scoring
   - Group discussion protocols

9. VIRTUAL WORKSHOP ADAPTATIONS
   If workshop is virtual:
   - How to adapt each activity for Zoom/Teams + Miro
   - How to maintain energy remotely
   - Breakout room strategies
   - Digital tool tips

10. FOLLOW-UP & ACTION ITEMS
    After workshop:
    - How to synthesize all ideas
    - How to share outcomes with participants
    - How to move ideas forward
    - Who decides next steps

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Complete workshop facilitation guide with:
- Detailed agenda with timing
- Step-by-step instructions for each ideation technique
- Facilitation scripts and talking points
- Templates and worksheets
- Materials checklist
- Virtual adaptation notes
```
