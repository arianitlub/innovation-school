# PROMPT 5.3.1: Pitch Review & Refinement

Use Case: Getting feedback to strengthen your pitch

```text
I have created a pitch deck and business case. I need help reviewing and strengthening it before presenting to stakeholders.

MY PITCH CONTENT:
[Paste or summarize your key slides and business case]

AUDIENCE:
[Who will I present to? What are their priorities?]

Help me review and improve:

1. MESSAGE CLARITY CHECK
   Review each key message:
   - Is the problem clear and compelling?
   - Is the solution easy to understand?
   - Is the value proposition obvious?
   - Are benefits quantified and believable?
   - Is the ask clear and specific?

   For each message, assess:
   - Clarity (1-10): How clear is it?
   - Impact (1-10): How compelling is it?
   - Evidence (Strong/Medium/Weak): How well-supported?

   Suggest improvements for weak areas.

2. AUDIENCE ALIGNMENT
   For my specific audience:
   - What do they care about most?
   - What language/framing will resonate?
   - What objections will they have?
   - What evidence will they find most convincing?
   - What's missing that they'll ask about?

3. NARRATIVE FLOW CHECK
   Assess story arc:
   - Does it follow logical progression?
   - Are transitions smooth?
   - Does it build momentum?
   - Does it address "so what?" at each step?
   - Is pacing right? (not too rushed or too slow)

   Identify weak points in flow and suggest fixes.

4. DATA & EVIDENCE REVIEW
   Check all quantified claims:
   - Are assumptions clearly stated?
   - Are calculations reasonable?
   - Is data source credible?
   - Are comparisons fair?
   - Are projections realistic (not too optimistic or pessimistic)?

   Flag any claims that need stronger evidence or clearer assumptions.

5. VISUAL & DESIGN FEEDBACK
   For described visuals:
   - Are charts appropriate for data type?
   - Will visualizations be easy to understand?
   - Is information hierarchy clear?
   - Are there too many or too few visuals?

   Suggest improvements for impact.

6. OBJECTION HANDLING
   Identify potential objections:
   - Technical feasibility concerns
   - ROI skepticism
   - Implementation risk worries
   - Customer adoption doubts
   - Competitive response concerns
   - Resource availability questions

   For each, provide:
   - Objection
   - Where to address proactively (in which slide)
   - Response strategy
   - Supporting evidence needed

7. IMPACT MAXIMIZATION
   How to make it more compelling:
   - Stronger customer stories/quotes
   - More vivid problem illustration
   - Clearer competitive context
   - Sharper differentiation
   - More confident closing

8. RISK MITIGATION NARRATIVE
   - Are risks acknowledged appropriately?
   - Do mitigation strategies sound credible?
   - Is overall confidence level right?

9. CALL TO ACTION STRENGTH
   - Is the ask clear and specific?
   - Is it appropriately sized for this stage?
   - Is urgency communicated?
   - Are next steps defined?

10. PRACTICE & DELIVERY TIPS
    - Which slides are most important to nail?
    - Where to slow down for emphasis?
    - Where to engage audience (ask questions)?
    - How to handle time constraints (what to cut if needed)?

11. CONFIDENCE BUILDERS
    What to emphasize:
    - Strength of customer validation
    - Quality of research process
    - Rigor of business case
    - Team capability
    - Strategic importance

12. RED FLAG CHECK
    Identify potential concerns:
    - Claims that sound too good to be true?
    - Missing critical information?
    - Logical gaps or inconsistencies?
    - Unrealistic timelines or costs?

13. BENCHMARK & COMPARISON
    How does this compare to successful pitches?
    - What are best practices we're following?
    - What are we missing?
    - Industry standards for innovation proposals?

14. FINAL POLISH
    Last refinements:
    - Headline optimization (catchier, clearer)
    - Quote selection (most impactful)
    - Slide title improvements
    - Memorable phrases or taglines
    - Strong opening and closing statements

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Review report with:
- Overall assessment (strengths and weaknesses)
- Specific improvement recommendations (prioritized)
- Revised key messages where needed
- Objection handling guide
- Delivery tips
- Confidence check (ready to present? What's still missing?)
```
