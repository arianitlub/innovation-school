# PROMPT 5.1.1: Business Case Development Framework

Use Case: Creating comprehensive business case for your solution

```text
I need to build a business case for my validated solution to secure funding and executive approval.

MY SOLUTION:
[DESCRIBE YOUR VALIDATED SOLUTION]

MY CHALLENGE CONTEXT:
- Innovation challenge: [YOUR CHALLENGE]
- Target customers/users: [WHO IS AFFECTED]
- Main problem solved: [KEY PAIN POINTS ADDRESSED]
- Expected impact: [WHAT YOU EXPECT TO IMPROVE]

WHAT DATA I HAVE (if available):
[LIST ANY METRICS OR DATA YOU HAVE - e.g., current performance metrics, customer feedback, cost data]

Help me build a compelling business case:

PART 1: HELP ME IDENTIFY WHAT FINANCIAL METRICS MATTER

Before I start calculating, help me understand:
- For MY specific challenge, what financial metrics should I focus on?
- What costs does the current problem create? (What should I quantify?)
- What value would the solution create? (What benefits should I measure?)
- What metrics will executives care about most for this type of initiative?
- What's realistic to quantify vs. what should stay qualitative?

Common metric categories to consider (suggest which apply to my challenge):
- Customer/User-related: retention, satisfaction, adoption, completion rates
- Revenue: direct revenue impact, sales conversion, deal size, growth
- Cost: operational costs, support costs, efficiency gains, waste reduction
- Quality: error reduction, rework costs, defect rates, accuracy improvements
- Time: cycle time reduction, faster processing, time savings
- Risk: compliance costs avoided, reputation protection, error reduction
- Strategic: market position, competitive advantage, innovation capability
- Employee: productivity, retention, satisfaction, capacity

PART 2: GUIDE ME ON QUANTIFICATION

Help me build a compelling business case:

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
   One-page overview including:
   - Problem statement (what's the business issue)
   - Proposed solution (what we're building)
   - Expected benefits (quantified outcomes)
   - Investment required (cost estimate)
   - ROI projection (payback period)
   - Recommendation (what we're asking for)

2. PROBLEM QUANTIFICATION
   Help me quantify the financial impact of the current problem:

   Guide me on how to calculate costs in categories relevant to my challenge, such as:
   - Inefficiency costs (wasted time × cost per hour)
   - Error/rework costs (error rate × cost to fix)
   - Lost opportunity costs (deals/transactions lost × value)
   - Support or overhead costs (volume × cost per incident)
   - Compliance or risk costs (incidents × cost per incident)
   - Customer/user satisfaction impact (correlation to business metrics)

   For each category:
   - Help me identify what data I need
   - Suggest reasonable estimation approaches if data isn't available
   - Provide formulas or calculation methods
   - Flag assumptions that need validation

   Total Annual Cost of Problem: [€X]

3. SOLUTION BENEFITS - QUANTIFIED
   Help me calculate expected financial benefits:

   Guide me on quantifying benefits relevant to my challenge, such as:
   - Efficiency gains (time saved × cost per hour × volume)
   - Quality improvements (errors reduced × cost per error)
   - Revenue impact (increased conversion/completion × transaction value)
   - Cost reductions (reduced support/overhead × unit cost)
   - Risk reduction (incidents avoided × cost per incident)
   - Capacity gains (capacity freed up × value per unit capacity)

   For each benefit:
   - Help me estimate realistic improvement percentages
   - Provide calculation formulas
   - Suggest conservative, expected, and optimistic scenarios
   - Help me validate assumptions

   Total Annual Benefit: [€X]

4. IMPLEMENTATION COSTS
   One-time costs:
   - Technology/platform: [€X]
   - Design and UX: [€X]
   - Development: [€X]
   - Integration: [€X]
   - Testing and QA: [€X]
   - Project management: [€X]
   - Change management and training: [€X]
   Total One-Time Cost: [€X]

   Ongoing costs (annual):
   - Maintenance and support: [€X]
   - Infrastructure/hosting: [€X]
   - Continuous improvement: [€X]
   Total Annual Cost: [€X]

5. FINANCIAL PROJECTIONS (3-year)
   Year 1:
   - Investment: [€X]
   - Partial year benefits (rollout phase): [€Y]
   - Net: [€Z]

   Year 2:
   - Ongoing costs: [€X]
   - Full year benefits: [€Y]
   - Net: [€Z]

   Year 3:
   - Ongoing costs: [€X]
   - Full year benefits (with scaling): [€Y]
   - Net: [€Z]

   Cumulative 3-Year Net Benefit: [€X]

6. ROI CALCULATION
   - Total investment (one-time + 3 years ongoing): [€X]
   - Total benefits (3 years): [€Y]
   - Net benefit: [€Z]
   - ROI: [(Benefits - Costs) / Costs] × 100 = [X%]
   - Payback period: [X months]

7. NON-FINANCIAL BENEFITS
   Strategic and qualitative value:
   - Improved user/customer experience and satisfaction
   - Enhanced reputation or brand value
   - Competitive differentiation
   - Foundation for future digital innovations
   - Regulatory compliance improvement
   - Employee efficiency and satisfaction
   - Data and insights capability

8. RISKS & MITIGATION
   Key risks:
   - Technology implementation risk → Mitigation: [strategy]
   - Customer adoption risk → Mitigation: [strategy]
   - Regulatory/compliance risk → Mitigation: [strategy]
   - Resource availability risk → Mitigation: [strategy]
   - Competitive response risk → Mitigation: [strategy]

9. ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED
   "Do nothing" scenario:
   - What happens if we don't invest?
   - Continuing cost of problem: [€X annually]
   - Competitive risk increasing

   Alternative approaches:
   - [Alternative 1]: Why not chosen?
   - [Alternative 2]: Why not chosen?

10. IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP
    Phase 1 (Months 1-3): [What's built]
    Phase 2 (Months 4-6): [What's built]
    Phase 3 (Months 7-12): [What's built]

    Key milestones and benefits realization timeline

11. SUCCESS METRICS & KPIs
    How we'll measure success:
    - Customer satisfaction: NPS target [X]
    - Retention: Churn reduction target [X%]
    - Digital adoption: Usage target [X%]
    - Financial: Cost savings target [€X]
    - Efficiency: Support ticket reduction [X%]

    Tracking and reporting plan

12. STAKEHOLDER ANALYSIS
    Who benefits:
    - Customers: Better experience
    - Business: Retention and efficiency
    - IT: Modern platform
    - Support: Reduced workload
    - Leadership: Competitive advantage

13. DECISION FRAMEWORK
    Investment decision criteria:
    - Strategic fit: [High/Medium/Low]
    - Financial return: [ROI X%]
    - Implementation risk: [High/Medium/Low]
    - Time to value: [X months]
    - Customer impact: [High/Medium/Low]

    Recommendation matrix scoring

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Complete business case document with:
- Executive summary (1 page)
- Problem quantification with calculations
- Benefit quantification with assumptions
- Cost breakdown
- 3-year financial projections
- ROI and payback period
- Risk assessment
- Recommendation

Include tables, charts descriptions, and clear section headers.
Maximum 2000 words for main content.
```
