# PROMPT 5.2.1: Pitch Deck Structure & Content

Use Case: Creating a persuasive pitch presentation for stakeholders

```text
I need to create a pitch deck to present my innovation project to [AUDIENCE - e.g., "Executive leadership committee", "Innovation board", "Steering committee"].

CONTEXT:
- Presentation time: [e.g., 20 minutes + 10 minutes Q&A]
- Audience background: [e.g., "Senior executives, mix of technical and business backgrounds"]
- What they care about: [e.g., "ROI, customer retention, competitive differentiation"]
- Decision we need: [e.g., "Approval to proceed with development and budget allocation of €X"]

MY SOLUTION & RESULTS:
- Solution: [DESCRIBE]
- Customer validation: [e.g., "Tested with 12 customers, 85% said they would use it"]
- Expected impact: [KEY METRICS]
- Investment needed: [€X]

Help me create a compelling pitch deck:

1. PITCH STRUCTURE (Slide-by-Slide)

SLIDE 1: TITLE
- Title: [Project name]
- Subtitle: [One-sentence value proposition]
- Your name and team
- Date

- What's the business problem?
- Why does it matter? (quantify impact)
- Emotional hook: Show customer pain
- Visual: Declining satisfaction chart or customer journey pain points

[Draft key messages and data points for this slide]

SLIDE 3: THE OPPORTUNITY
- Size of the problem (€X at risk, X customers affected)
- Strategic importance
- Competitive context (we're falling behind competitors)
- Visual: Market comparison or competitive analysis

[Draft key messages]

SLIDE 4: CUSTOMER INSIGHTS
- "We talked to [X users/customers/stakeholders] and learned..."
- Key pain points discovered
- Powerful customer quotes (2-3)
- Visual: Persona or journey map highlighting issues

Suggested content:
[Draft key messages and select quotes]

SLIDE 5: CUSTOMER NEEDS
- What do customers actually need?
- Prioritized needs from research
- "Job to be done"

Suggested content:
[Draft key needs statements]

- How it addresses customer needs
- Key features/capabilities (3-5 bullets)
- Visual: Solution diagram or storyboard

Suggested content:
[Draft solution description]

- Step-by-step or scenario
- Show prototype screenshots/mockups
- Visual: User flow or storyboard frames

Suggested content:
[Draft user scenario]

SLIDE 8: CUSTOMER VALIDATION
- "We tested with X customers"
- Key test results (satisfaction scores, task completion, etc.)
- Positive feedback quotes
- Customer enthusiasm indicators
- Visual: Test results dashboard

Suggested content:
[Draft validation summary with metrics]

SLIDE 9: THE BENEFITS - CUSTOMERS
- How customers benefit
- Better experience (qualitative)
- Time saved, easier, less frustration
- Visual: Before/after comparison

Suggested content:
[Draft customer benefit statements]

SLIDE 10: THE BENEFITS - BUSINESS
- Quantified business impact
- Churn reduction: X%
- Cost savings: €X
- Revenue impact: €X
- NPS improvement: X points
- Visual: Benefits waterfall chart

Suggested content:
[Draft business benefits with numbers]

SLIDE 11: FINANCIAL CASE
- Investment required: €X
- Expected annual benefit: €Y
- ROI: X%
- Payback period: X months
- 3-year projection
- Visual: ROI chart or cumulative benefit graph

Suggested content:
[Draft financial summary]

SLIDE 12: IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
- Timeline: X months
- Key phases and milestones
- Quick wins and long-term goals
- Resource requirements
- Visual: Roadmap timeline

Suggested content:
[Draft implementation phases]

SLIDE 13: SUCCESS METRICS
- How we'll measure success
- KPIs and targets
- Tracking and reporting plan
- Visual: Scorecard or dashboard mockup

Suggested content:
[Draft metrics and targets]

SLIDE 14: RISKS & MITIGATION
- Key risks identified
- Mitigation strategies for each
- Confidence level
- Visual: Risk matrix

Suggested content:
[Draft risk summary]

SLIDE 15: COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE
- How this differentiates us
- What competitors do (or don't do)
- Why customers will choose us
- Visual: Competitive positioning map

Suggested content:
[Draft differentiation points]

SLIDE 16: THE ASK
- What we need to proceed
- Budget approval: €X
- Team resources: X people
- Executive sponsorship
- Timeline: Start date and duration
- Visual: Clear, bold ask

Suggested content:
[Draft specific requests]

SLIDE 17: CLOSING / CALL TO ACTION
- Recap: Problem → Solution → Impact
- Urgency: Why now?
- Vision: Where this takes us
- Confident recommendation
- Visual: Inspiring future state image

Suggested content:
[Draft closing message]

2. STORYTELLING & NARRATIVE FLOW
   Connect slides with narrative arc:
   - Hook (problem that matters)
   - Journey (how we got here, what we learned)
   - Solution (the answer)
   - Validation (proof it works)
   - Vision (exciting future)
   - Ask (what we need)

3. DESIGN PRINCIPLES
   - One key message per slide
   - Minimal text (headlines + bullets, not paragraphs)
   - Strong visuals (charts, images, diagrams)
   - Consistent branding
   - Data visualization best practices
   - Readability (font size, contrast)

4. SPEAKER NOTES
   For each slide, provide:
   - Opening statement (how to introduce slide)
   - Key points to emphasize
   - Transitions to next slide
   - Anticipated questions and answers

5. HANDLING Q&A
   Prepare for likely questions:
   - "What if customers don't adopt it?"
   - "Why not buy existing solution instead of build?"
   - "What about regulatory concerns?"
   - "How confident are you in ROI estimates?"
   - "What happens if this fails?"
   - [Add 5-10 more likely questions with suggested answers]

6. BACKUP SLIDES
   Additional detail slides (not in main deck):
   - Detailed financial model
   - Full test results
   - Technical architecture
   - Detailed roadmap
   - Team bios

7. PRESENTATION TIPS
   - Practice timing (don't rush or run over)
   - Engage with eye contact
   - Use confident, enthusiastic tone
   - Tell stories, not just data
   - Pause for impact
   - Handle objections gracefully

OUTPUT FORMAT:
- Slide-by-slide outline with suggested content
- Narrative flow and transitions
- Speaker notes for each slide
- Q&A preparation guide
- Design and visual recommendations

For each slide, provide:
- Slide title
- Key message (headline)
- Supporting points (bullets)
- Visual suggestion (what chart/image)
- Speaker notes (what to say)
```
