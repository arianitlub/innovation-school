## How to use

**Evidence:** fill placeholders from [`../EVIDENCE.md`](../EVIDENCE.md), the shared map of current interviews (C1, C2, UX, experts), synthesis, decisions and research. Read it first. Customer evidence ranks above expert views, and both above desk research.

1. Pick the prompt and read its file.
2. Fill placeholders from `docs/challenges/00-overview.md`, the stage files 03–05 and their `-rbi/` folders (needs, quotes from C1/C2, chosen solution My Wealth, test results), the Problem One-Pager `03-empathize-rbi/2.4-problem-one-pager.md`, and market facts in `02-understand-rbi/`. Ask for anything missing, especially the audience and the time limit.
3. Every euro figure, percentage and ROI must show its assumption and source. If we have no internal data, label numbers as illustrative scenarios (conservative / expected / optimistic), not forecasts. Never present the strategy paper as RBI data.
4. The pitch must not claim validation we did not do: use the real number of interviews and tests.
5. Existing deck generators are in `tools/` (see `docs/presentation/README.md`, incl. `my-wealth-proposed-solution.pdf`); reuse them if the user wants a PDF. Save full outputs to `docs/challenges/06-pitch-rbi/<prompt>.md` and the team version to `docs/challenges/06-pitch.md`.
