# PROMPT 2.3.2: Persona Development from Research

Use Case: Creating research-based personas from interview insights

```text
Based on my customer interviews, I need to create 2-4 personas that represent distinct user types with different needs and behaviors regarding [your challenge area].

Here are my key findings from interviews:

COMMON CHARACTERISTICS OBSERVED:
[List demographic patterns, tech proficiency levels, industry relationship types you observed]

COMMON BEHAVIORS & USAGE PATTERNS:
[List how different groups use our digital services/channels, when, how often, what for]

DISTINCT PAIN POINTS BY GROUP:
[List different frustrations mentioned by different customer types]

DISTINCT NEEDS BY GROUP:
[List different priorities and desires]

NOTABLE QUOTES:
[Include 5-10 memorable quotes that represent different perspectives]

Help me create research-based personas:

1. IDENTIFY DISTINCT PERSONA TYPES
   - How many personas should I create? (typically 3-4 for this scope)
   - What are the KEY DIFFERENTIATING FACTORS?
     * Not just demographics, but behaviors, needs, goals, pain points
   - What makes each persona meaningfully different from others?
   - Ensure personas cover main user groups but avoid over-segmentation

2. PERSONA STRUCTURE
   For each persona, create a comprehensive profile:

   A. PERSONA SNAPSHOT
   - Name (realistic, memorable)
   - Age
   - Occupation
   - Location (urban/suburban/rural if relevant)
   - One-sentence description

   B. DEMOGRAPHIC & BACKGROUND
   - Industry relationship (products used, account tenure)
   - Life situation (family status, financial goals)
   - Digital proficiency level (tech-savvy, comfortable, struggling)

   C. DIGITAL SERVICE USAGE BEHAVIOR
   - Primary channel (system, website, in-person)
   - Frequency of use (daily, weekly, monthly, rarely)
   - Main use cases (checking balance, paying bills, transfers, support, etc.)
   - Device preferences (smartphone, tablet, desktop)
   - When they use our digital services/channels (on-the-go, at home, at work)

   D. GOALS & MOTIVATIONS
   - What are they trying to accomplish with our digital services/channels?
   - What would make their experience better?
   - What outcomes matter most to them?
   - Frame as: "I want to [goal] so that [benefit]"

   E. PAIN POINTS & FRUSTRATIONS
   - Top 3-5 specific frustrations (prioritized)
   - What causes them stress or anxiety?
   - What makes them consider leaving?
   - Include emotional aspects (fear, confusion, annoyance)

   F. NEEDS & PRIORITIES
   - What do they need most from our digital services/channels?
   - What trade-offs matter to them? (speed vs. security, simplicity vs. features, etc.)
   - What would delight them?

   G. BEHAVIORS & WORKAROUNDS
   - How do they currently cope with problems?
   - What alternatives do they use?
   - When do they abandon digital and call/visit branch?

   H. REPRESENTATIVE QUOTE
   - Select one powerful quote from interviews that captures this persona
   - Should be authentic, in customer's voice

   I. A DAY IN THEIR LIFE (Scenario)
   - Brief narrative showing how this persona interacts with our digital services/channels
   - Include a specific situation that highlights their needs and pain points
   - Example: "Maria opens the system on her commute to check if her paycheck arrived..."

   J. TECHNOLOGY ATTITUDE
   - How do they feel about technology in general?
   - What other apps/services do they use and love?
   - What sets their expectations?

   K. COMPETITIVE PERSPECTIVE
   - Have they used other companies' digital services?
   - What do they compare us to?
   - What would make them switch?

3. PERSONA COMPARISON MATRIX
   Create a table comparing personas across key dimensions:
   - Digital proficiency
   - Usage frequency
   - Primary pain points
   - Primary needs
   - Channel preference
   - Emotional drivers (what matters most emotionally)

4. DESIGN IMPLICATIONS PER PERSONA
   - What does each persona need from our solution?
   - How might solutions differ for each persona?
   - Are there conflicts between personas' needs?
   - Who should we prioritize and why?

5. VALIDATION & QUALITY CHECK
   - Do these personas cover most participants from interviews?
   - Are they distinct enough to be useful?
   - Are they based on real research patterns (not stereotypes)?
   - Do they avoid edge cases or extreme outliers?
   - Are they actionable (can designers/developers use them)?

OUTPUT FORMAT:
2-4 complete persona profiles (detailed descriptions) plus comparison matrix.
Each persona should feel like a real person, grounded in actual research data.
Include specific examples and quotes from interviews to bring personas to life.
Maximum 500 words per persona.
```
