# PROMPT 2.3.3: Customer Journey Mapping

Use Case: Creating current-state journey maps showing pain points and opportunities

```text
Based on my customer research, I need to create a detailed customer journey map for [your challenge area] that shows where customers experience friction and satisfaction.

Journey to map: [SPECIFY - e.g., "Onboarding and first-time usage" or "Completing a complex process" or "Overall [your challenge area]"]

Data from research:
[Paste key findings about customer behaviors, pain points, emotions at different stages]

Help me structure a comprehensive journey map:

1. JOURNEY PHASES/STAGES
   Break the journey into logical stages:
   - What are the main phases customers go through?
   - For our digital services/channels onboarding example:
     * Awareness/Discovery
     * Download/Registration
     * Identity Verification
     * First Login
     * Feature Exploration
     * First Transaction
     * Ongoing Usage
   - For my specific journey, what stages make sense?

2. FOR EACH STAGE, MAP THE FOLLOWING:

   A. CUSTOMER ACTIONS
   - What does the customer do in this stage?
   - What specific steps or tasks?
   - What channels/touchpoints are involved?

   B. CUSTOMER THOUGHTS
   - What is the customer thinking?
   - What questions do they have?
   - What are they wondering or worrying about?
   - Base this on interview quotes where possible

   C. CUSTOMER EMOTIONS
   - How do they feel at this stage?
   - Emotion scale or indicators (happy, neutral, frustrated, anxious, confused, satisfied)
   - Visualize as emotion curve (ups and downs)

   D. PAIN POINTS & FRICTION
   - What specific problems occur here?
   - What causes frustration or abandonment?
   - How severe/frequent is each pain point?
   - Include quotes from interviews

   E. MOMENTS OF DELIGHT
   - What works well?
   - Where are customers satisfied?
   - What positive surprises occur?

   F. TOUCHPOINTS/CHANNELS
   - What systems/interfaces are involved? (system, website, SMS, email, chatbot, call center)
   - How do channels interconnect?

   G. BEHIND THE SCENES (Backstage)
   - What happens behind the scenes that customer doesn't see?
   - What systems, processes, teams are involved?
   - Where do delays or errors originate?

   H. OPPORTUNITIES
   - Based on pain points, what could be improved?
   - Quick wins vs. long-term fixes
   - What would reduce friction here?

3. JOURNEY MAP VISUALIZATION FORMAT

   Suggest how to visualize this (format for PowerPoint or Miro):
   - Horizontal timeline across top (stages)
   - Swim lanes for each dimension (actions, thoughts, emotions, etc.)
   - Use icons, colors, emotion curves
   - Highlight pain points in red, delights in green
   - Include representative quotes in callouts

4. MULTIPLE JOURNEY VARIANTS (If Needed)

   Should I create separate journey maps for different personas?
   - How do journeys differ by persona?
   - Where do paths diverge?
   - What's common vs. unique?

5. CURRENT STATE vs. FUTURE STATE

   This is current-state mapping (as-is):
   - What does the journey look like today?
   - Where are the problems?

   Later, I'll need future-state (to-be):
   - What should the journey look like after improvements?
   - How to show transition from current to future?

6. PRIORITIZATION OF PAIN POINTS

   Across the journey:
   - Which pain points are most critical?
   - What are "moments of truth" that make-or-break customer satisfaction?
   - Use scoring: Impact (high/med/low) × Frequency (high/med/low)

7. QUANTITATIVE OVERLAY

   If I have data, how to include:
   - Drop-off rates at each stage
   - Time spent in each stage
   - Support ticket volumes per stage
   - Satisfaction scores per stage

8. INSIGHTS & PATTERNS

   Looking at the full journey:
   - Where are the biggest friction points?
   - Where does emotion dip most severely?
   - Are problems concentrated in certain stages?
   - What systemic issues span multiple stages?
   - Where are quick-win opportunities?

9. CROSS-CHANNEL INCONSISTENCIES

   - Where does experience break when customers switch channels?
   - Example: start on system, continue on web - what's lost?
   - Where is context not preserved?

10. JOURNEY MAP NARRATIVE

    Create a written narrative that accompanies the visual:
    - Tell the story of a typical customer going through this journey
    - Use one of my personas as the protagonist
    - Include specific examples and quotes
    - Highlight key pain points and emotional moments
    - 300-500 words

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Detailed journey map structure with:
- Stage-by-stage breakdown
- All dimensions mapped per stage
- Visualization guidance (how to format in PowerPoint/Miro)
- Supporting narrative
- Prioritized list of improvement opportunities

Provide in table format that can be easily transferred to visual design tool.
```
