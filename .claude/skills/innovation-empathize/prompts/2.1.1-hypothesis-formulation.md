# PROMPT 2.1.1: Hypothesis Formulation

Use Case: Creating testable hypotheses about user behavior and pain points

```text
I am working on: "[INSERT YOUR CHALLENGE]"

Based on initial data analysis, I need to formulate hypotheses to test through interviews with users/customers/stakeholders.

What I know from data:
[INSERT YOUR KEY DATA FINDINGS - Examples:]
- "Processing time increased from 2 days to 5 days over 6 months"
- "30% of users cite 'confusing process' as their main complaint"
- "Drop-off rate is 45% at step 3 of the workflow"
- "Support requests increased 30% related to [specific issue]"
- "Satisfaction scores for [area] are 20 points lower than benchmark"

What I'm uncertain about:
[INSERT YOUR KEY UNCERTAINTIES - Examples:]
- WHY users find this process frustrating (specific causes)
- Which pain points matter most to users
- Whether issues are process problems, tool problems, or communication gaps
- How needs differ by user segment or situation
- What would make users more satisfied or successful

Help me formulate testable hypotheses:

1. PROBLEM HYPOTHESES
   Convert my uncertainties into specific, testable hypotheses about problems:
   - Format: "We believe that [specific user group] experiences [specific problem] when [specific situation] because [hypothesized reason]"
   - Example: "We believe that new employees struggle to complete onboarding paperwork because the instructions are unclear and they don't know which forms are required for their role"

   Generate 10-15 problem hypotheses covering relevant areas such as:
   - Process or workflow issues
   - Tool or system usability problems
   - Information gaps or communication failures
   - Resource or support limitations
   - Training or knowledge gaps

2. USER/CUSTOMER BEHAVIOR HYPOTHESES
   What assumptions am I making about what users want, value, or do?
   - Format: "We believe that [specific user group] prefers/values/does [specific behavior] because [hypothesized reason]"
   - Example: "We believe that experienced users skip the standard process and use workarounds because the official method is too slow for their needs"

   Generate hypotheses about:
   - Method or approach preferences
   - Priorities (what matters most to them)
   - When and how they currently handle this area
   - What drives their satisfaction or frustration
   - What influences their success or compliance

3. SEGMENTATION HYPOTHESES
   How might needs differ across user groups?
   Suggest relevant segments based on my challenge, such as:
   - By experience level (new vs. experienced)
   - By role or responsibility
   - By frequency of interaction (daily vs. occasional)
   - By location or context
   - By goals or priorities

4. MISSION-CRITICAL IDENTIFICATION
   - Which hypotheses, if wrong, would completely change our solution direction?
   - What makes each one mission-critical?
   - Mark top 5 mission-critical hypotheses

5. TESTABILITY ASSESSMENT
   For each hypothesis, suggest:
   - How could we test it in interviews? (specific questions to ask)
   - What evidence would validate it?
   - What evidence would falsify it?
   - How could we observe this in customer behavior?

6. HYPOTHESIS PRIORITIZATION
   Help me prioritize which hypotheses to test first based on:
   - Impact if true (would it significantly change our solution?)
   - Uncertainty level (how confident are we already?)
   - Feasibility to test (can we learn this from interviews?)

OUTPUT FORMAT:
List of 15-20 hypotheses organized by category, with mission-critical ones marked, and testing approach for each. Include a prioritized list of top 10 to focus on.
```
