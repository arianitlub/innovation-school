# PROMPT 2.1.3: Interview Participant Recruitment Strategy

Use Case: Planning who to interview and how to recruit them

```text
I need to recruit participants for empathy interviews about: "[INSERT YOUR CHALLENGE]"

My research goals:
[INSERT YOUR RESEARCH GOALS - Examples:]
- "Understand friction points in [process/experience]"
- "Learn why users are dissatisfied or struggling"
- "Validate hypotheses about [specific issues]"
- "Identify differences across user segments"

Help me develop a recruitment strategy:

1. SAMPLE COMPOSITION - WHO TO INTERVIEW

   How many interviews should I conduct? (recommend based on research scope)

   What mix of participants ensures diverse insights?

   Segment by EXPERIENCE/SATISFACTION LEVEL:
   - How many highly satisfied users? (to learn what works)
   - How many dissatisfied users? (to learn pain points)
   - How many neutral/middle users?
   - How many who recently stopped using or had problems?

   Segment by DEMOGRAPHICS (if relevant):
   Suggest relevant demographic splits based on my challenge, such as:
   - Age ranges (if relevant)
   - Gender diversity (if relevant)
   - Geographic spread (if relevant)
   - Other diversity dimensions

   Segment by EXPERIENCE LEVEL:
   - Expert/experienced users
   - Moderate experience
   - New or less experienced users
   - How to identify experience level?

   Segment by RELATIONSHIP/USAGE:
   Suggest relevant segments based on my challenge, such as:
   - Basic vs. advanced usage
   - Different product/service types
   - Long-tenure vs. new
   - High-frequency vs. low-frequency users

   Segment by BEHAVIOR PATTERNS:
   Suggest behavioral segments relevant to my challenge, such as:
   - Frequency of interaction (daily, weekly, occasional)
   - Primary method or channel used
   - Those who have requested support or raised issues
   - Different use cases or scenarios

   Create a recruitment matrix showing how many of each segment type

2. RECRUITMENT SOURCES - WHERE TO FIND PARTICIPANTS

   Internal sources:
   - Customer/user database (how to select)
   - Recent survey respondents (both satisfied and dissatisfied)
   - Recent complaints or issue reports
   - Support ticket filers
   - Feedback submitted through various channels

   External sources (if needed):
   - User research panels
   - Social media recruitment
   - Branch referrals

   What's the best approach for a company? (privacy, compliance considerations)

3. OUTREACH MESSAGING

   Draft outreach approaches for different channels:

   EMAIL INVITATION TEMPLATE:
   - Subject line that gets opens
   - Message that explains purpose without biasing
   - What incentive to offer? (gift card amount, contribution to charity)
   - How to make it feel important (their voice matters)
   - Clear logistics (time required, format, confidentiality)

   SMS/TEXT INVITATION (if appropriate):
   - Shorter version for mobile

   PHONE SCRIPT (if recruiting by call):
   - How to introduce and screen participants
   - How to confirm they fit recruitment criteria
   - How to schedule

   What tone to use? (formal vs. friendly, business context)

4. SCREENING CRITERIA & QUESTIONS

   How to screen participants to ensure right fit?

   Screening questions to ask:
   - Confirm they have relevant experience with the challenge area
   - Confirm recency of experience (within last X days/months?)
   - Check for segment fit (relevant demographics, role, usage level)
   - Screen out employees, competitors, professional respondents
   - Identify any accessibility needs

   Disqualification criteria (who NOT to interview and why)

5. INCENTIVE STRATEGY

   What incentive is appropriate?
   - Gift card amount (what's fair for 45-60 minute interview?)
   - What type of gift card? (Amazon, Visa prepaid, company credit?)
   - When to provide? (before, after, or split?)
   - Tax/compliance considerations in our industry context?

   Non-monetary incentives:
   - Contribution to charity in their name?
   - Early access to improvements or new capabilities?
   - Direct line to the team for their feedback?

6. LOGISTICS PLANNING

   Format:
   - In-person, video call, or phone? (pros/cons of each)
   - What platform? (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet)
   - How to record (with permission)?

   Scheduling:
   - How much time per interview? (45 min? 60 min?)
   - How much buffer between interviews? (for notes, breaks)
   - Best times to offer? (avoid work hours, evenings, weekends)
   - How far in advance to schedule?

   Team roles:
   - Who leads interview?
   - Who takes notes?
   - Should multiple team members observe?

7. SAMPLE SIZE RECOMMENDATION

   How many interviews are enough to:
   - Reach pattern saturation (when no new insights emerge)?
   - Cover all major segments?
   - Have confidence in findings?

   Suggest: Plan for X interviews, assess after Y, extend if needed

8. ETHICAL & COMPLIANCE CONSIDERATIONS

   Industry context:
   - Privacy/data protection requirements (GDPR)
   - Customer confidentiality
   - Consent forms needed?
   - What can/can't be promised?
   - How to store customer data and recordings?

   Research ethics:
   - Informed consent (what to explain about purpose, recording, usage)
   - Right to withdraw
   - Anonymization of data

9. BACKUP PLAN

   What if recruitment is difficult?
   - How to increase response rate?
   - Alternative sources?
   - Can we adjust incentive?
   - Should we widen criteria?

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Comprehensive recruitment strategy with:
- Participant mix matrix
- Recruitment message templates
- Screening questionnaire
- Logistics checklist
- Timeline for recruitment process
```
