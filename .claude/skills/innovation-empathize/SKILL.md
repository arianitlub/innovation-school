## Hard rules from the booklet

- Interviews must be with real people. Never simulate interviews, customers, quotes or answers with AI.
- Run 2.3.x only on real notes or transcripts. If none exist, say so and stop; do not invent data to fill the prompt.
- 2.3.4 comes last, after patterns, personas and the journey map have been discussed.

## How to use

1. Pick the prompt and read its file.
2. Fill placeholders from the repo: challenge and goal in `AGENTS.md` and `docs/challenges/00-overview.md`; the Understand draft in `docs/challenges/02-understand.md`; Sabrina's questions in `docs/team-research/interview-questions.md`; the expert transcript in `docs/team-research/Innovation_School_English_Transcript-Veljko.txt` (speaker labels unreliable, many gaps). Ask for anything missing.
3. The expert interview is one internal view, not customer evidence. Keep it separate from customer interviews in any analysis.
4. Replace the booklet's retail-app examples (account opening, paycheck, balance checks) with our advice journey.
5. Keep interview notes anonymised. Save results only when asked, to `docs/challenges/03-empathize.md` (create it only when the team reaches this step) and log decisions in `docs/challenges/team-log.md`.
