# PROMPT 1.2.1: Terminology Clarification & Alignment

Use Case: Ensuring your team has shared understanding of key concepts

```text
I am working on an innovation project at a company. My challenge is: "[INSERT YOUR CHALLENGE - e.g., 'Improving customer onboarding completion rates' or 'Optimizing supply chain delivery times']"

Our team needs clear, aligned definitions for key terms relevant to our challenge.

KEY TERMS TO DEFINE:
[INSERT 5-10 TERMS RELEVANT TO YOUR CHALLENGE - Examples below:]

Example terms for a customer experience challenge:
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT) vs. Net Promoter Score (NPS) vs. Customer Effort Score (CES)
- Customer journey vs. User flow vs. Touchpoint
- Customer retention vs. Customer loyalty vs. Customer lifetime value (CLV)

Example terms for an operations challenge:
- Process efficiency vs. Process effectiveness
- Cycle time vs. Lead time vs. Throughput
- Bottleneck vs. Constraint
- Utilization rate vs. Capacity

Example terms for a digital/tech challenge:
- User experience (UX) vs. User interface (UI)
- Adoption rate vs. Engagement rate
- Automation vs. Augmentation

For each term, please provide:

1. GENERAL DEFINITION
   - What does this term mean in general business context?

2. INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC DEFINITION
   - How is this term specifically used in our industry?
   - Are there any regulatory, technical, or industry-specific meanings?

3. KEY DISTINCTIONS
   - What similar terms are often confused with this?
   - What are the critical differences?

4. PRACTICAL EXAMPLE
   - Give one concrete example relevant to my challenge
   - Show how understanding this term matters for our project

5. HOW TO MEASURE (if applicable)
   - If it's a metric, how is it typically calculated?
   - What tools or methods are used to track it?

OUTPUT FORMAT:
For each term, provide one clear section (3-4 paragraphs) that any team member can understand, regardless of technical background. Use simple language and relevant examples.
```
