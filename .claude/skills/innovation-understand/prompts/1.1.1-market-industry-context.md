# PROMPT 1.1.1: Market & Industry Context Research

Use Case: Understanding market landscape and industry trends related to your challenge

```text
I am an Innovation Manager at a company. My challenge is: "[INSERT YOUR CHALLENGE - e.g., 'Reducing product return rates by 30%' or 'Improving employee onboarding completion' or 'Increasing conversion rates in our sales funnel']"

Please provide a comprehensive market overview covering:

1. CURRENT STATE IN OUR CHALLENGE AREA
   - How do leading companies in our industry (both regionally and globally) currently approach this challenge area?
   - What are industry standard practices and common solutions?
   - What metrics do companies typically track for this type of challenge?

2. INDUSTRY TRENDS (2024-2026)
   - What emerging trends are relevant to solving this challenge in our industry?
   - What new technologies, methodologies, or approaches are being adopted?
   - How are expectations changing for this area (from customers, employees, or stakeholders)?
   - What innovations are companies testing or implementing?

3. KEY CHALLENGES
   - What are the most common pain points or obstacles companies face in this area?
   - What barriers have prevented better solutions so far?
   - What are the typical cost or resource implications?

4. BENCHMARK EXAMPLES - BOTH OUR INDUSTRY AND OTHER SECTORS
   - Which companies in our industry are recognized leaders in addressing this challenge? (Include regional and global examples)
   - Which companies from OTHER industries have solved similar challenges exceptionally well?
   - What specific innovations or approaches have they introduced?
   - What measurable results have they achieved?

5. CROSS-INDUSTRY INSIGHTS
   - What companies outside our industry have tackled similar challenges in creative ways?
   - What principles, practices, or solutions could be adapted to our context?

CONSTRAINTS:
- Include both regional and global examples
- Cite specific examples with real company names
- Include recent data (last 2 years)
- If you're uncertain about any information, clearly state "This requires verification"
- Highlight insights from outside our industry that could be adapted

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Provide a structured report with clear sections, bullet points, and specific examples. Maximum 1200 words.
```
