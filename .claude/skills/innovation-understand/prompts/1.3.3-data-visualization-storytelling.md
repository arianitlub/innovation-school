# PROMPT 1.3.3: Data Visualization & Storytelling

Use Case: Creating compelling visualizations and narratives from your data analysis

```text
I have completed data analysis on our [your challenge area]. Now I need to visualize findings and tell a compelling story to stakeholders.

My key findings include:
[SUMMARIZE 3-5 KEY INSIGHTS FROM YOUR ANALYSIS - e.g., "Processing time increased 40% over 6 months", "30% of errors occur in stage 3 of the process", "Top-performing region achieves 2x better results than average"]

Help me create a data storytelling framework:

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY DASHBOARD
   - What are the 4-6 most important metrics to show at a glance?
   - What visualization types work best for executives? (KPI cards, trend lines, heat maps, comparison charts)
   - How to show "so what?" (impact on business outcomes)
   - Design principles for clarity and impact

2. PROBLEM VISUALIZATION
   - How to visually demonstrate the scale of the problem?
   - Chart types to show:
     * Declining performance trends (line charts)
     * Problem frequency or distribution (bar charts, pareto charts)
     * Process bottlenecks or drop-offs (funnel charts, Sankey diagrams)
     * Segmentation patterns (pie charts, treemaps, heatmaps)

3. ROOT CAUSE VISUALIZATION
   - How to show relationships between variables?
   - How to demonstrate cause-and-effect patterns?
   - Use scatter plots, correlation matrices, fishbone diagrams, waterfall charts?
   - How to make causal relationships clear vs. just correlations?

4. IMPACT QUANTIFICATION VISUALIZATION
   - How to show business impact?
   - How to visualize costs, lost revenue, or inefficiencies?
   - How to show value at risk or opportunity size?
   - Make financial impact crystal clear to leadership

5. COMPARATIVE BENCHMARKING VISUALIZATION
   - How to show our performance vs. competitors or industry standards?
   - How to display internal comparisons (regions, teams, time periods)?
   - Use comparison bars, gap analysis charts, quadrant plots?

6. STORYTELLING STRUCTURE
   Recommend a narrative flow:
   - Opening: What's the business problem or opportunity?
   - Data: What does the data tell us? (key findings)
   - Analysis: Why is this happening? (root causes)
   - Impact: What does this cost us or what are the consequences?
   - Opportunity: What could we achieve if we address this?
   - Call to Action: What do we need to do?

7. POWERPOINT/PRESENTATION STRUCTURE
   Suggest slide sequence with:
   - Recommended chart type for each slide
   - Key message headline for each slide
   - Supporting data points
   - What to emphasize (visual hierarchy)

8. INTERACTIVE DASHBOARD (if applicable)
   - If creating a BI dashboard (Power BI, Tableau, Excel), what layout?
   - What filters or drill-down capabilities to include?
   - How to enable stakeholders to explore data themselves?

9. DATA CREDIBILITY & CONFIDENCE
   - How to show data quality and confidence levels?
   - When to include sample sizes, margins of error, or data limitations?
   - How to address data limitations transparently?

10. COMMON VISUALIZATION MISTAKES TO AVOID
    - What chart types to avoid and why?
    - How to prevent misleading visualizations?
    - Accessibility considerations (color blindness, screen readers)

For each visualization recommendation, provide:
- When to use it (what data/story it tells best)
- How to create it (Excel, PowerPoint, BI tool)
- Example sketch or description
- What makes it effective

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Visualization guide with chart type recommendations, storytelling structure, and slide-by-slide presentation outline. Include design principles and examples.
```
