# PROMPT 1.2.2: Comprehensive Stakeholder Mapping

Use Case: Identifying all parties affected by or influencing your innovation initiative

```text
I am working on this innovation challenge: "[INSERT YOUR CHALLENGE - e.g., 'Implementing a new inventory management system' or 'Redesigning the customer support process']"

Help me create a comprehensive stakeholder map:

1. PRIMARY STAKEHOLDERS (Directly Affected)
   - Who are the primary users, customers, or beneficiaries affected by this challenge?
   - What segments or groups exist within them?
   - What does each group care about most?

2. INTERNAL STAKEHOLDERS
   - Which departments or teams will be involved or affected?
   - Suggest typical departments based on my challenge type (e.g., for operations: Operations, IT, Finance, Quality; for customer-facing: Customer Service, Sales, Marketing, Product)
   - What are their typical concerns and priorities?
   - Who needs to approve or support this initiative?
   - Who might resist this change and why?

3. EXTERNAL STAKEHOLDERS
   - Which external parties are affected or have influence?
   - Examples: Vendors/partners, Customers, Regulators, Industry bodies, Service providers
   - What influence or constraints do they create?

4. HIDDEN/OFTEN OVERLOOKED STAKEHOLDERS
   - Who might be indirectly affected but often forgotten?
   - Internal: Training teams, Change management, Communications, Business continuity
   - External: End users who aren't direct customers, Community groups, Adjacent teams
   - What concerns might they have?

For each stakeholder group, analyze:
- Their PRIMARY INTERESTS in this initiative
- Their CONCERNS or potential objections
- Their INFLUENCE level (High/Medium/Low)
- Their INTEREST level in this project (High/Medium/Low)
- ENGAGEMENT APPROACH (how to involve them)
- SUCCESS CRITERIA (what would make them happy)

Create a stakeholder matrix:
- High Influence + High Interest = Manage Closely
- High Influence + Low Interest = Keep Satisfied
- Low Influence + High Interest = Keep Informed
- Low Influence + Low Interest = Monitor

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Detailed stakeholder table with analysis, plus a prioritization matrix showing how to engage each group.
```
