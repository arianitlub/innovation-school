# PROMPT 1.1.2: Competitive Landscape Analysis

Use Case: Detailed analysis of how competitors and other companies handle your challenge area

```text
I am researching how different companies handle my challenge area: "[INSERT YOUR CHALLENGE - e.g., 'Reducing inventory waste' or 'Improving first-call resolution rates' or 'Accelerating project delivery timelines']"

Provide a comparative analysis:

1. DIRECT COMPETITORS (Regional companies)
   - Which companies in our industry and region are known for excellence in this challenge area?
   - What specific approaches, features, or innovations do they use?
   - What results or performance metrics are they achieving?
   - What makes their approach stand out?

2. GLOBAL INDUSTRY LEADERS
   - Which global companies in our industry are considered best-in-class for this challenge?
   - What specific approaches have they taken?
   - What results have they achieved (cost savings, efficiency gains, satisfaction improvements)?

3. INNOVATIVE DISRUPTORS
   - Which newer or digital-first companies in related sectors are doing this differently?
   - What do they do that customers/users appreciate?
   - What can traditional companies learn from their approach?

4. CROSS-INDUSTRY LEADERS
   - Which companies from OTHER industries have solved similar challenges exceptionally well?
   - What principles or practices could be adapted to our industry?

5. COMPARISON DIMENSIONS
   Create a comparison considering relevant dimensions such as:
   - Process efficiency or speed
   - Quality or accuracy of outcomes
   - Resource requirements (cost, staffing)
   - Technology enablement
   - User/customer satisfaction
   - Scalability
   - Innovation approach

6. DIFFERENTIATION OPPORTUNITIES
   - What gaps exist in current industry approaches?
   - What do most competitors struggle with?
   - What could make our approach unique and valued?

CONSTRAINTS:
- Focus on actionable insights, not just descriptions
- Include both regional and global examples
- Highlight cross-industry best practices
- Flag information that needs verification

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Structured analysis with comparison table, specific examples, and strategic recommendations. Maximum 1500 words.
```
