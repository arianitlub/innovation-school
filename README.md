# Innovation School

An educational workspace for learning and practising Design Thinking, innovation culture and facilitation through Innovation School 2026.

The working case explores how mass-affluent customers receive wealth management advice, where their needs remain unmet, and how services could create more value. The aim is to learn a method: understand people, define a problem, explore ideas, test a small improvement and explain its value.

Research notes distinguish documented services, reported experience, assumptions and possible solutions. They are learning material and do not constitute personal investment advice or an official statement of a bank's services.

## How the work develops

We follow Understand → Empathize & Synthesize → Ideate → Prototype & Test → Business Case & Pitch. The stage notes are updated as the team works through them. Preparation ideas do not count as completed stages or agreed team decisions.

Original team inputs remain separate from our research. Sources and evidence limits accompany the research so readers can check claims and identify what still needs validation.

This local workspace also contains personal Learning Transfer notes and internal team material. The version intended for public publication will be selected and reviewed separately.
